[Verse 1]
Under the trees, A finds Acorn. It is the hard-shelled seed of an oak tree, topped by a little cup.
B — Bear. A bear is a large furry mammal with strong legs, broad paws, and an excellent sense of smell.
What waits at C? Chipmunk! Hop or hide | little mammal moves outside
D is for Deer. A deer has sturdy legs and hooves and moves around on land.

[Verse 2]
Eagle — our E. Feather light | beak and wings come into sight
Under the trees, F finds Fern. It unfurls divided green fronds from curled young growth called fiddleheads.
G — Grouse. A grouse is a sturdy woodland bird that spends much of its time on the ground and can burst into fast flight.
What waits at H? Hollow Log! It is a fallen trunk with an empty space inside that can shelter small animals.

[Verse 3]
I is for Ivy. Climb and wind | ivy leaves trail close behind
Juniper — our J. It is an evergreen shrub or tree with needle- or scale-like leaves and berry-like seed cones.
Under the trees, K finds Knot. It is a hard, twisted place in wood where a branch grew from the trunk.
L — Log. A log is a fallen or cut section of tree trunk that can become shelter for small forest life.

[Verse 4]
What waits at M? Mushroom! It is the fruiting body of a fungus, often shaped like a cap on a stalk.
N is for Nest. A nest is a place birds build to hold eggs and chicks.
Owl — our O. Owl in the night | round eyes watching, wings in flight
Under the trees, P finds Pinecone. It is a woody cone made of overlapping scales that protects a pine tree's seeds.

[Verse 5]

What waits at R? Rabbit! It has long ears and powerful back legs for quick hops.
S is for Spruce. Roots down low | branches reach and grow
Trail — our T. It is a path people follow through outdoor places.

[Verse 6]
Under the trees, U finds Underbrush. Sway and grow | plant finds light from down below
V — Vine. Vine is a plant adapted to its habitat, using leaves, stems, roots, or climbing growth to reach water and light.
What waits at W? Woodpecker! Tap-tap tree | woodpecker searches bark carefully
X is for Xeric Shrub. A xeric shrub is a woody plant adapted to dry conditions.

[Verse 7]
Yellow Warbler — our Y. Wing to sky | feathered bird goes gliding by
Under the trees, Z finds Zebra Swallowtail. It is a butterfly with black-and-white striped wings and long tails.
