[Intro]
Whoosh... one red leaf spins down.
Autumn letters drift without a sound.

[Verse 1]
Look at A: Acorn! It is the hard-shelled seed of an oak tree, topped by a little cup.
B — Birch. Birch trees are easy to notice by their pale bark, often peeling in thin papery layers.
Under the trees, C meets Chipmunk. Hop or hide | little mammal moves outside
D ... D ... Deer! A deer has sturdy legs and hooves and moves around on land.

[Verse 2]
What did E find? Evergreen! Trunk stands strong | leaves or needles spread along
Look at F: Fox! It has pointed ears, a long bushy tail, and quiet paws for moving through its habitat.
G — Grass. Root down low | leaves reach up and grow
Under the trees, H meets Hedgehog. It has a coat of stiff spines and can curl its body into a tight ball.

[Chorus]
Leaves drift down—A-B-C!

[Verse 3]
I ... I ... Ivy! Climb and wind | ivy leaves trail close behind
What did J find? Juniper! It is an evergreen shrub or tree with needle- or scale-like leaves and berry-like seed cones.
Look at K: Knot! It is a hard, twisted place in wood where a branch grew from the trunk.
L — Lynx. A lynx is a furry mammal that walks quietly and uses keen senses to explore its habitat.

[Verse 4]
Under the trees, M meets Mushroom. It is the fruiting body of a fungus, often shaped like a cap on a stalk.
N ... N ... Nest! A nest is a place birds build to hold eggs and chicks.
What did O find? Oak! Tree to sky | branches lift up high
Look at P: Pine! It is an evergreen tree with bundles of needles and woody cones.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quail. Quail on the trail | ground bird with a little tail
Under the trees, R meets River. A river flows downhill in a channel toward other water.
S ... S ... Spruce! Roots down low | branches reach and grow
What did T find? Tree! It has a woody trunk lifting branches and leaves high above its roots.

[Chorus]
Leaves drift down—A-B-C!

[Verse 6]
Look at U: Upland Fern! Sway and grow | plant finds light from down below
V — Violet. A violet is a small low-growing plant with soft purple, blue, white, or yellow flowers.
Under the trees, W meets Wolf. It is a furry mammal that walks quietly and uses keen senses to explore its habitat.
X ... X ... Xylophone! A xylophone is our X-word helper: a row of tuned bars makes different notes when struck.

[Verse 7]
What did Y find? Yellow Warbler! Wing to sky | feathered bird goes gliding by
Look at Z: Zebra Swallowtail! It is a butterfly with black-and-white striped wings and long tails.
