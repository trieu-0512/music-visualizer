[Intro]
Tall dresser may need anchoring, mirror needs secure support, plant needs light, refrigerator needs airflow and a safe door.
Where an object goes depends on what it does.

[Verse 1]
A — Alarm Clock! | Timepiece with alarm function signals a set time; it does not know whether a person has slept enough.
B ... B ... Bed! | Furniture supports sleep/rest; safe setup depends on age, frame, mattress, rails, and surrounding space.
C? Chair! | Raised seat supports sitting; standing or rocking on ordinary chairs increases fall risk.
D is Dresser. Storage furniture with drawers; tall units can tip and should be anchored where appropriate. |

[Verse 2]
E — End Table! | Small table beside seating/bed holds lightweight items; heavy/hot objects need stable adult placement.
F ... F ... Frame! | Border/support presents or protects a picture; glass-front frames can break.
G? Glass! | Drinking vessel or transparent material depending context; breakable glassware needs careful adult judgment around children.
H is Hanger. Clothing support hangs garments from a rail/hook; wire/plastic shapes are not toys. |

[Chorus]
Place, anchor, clear—A-B-C!
A shelf is not the answer for every thing.

[Verse 3]
I — Indoor Plant! | Plant grown indoors needs species-appropriate light/water; some houseplants are toxic to children/pets if eaten.
J ... J ... Jar! | Rigid lidded container stores foods/items; glass can break and sealed contents may need adult access.
K? Key! | Cut or coded object operates a lock; small keys can be choking hazards and access keys should stay with responsible users.
L is Laundry Basket. Container gathers/carries laundry; it is not a ride-on toy and loads can block visibility. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mirror! | Reflective surface forms an image; securely mounted/nonbreakable designs reduce injury risk.
N ... N ... Nightlight! | Low-level light helps orientation at night; electrical units still need safe outlet/cord placement.
O? Ottoman! | Low upholstered footrest/seat; storage ottomans may have lids/hinges needing finger safety.
P is Picture. Image/photo/artwork represents a scene or subject; it is a visual item rather than a functional appliance. |

[Chorus]
Place, anchor, clear—A-B-C!
A shelf is not the answer for every thing.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilted Cushion! | Padded fabric cushion stitched through layers; it is seating/decor support, not safe climbing equipment.
R ... R ... Refrigerator! | Insulated appliance keeps food cold; doors, shelves, and food storage have safety rules and it is never a hiding place.
S? Soap! | Cleaning substance lifts oils/dirt with water; avoid eyes/mouth and use the right product for hands, dishes, or surfaces.
T is Towel. Absorbent textile dries skin or surfaces; wet towels should be hung/spread to dry rather than left on floors. |

[Verse 6]
U — Umbrella! | Portable canopy protects from rain/sun; pointed tips and opening mechanism need space.
V ... V ... Vacuum! | Powered appliance removes dust/debris by airflow; cords, moving brush, and electricity require supervised use.
W? Window! | Opening admits light/air/view; screens are not fall protection and children stay away from unsafe open heights.
X is X-shaped Shelf. Shelf/support arranged in an X-like form; wall anchoring/load limits still matter. |

[Chorus]
Place, anchor, clear—A-B-C!
A shelf is not the answer for every thing.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Cushion! | Cushion with yellow cover; color changes appearance, not its support function.
Z ... Z ... Zen Garden! | Small arranged sand/stone garden used for visual/tactile contemplation; tiny stones may be choking hazards for young children.
