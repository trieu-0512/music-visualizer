B lands on Booster. A rocket booster provides extra thrust during an early part of a launch.
Capsule? Yes—C. A space capsule is a compact spacecraft section built to carry crew, cargo, or experiments.
past the quiet stars, the D clue is Dish Antenna. A dish antenna focuses radio waves so a spacecraft or ground station can send and receive signals.

[Verse 2]
Say E, then Eclipse. Eclipse up high | shape, motion, or job tells us why
Look—F! Look—Fuel Tank! A spacecraft fuel or propellant tank stores material used by an engine or thruster system.
G lands on Gemini Capsule. Gemini takes flight | two-seat capsule trained for lunar flight
Hubble Telescope? Yes—H. The Hubble Space Telescope orbits Earth and observes the universe above much of our atmosphere.

[Verse 3]
past the quiet stars, the I clue is International Space Station. Station circles round | orbiting lab above the ground
Say J, then Jet Pack. A spacecraft maneuvering pack can use small thrusters to help an astronaut move in space during specially planned operations.
Look—K! Look—Krypton Tank! Krypton stored just right | gas can feed an ion-thruster flight
L lands on Lander. A lander is a spacecraft built to come down onto the surface of another world.

[Verse 4]
Moon? Yes—M. Moon in view | sunlight makes its phases new
past the quiet stars, the N clue is Nebula. A nebula is a cloud of gas and dust in space, sometimes linked to star birth or the remains of stars.
Say O, then Orbit. Orbit bends around | motion joined with gravity keeps the path bound
Look—P! Look—Planet! It is a large round world that travels in an orbit around a star.

[Verse 5]
Q lands on Quasar. Quasar in view | space clue coming through
Rocket? Yes—R. It produces thrust by pushing exhaust in the opposite direction of travel.
past the quiet stars, the S clue is Spacesuit. A spacesuit provides pressure, oxygen support, temperature control, and protection for an astronaut outside a spacecraft.
Say T, then Thruster. A spacecraft thruster produces controlled force used to change motion or orientation.

[Verse 6]
Look—U! Look—Universe! Universe in view | space clue coming through
V lands on Visor. A spacesuit visor protects an astronaut's eyes and face and can filter intense sunlight.
World? Yes—W. World in the frame | planet or moon can carry the name
past the quiet stars, the X clue is Xenon Thruster. A xenon thruster uses electrically accelerated xenon ions to produce gentle, efficient thrust.

[Verse 7]
Say Y, then Yoke. Yoke in hand-control view | steering input is the clue
Look—Z! Look—Zero-gravity Chair! It is a reclining chair named for a body position that spreads support; it does not create real weightlessness.
