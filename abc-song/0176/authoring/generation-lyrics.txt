[Intro]
A face gives a clue, not a mind-reading answer; a question or voice can add context.
Feelings can be named without calling any one of them bad.

[Verse 1]
A — Affection Heart! | Heart card represents care; affection can be words, time, help, or consented touch.
B ... B ... Bashful Bunny! | Character suggests bashfulness; it is a discussion cue, not a diagnosis.
C? Calm Cloud! | Calm metaphor; nobody must feel calm on command.
D is Disappointed Face. Disappointment cue; a face alone cannot prove another person's feeling. |

[Verse 2]
E — Excited Eyes! | Excitement cue; wide eyes have many meanings, so ask rather than assume.
F ... F ... Friendship Bracelet! | Friendship symbol; exchanging one is optional and does not measure friendship quality.
G? Gentle Hands! | Cue for safe hands; gentleness includes respecting space and permission.
H is Happy Face. Happiness cue; people show happiness differently and smiles have many meanings. |
