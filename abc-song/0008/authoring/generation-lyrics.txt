[Intro]
Sway—paddle—hop—glide.
Watch the water teach each body how to move.

[Verse 1]
A finds Alder. Bank-side sway | roots stay damp while branches move with the day.
B — Beaver! Flat tail paddles; branches travel away.
What moves with C? Canoe! Paddle left, paddle right; narrow hull slides on its way.
Look at D: Duck. Webbed feet paddle while the broad bill dips through spray.

[Verse 2]
E is for Eel. Easy glide | long winding body bends from side to side.
F finds Frog. Waterside hop | strong back legs kick to swim, then stop.
G — Green Algae! Soft green patches spread and shift where water moves over wet surfaces.
What moves with H? Hydrilla! Leafy stems sway underwater with the current.

[Chorus]
Sway, paddle, hop, then glide!
Freshwater letters move side by side.

[Verse 3]
Look at I: Insect. Six-leg line | three main body parts make the design.
J is for Jetty. Shore, then water | a straight structure reaches farther out.
K finds Kingfisher. Flash and splash | perch, quick dive, then back in a dash.
L — Lake. Wide water holds every little ripple inside a boundary of land.

[Verse 4]
What moves with M? Muskrat! Body low | paddles beside wet banks where waters flow.
Look at N: Newt. Four legs walk low | long tail follows as the little amphibian goes.
O is for Oar. Row, row | long blade pulls water and helps the boat go.
P finds Pond. Small and still, it holds a whole home where freshwater plants and animals can move.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quail! Quick ground steps | small bird walks and pecks.
What moves with R? River! Flow as we go | water follows a downhill channel.
Look at S: Salamander. Long and low | four legs and a tail help the moist-skinned body go.
T is for Tadpole. Wiggle, little tail | water-stage youngster grows legs later on.

[Chorus]
Sway, paddle, hop, then glide!
Freshwater letters move side by side.

[Verse 6]
U finds Umbrella Sedge. Green rays fan and spread | leaf-like spokes above the stem.
V — Valley. Low where waters flow | land dips between hills or mountains.
What moves with W? Water Lily! Round leaves float | one flower lifts above the water.
Look at X: Xiphophorus. Small freshwater fish group including swordtails and platies.

[Verse 7]
Y is for Yellow Perch. Bars in rows | yellow sides flash and off it goes.
Z finds Zebra Danio. Stripes in a row | small freshwater fish darts to and fro.
