[Intro]
Socks go with feet, coat with outer layer, knit cap with head, pajamas with sleep.
Closet color is easy to see; function takes another look.

[Verse 1]
A — Anorak! | Hooded outer jacket, often wind/water resistant; exact insulation and waterproofing depend on fabric/construction.
B ... B ... Belt! | Strap fastens around waist to hold clothing or accessories; fit should be comfortable, not tight.
C? Coat! | Outer garment provides warmth/weather protection; material and length vary.
D is Denim Jacket. Jacket made mainly from denim; durable woven cotton construction gives structure but not waterproofing by itself. |

[Verse 2]
E — Earmuffs! | Pads cover outer ears for warmth or hearing protection depending design; winter earmuffs do not replace certified hearing protectors.
F ... F ... Flip-flops! | Open sandals held by toe/foot straps; easy airflow but little toe/heel protection or traction for rough activity.
G? Gown! | Long garment; can mean formal dress, nightwear, medical gown, or robe depending context.
H is Hat. Head covering provides shade, warmth, protection, or style depending type. |

I — Indoor Shoes! | Footwear reserved for indoor surfaces; keeps outdoor dirt separate and often emphasizes light comfort.
J ... J ... Jeans! | Denim trousers with sturdy woven fabric and seams; fit should allow normal movement.
K? Knit Cap! | Soft knitted close-fitting hat retains warmth around head; fiber and thickness affect insulation.
L is Leggings. Stretch close-fitting trousers; flexible for movement but warmth/weather protection depends fabric. |

[Verse 4]
M — Moccasins! | Soft footwear inspired by Indigenous North American moccasin traditions; modern products vary, so avoid treating one commercial style as universal.
N ... N ... Necktie! | Long fabric accessory knotted at neck; should not be worn where loose neckwear could catch in machinery/play equipment.
O? Overcoat! | Long outer coat worn over other clothes for cold-weather insulation.
P is Pajamas. Sleepwear designed for comfort; fabric, fit, and fire-safety standards vary by market/age. |

[Verse 5]
Q — Quarter Socks! | Short socks rising above ankle but below crew height; naming varies by brand/region.
R ... R ... Raincoat! | Outer layer designed to resist rain; waterproof/breathable performance depends material and seams.
S? Socks! | Foot layers absorb moisture, reduce friction, and add warmth; clean dry fit supports comfort.
T is T-shirt. Light knit shirt with short sleeves in common form; works as base/single layer depending weather. |

[Verse 6]
U — Ugg Boots! | UGG is a brand strongly associated with sheepskin-style boots; mapped boots are warm footwear, not a generic technical category.
V ... V ... Velcro Shoes! | Shoes using hook-and-loop straps for closure; fit still matters even when fastening is easy.
W? Wool Hat! | Hat made partly/fully from wool; wool fibers can insulate even when slightly damp.
X is X-shaped Suspenders. Suspenders whose back straps cross in an X; they support trousers from shoulders rather than tightening waist. |
