[Intro]
Apple hangs on a tree; carrot thickens below ground; vanilla grows on an orchid vine.
A market basket can come from many kinds of plants and places.

[Verse 1]
A — Apple! Pome fruit from a woody orchard tree | cultivars differ in color, texture, and season.
B ... B ... Banana! Fruit grows in hands on a giant herb, not a woody tree | tropical/subtropical crop.
C? Carrot! Taproot enlarges below soil | biennial plant usually harvested in its first growing season.
D is Daikon. Radish root crop | cool-season brassica grown below ground with leafy top.

[Verse 2]
E — Endive! Leafy chicory crop | leaves, not fruit, form the edible head/rosette.
F ... F ... Fennel! Bulb fennel has swollen leaf bases; fronds and seeds are also used | not a true underground bulb.
G? Grapes! Berries grow in clusters on woody vines | vineyard or arbor crop.
H is Honeydew. Melon grows on a trailing vine | fruit develops near ground, not on a tree.

[Verse 3]
I — Indian Fig! Common name for prickly-pear cactus fruit, often Opuntia ficus-indica | cactus crop with glochids requiring careful preparation.
J ... J ... Jackfruit! Huge multiple fruit grows on tropical tree trunks/branches | tree crop, not garden vine.
K? Kiwi! Kiwifruit grows on vigorous woody vines | fuzzy fruit is not related to the kiwi bird.
L is Leek. Allium grown for long white/pale leaf sheaths | leafy stem-like crop, not a root.

[Verse 4]
M — Mango! Stone fruit from tropical evergreen tree | tree crop.
N ... N ... Napa Cabbage! Leafy brassica forms an elongated head close to ground.
O? Okra! Flowering plant produces ridged edible pods | fruit/pod crop above ground.
P is Pumpkin. Large pepo fruit grows on sprawling vines | ground-level vine crop.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quandong! Australian native sandalwood relative bearing red stone fruits | tree/shrub crop with distinctive regional origin.
R ... R ... Raspberry! Aggregate fruit grows on bramble canes | cane crop, not tree.
S? Strawberry! Low herbaceous plant sends runners; aggregate fruits sit near ground.
T is Tomato. Herbaceous vine/bush crop producing berries | supported or sprawling depending cultivar.

[Verse 6]
U — Ugli Fruit! UGLI® is a trademark for a Jamaican tangelo, a citrus fruit | brand/name clue, not a botanical species name.
V ... V ... Vanilla Bean! Cured pod-like fruit of vanilla orchid vines | spice crop after pollination and curing.
W? Watermelon! Trailing vine bears large fruits on ground | warm-season crop.
X is Xoconostle. Sour prickly-pear-type cactus fruit from Opuntia species, strongly associated with Mexican cuisine | cactus growth form.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Pepper! Pepper fruit grows on branching herbaceous plants | color marks maturity/cultivar, not plant family difference.
Z ... Z ... Zinfandel Grape! Wine/table grape cultivar on woody vines | cultivar identity belongs inside grape growth form.
