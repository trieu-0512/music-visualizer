Crisp preschool classroom-pop at 88 BPM, restrained piano, marimba, soft bass, woodblock, dry lead. Begin sparse at the backpack hook, widen at map/globe reference, tighten rhythm around organizers, then resolve into a quiet reset. Protect every target onset. No school-bell masking, no chant wall, no tool sound effects over Scissors or Stapler. Opening stays sparse. Round 1 teaches with a narrow stepwise melody; Round 2 retrieval leaves a response gap. Arrangement builds lightly into the chorus, then thins. Duck backing vocals and percussion around every target-word. End with a clean cadence.
