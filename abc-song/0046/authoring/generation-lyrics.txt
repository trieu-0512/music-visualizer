[Intro]
Pitter-patter, whoosh, then hush.
The sky has a letter in today's forecast.

[Verse 1]
A — Altocumulus! Cloudlets in rows | middle-sky patches where the wind blows
B ... B ... Breeze! A breeze is a light wind you may feel moving across your skin or leaves.
C is for Cloud. Cloud up high | tiny drops are floating in the sky
What does D bring? Dew! Dew is water that forms as tiny drops on cool surfaces.

[Verse 2]
Under the changing sky, E meets Earmuffs. Ears tucked warm | padded cups for winter storm
F — Fog! It is a cloud close to the ground that makes distant things harder to see.
G ... G ... Gust! Gust goes rush | quick strong wind, then back to hush
H is for Hail. It is made of balls or lumps of ice that fall from storm clouds.

[Chorus]
Weather watch—A-B-C!
Look at the sky and sing with me!

[Verse 3]
What does I bring? Ice! Cold and bright | frozen water catches light
Under the changing sky, J meets Jet Stream. It is a fast-moving band of air high in the atmosphere.
K — Kelvin Thermometer! Kelvin scale | temperature numbers tell the tale
L ... L ... Lightning! Lightning is a bright electrical flash in a storm; we watch storms safely from indoors.

[Verse 4]
M is for Mist. Mist near ground | tiny floating droplets all around
What does N bring? Night Sky! The night sky is the dark sky we see after sunset, often with stars or the moon.
Under the changing sky, O meets Overcoat. Long coat on | extra warmth when cold comes on
P — Puddle! It is a small shallow pool of water on the ground.

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Quiet Sky! A quiet sky has little dramatic weather, with calm-looking clouds or clear air.
R is for Raincoat. It uses water-resistant material to help keep clothing dry in rain.
What does S bring? Snowflake! Branching white | six-sided crystal, tiny and light
Under the changing sky, T meets Thermometer. It measures temperature.

[Chorus]
Weather watch—A-B-C!
Look at the sky and sing with me!

[Verse 6]
U — Updraft! Air goes high | rising current climbs the sky
V ... V ... Vapor! Water vapor is water in its invisible gas form in the air.
W is for Windsock. Fill and fly | wind direction meets our eye
What does X bring? Xylophone! A xylophone is our X-word helper: a row of tuned bars makes different notes when struck.

[Verse 7]
Under the changing sky, Y meets Yellow Sun. Picture sun high | bright yellow circle in the sky
Z — Zephyr! It is a soft, gentle breeze.

[Interlude]
The forecast pauses—now call the weather words back.
