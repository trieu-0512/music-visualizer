[Intro]
Wash hands when the routine calls for it, dry with a clean towel, keep food containers clean, let adults manage vitamins.
Clean means useful care—not fear of every speck.

[Verse 1]
A — Apron! | Protective garment covers clothing during cooking/craft/cleaning; it does not replace handwashing or safe tool use.
B ... B ... Bath! | Washing body in tub/shower; young children need close adult supervision around water and safe water temperature.
C? Cup! | Small open drinking/measure container; exact role depends on context.
D is Dinner. Evening/main meal in many routines; timing, foods, and family structure vary widely. |

[Verse 2]
E — Evening Book! | Book used during an evening routine; reading can cue transition without guaranteeing sleep.
F ... F ... Face Towel! | Small towel dries face; personal clean towels reduce cross-contamination and eye irritation.
G? Getting-dressed Chart! | Visual sequence supports remembering clothing steps; it is a prompt, not a rule that every outfit uses every item.
H is Hairbrush. Bristled/pinned grooming tool detangles/styles hair; gentle technique avoids pulling and personal brushes should stay clean. |

[Chorus]
Ready, wash, dry—A-B-C!
Clean enough for the task is the goal.

[Verse 3]
I — Indoor Shoes! | Footwear reserved for indoor surfaces; helps separate outdoor dirt and may support household/school routines.
J ... J ... Juice! | Drink made from fruit/vegetable juice; amount, added sugar, and dental context matter, and water is still a core hydration drink.
K? Key! | Lock-opening object; small keys can be choking hazards and access keys stay with responsible users.
L is Lamp. Electrical light source; stable placement, hot bulbs, cords, and outlets need safe setup. |

[Verse 4]
M — Morning Chart! | Visual routine prompt lists morning tasks in an order that can be adapted to the household.
N ... N ... Napkin! | Cloth/paper wipes hands/mouth during meals; it does not replace handwashing when cleaning is needed.
O? Oatmeal! | Cooked oat cereal absorbs liquid and thickens; it can stay very hot after cooking, so temperature is checked before eating.
P is Pajamas. Sleepwear designed for comfort; fabric/fit and sleep-safety guidance vary by age. |

[Chorus]
Ready, wash, dry—A-B-C!
Clean enough for the task is the goal.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilt! | Layered stitched textile provides warmth; sleep suitability depends on age and context.
R ... R ... Routine Chart! | Visual list helps remember repeated tasks; routines can flex when needs or schedules change.
S? Slippers! | Soft indoor footwear; traction and fit matter, especially on stairs or smooth floors.
T is Towel. Absorbent textile dries skin/surfaces; wet towels should dry rather than stay on floor. |

[Verse 6]
U — Uniform! | Standardized clothing for school/work/team; it identifies context but not a person's ability or worth.
V ... V ... Vitamins! | Micronutrients needed in small amounts; supplements are not candy and should only be given by responsible adults according to need/guidance.
W? Water Bottle! | Personal container carries drinking water; keeping it clean and not sharing mouthpieces supports hygiene.
X is Xylophone. Tuned-bar musical instrument; deliberate routine-set comparison item, not a required morning chore. |

[Chorus]
Ready, wash, dry—A-B-C!
Clean enough for the task is the goal.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yogurt! | Fermented dairy food or plant-based analogue depending product; label reveals ingredients/allergens and refrigeration needs.
Z ... Z ... Zipper! | Interlocking teeth/slider closes clothing/bags; move slowly to avoid catching skin or fabric.
