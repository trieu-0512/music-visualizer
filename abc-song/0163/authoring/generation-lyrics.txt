[Intro]
Open a door carefully, put laundry in the basket, wash with soap, return objects after use.
Everyday home routines include resetting the space.

[Verse 1]
A — Armchair! | Upholstered single-seat chair with arms; stable floor placement keeps it from becoming climbing equipment.
B ... B ... Bed! | Furniture supports sleep/rest; safe setup depends on age, frame, mattress, rails, and surrounding space.
C? Cupboard! | Enclosed storage furniture with shelves/doors; contents determine whether child locks or high storage are needed.
D is Door. Movable barrier opens/closes a passage; hinges and closing edges are pinch points. |

[Verse 2]
E — Envelope! | Folded paper container carries letters/small flat items; it belongs to storage/mail, not furniture.
F ... F ... Fan! | Device moves air with rotating blades or blower; guards and electrical safety matter, fingers stay away.
G? Garage! | Vehicle/storage/work room; moving cars, tools, chemicals, and doors make it adult-managed space.
H is Hallway. Passage connects rooms; keeping it clear supports safe movement and emergency exit. |

[Chorus]

A small safe task can be enough.

[Verse 3]
I — Indoor Plant! | Plant grown indoors needs species-appropriate light/water; some houseplants are toxic to children/pets if eaten.
J ... J ... Jar! | Rigid lidded container stores foods/items; glass can break and sealed contents may need adult access.
K? Kettle! | Appliance/vessel heats water; boiling water, steam, and hot surfaces are adult-only.
L is Laundry Basket. Container gathers/carries laundry; it is not a ride-on toy and loads can block visibility. |

O? Ottoman! | Low upholstered footrest/seat; storage ottomans may have lids/hinges needing finger safety.
P is Pillow. Soft head/body support; sleep-safety recommendations vary with age, especially for infants. |

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilt! | Layered stitched bedcover/textile; warmth and sleep suitability depend on age/context.
R ... R ... Refrigerator! | Insulated appliance keeps food cold; doors, shelves, and food storage have safety rules and it is never a hiding place.
S? Soap! | Cleaning substance lifts oils/dirt with water; avoid eyes/mouth and use the right product for hands, dishes, or surfaces.
T is Table. Flat raised work/eating surface; stable use means no climbing or edge-loading. |

[Verse 6]

V ... V ... Vacuum! | Powered appliance removes dust/debris by airflow; cords, moving brush, and electricity require supervised use.

X is X-shaped Shelf. Shelf/support arranged in an X-like form; wall anchoring/load limits still matter. |
