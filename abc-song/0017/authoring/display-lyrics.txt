[Intro]
Bzz—one wing skims a petal, then hangs in the air.
Follow the tiny flight; every color changes the path.

[Verse 1]
A — Apron! Cloth front, garden smudge | what we wear before following the tiny flight.
B ... B ... Bee! Wing blur, flower pause | it visits blooms for nectar or pollen and can carry pollen onward.
C? Carrot! Feathery leaves above | orange root stays hidden below the flight.
D is Dirt. Dark crumbs under every color | soil holds the roots beneath our flight path.

[Verse 2]
Low below, E — Earthworm. Bend and burrow | soft body tunnels through moist soil.
F ... F ... Fern! Fronds uncurl in green spirals | a leafy roof close to the ground.
G? Garden Hose! Long green curve across the path | water travels through it toward thirsty plants.
H is Hoe. Long handle, flat working end | an adult soil tool we notice from a safe distance.

[Verse 3]
Now color opens at I — Iris. Blade-like leaves below | showy petals lift into the flight line.
J ... J ... Jasmine! Small pale flowers release a noticeable scent | a soft clue carried on the air.
K? Kale! Crinkle, curl | broad leafy greens make a textured patch below the flight.
L is Lavender. Purple spikes, narrow leaves | scent and color gather along each stem.

[Chorus]
Wing to bloom—A-B-C!
Color follows where the little wings lead!

[Verse 4]
M — Marigold! Gold-orange head | many petals pack color close together.
N ... N ... Nasturtium! Round leaves like little plates | bright blooms sit between them.
O? Orchid! Petals make a curious shape | one special lip sits near the flower's center.
P is Pot. Soil held in a small round home | roots stay contained while flowers rise above.

[Verse 5]
At ground level, Q — Quail. Peck, step, pause | a small bird passes beneath the flower route.
R ... R ... Radish! Leaves above, crisp root below | another color hiding under soil.
S? Sunflower! Broad head high | yellow rays ring a center packed with many tiny flowers.
T is Tomato. Green stems branch | round fruit hangs below small yellow blossoms.

[Chorus]
Wing to bloom—A-B-C!
Color follows where the little wings lead!

[Verse 6]
Rain comes; U — Umbrella! Wide canopy opens | drops slide away while the garden keeps its color.
V ... V ... Violet! Small and low | purple, blue, white, or yellow blooms near the ground.
W? Watering Can! Gentle pour, silver arc | the flight pauses while water darkens the soil.
X is Xeranthemum. Papery flower heads | dry-looking petals keep a crisp texture.

[Verse 7]
Y ... Y ... Yarrow! Many tiny flowers gather in flat clusters | a whole landing field in miniature.
Z? Zinnia! Layer after layer of petals | the final bright stop before the small wings leave.
