[Intro]
Leaf, root, citrus, melon—different plants bring different textures and nutrients.
No single produce card has to win the basket.

[Verse 1]

B ... B ... Banana! Starchy/sweet fruit with peel | texture changes as starches convert during ripening.
C? Cherry! Small stone fruit with pigmented skin | pit stays a safety/structure clue.
D is Date. Date-palm fruit often concentrated in sugars when dried | small portion has a different texture from fresh melon.

[Verse 2]
E — Endive! Leafy chicory with crisp/bitter leaves | leaf vegetable adds a flavor contrast.
F ... F ... Fennel! Crisp aromatic leaf bases and fronds | different plant part and aroma from fruit.
G? Guava! Tropical fruit with fragrant flesh and seeds | fruit diversity clue.
H is Horseradish. Pungent root used mainly as condiment | strong flavor does not make it a main meal by itself.

[Verse 3]
I — Iceberg Lettuce! Crisp water-rich leaves | milder texture/flavor than kale, still a leafy vegetable.
J ... J ... Jalapeno! Chile fruit containing capsaicinoids | heat sensation varies with pepper and person.
K? Kale! Leafy brassica with sturdy leaves | texture changes with preparation.
L is Lemon. Acidic citrus fruit used often for juice/zest/flavor | not usually eaten like a whole sweet fruit.

O? Orange! Citrus segments contain juice vesicles | peel/aroma separate it from apple texture.

T is Turnip. Root crop with edible greens too | one plant can provide more than one edible part.
