[Intro]
Sharp blade? Stop. Live-voltage question? Stop. Unstable ladder? Stop. Moving machine? Stay outside.
Safety gear works with rules and training, not instead of them.

[Verse 1]
A — Axe! | Sharp edged chopping tool for wood; professional/adult-only and never a preschool action target.
B ... B ... Bolt! | Externally threaded fastener commonly works with nut or threaded hole; diameter/pitch/grade must suit the joint.
C? Cement Mixer! | Rotating drum combines cement, aggregate, and water into concrete mix; moving machinery stays in restricted work zone.
D is Drill. Rotating tool makes holes or drives bits; powered drill is adult/professional equipment here. |

[Verse 2]
E — Extension Ladder! | Ladder with sliding sections for extra reach; setup and fall protection require trained adult practice.
F ... F ... Framing Hammer! | Longer/heavier carpenter hammer designed for framing; adult/professional striking tool.
G? Gravel! | Loose stone aggregate supports drainage, base layers, concrete mixes, or landscaping depending size/use.
H is Hard Hat. Protective helmet reduces head injury from some impacts/falling objects; correct type/fit matters. |

[Verse 3]
I — Impact Driver! | Powered rotary impact tool drives screws/fasteners with high torque; adult/professional operation in this preschool set.
J ... J ... Joint! | Connection where two building parts meet; joint design controls alignment, load transfer, sealing, or movement.
K? Keyhole Saw! | Narrow pointed hand saw cuts small openings/curves; sharp teeth make it adult-only here.
L is Ladder. Portable climbing equipment; stable setup, angle, footing, and fall rules matter—children do not imitate jobsite climbing. |

N ... N ... Nail! | Metal fastener driven into material; sharp point and hammer use keep it adult-managed.

P is Pliers. Hand tool grips, bends, or cuts depending jaw design; pinch/cut edges require supervised adult use. |

[Verse 5]
Q — Quick Clamp! | Clamp rapidly holds workpieces together; pinch points require careful adult positioning.
R ... R ... Rake! | Long-handled tool gathers or grades loose material; clear spacing prevents striking others.
S? Shovel! | Long-handled blade lifts or moves soil/gravel; jobsite use requires space and adult control.
T is Trowel. Hand tool spreads mortar/concrete/plaster or works soil depending shape; construction versions are adult tools. |

[Verse 6]

V ... V ... Voltage Tester! | Electrical test instrument indicates or measures voltage depending design; qualified adults use it before electrical work.
W? Wrench! | Tool turns nuts/bolts by gripping flats or fittings; correct size reduces slipping.
X is X-brace. Diagonal members cross in an X to resist racking/lateral movement in a frame. |
