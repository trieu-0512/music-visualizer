[Intro]

Listen for the letter first; then let the animal come.

[Verse 1]

B ... B ... Baboon. Baboon: primate hands, forward-looking face, and movement help identify it.
Camel steps in for C. It walks on hooves; horns, ears, neck, or coat add the next clue.
What does D reveal? Deer! Deer is hoofed wildlife; legs, hoof shape, head, and coat help identify it.

[Verse 2]
across the habitat trail, E meets Emu. Emu in its place | wings and legs give clues to trace
F—Fox! It has a dog-family silhouette with a long muzzle and walking paws.
G ... G ... Gorilla. Gorilla is a primate; hands, feet, face, and climbing posture make strong clues.
Hyena steps in for H. It is a wild mammal; body shape and habitat give the clue.

[Chorus]

Wild-world chorus, A to Z!

L ... L ... Lion. Lion is a wild cat; paws, tail, coat pattern, and feline body shape give clues.

What does N reveal? Nyala! Nyala is hoofed wildlife; legs, hoof shape, head, and coat help identify it.
across the habitat trail, O meets Okapi. It is a forest-dwelling relative of the giraffe with striped markings on its legs.
P—Panda! A giant panda is a bear with black-and-white fur that eats mostly bamboo.

across the habitat trail, T meets Tapir. It is a plant-eating mammal with a short flexible snout and sturdy body.

Wolf steps in for W. It is a wild canid; muzzle, pointed ears, paws, and tail help reveal it.
What does X reveal? Xenopus! Xenopus is an aquatic frog with webbed hind feet and eyes positioned high on its head.
