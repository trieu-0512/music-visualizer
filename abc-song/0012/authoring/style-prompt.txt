Clear warm adult lead with crisp consonants. Gentle 6/8 country-pop at 90 BPM with muted banjo, upright bass, brushes and one small bell color used as navigation, not constant decoration. Open with two separated bell rings—near then farther—so the listener learns the route idea immediately. Round 1 should move spatially from barn to stall, field, grain area, orchard, tools and animal care; each four-letter block feels like another stop. Use delayed rhyme and repeated vowel colors across a block when natural, but let technical lines stay plain. Chorus is the near/far bell hook. Round 2 begins with a single bell cue, leaves a real silent beat, then confirms the target; never strike the bell on the target's first syllable. Keep rare or technical targets near speech rhythm. Stable harmony, no fast bluegrass picking, no cowbell barrage. End with one distant bell and a clean cadence.
