[Intro]
Ding—barn door. Ding—field path.
Follow each ring; every stop holds a letter.

[Verse 1]
First bell, A — Apple! Round fruit rests in a farm basket | our route begins with a crisp orchard clue.
B is Barn. Door on hinges, roof overhead | shelter for animals, hay, tools, or supplies.
At the near stall, C is Cow. Chew, step, moo | split hooves press the bedding slow.
D? Donkey! Long ears rise above the half-door | sturdy hooves wait in the lane.

[Verse 2]
Second ring finds E — Ewe. Wool over her back | an adult female sheep moving with the flock.
F is Farmer. Boots cross from barn to field | caring for crops, animals, land, and daily jobs.
At the fence, G is Goat. Cloven hooves climb and balance | a curious browser reaching for leaves.
H? Hay! Dry stems stacked high | cut plants saved as animal feed.

[Verse 3]
Next bell, I — Ice Chest. Insulated walls, lid shut | helping food or drinks stay cold beside the work.
J is Jar. Wide mouth, firm sides, lid on top | small things stay gathered inside.
At the grain bin, K is Kernel. One tiny seed | corn or another grain held in your fingertips.
L? Ladder! Rung over rung reaches up | an adult tool children only observe safely.

[Chorus]
Ding near, ding far—A-B-C!
Every ring reveals what we see!

[Verse 4]
Midday bell, M — Milk. Pale food made by mammals | on dairy farms it may be collected from animals such as cows or goats.
N is Nectarine. Smooth skin, round fruit | ripening among the orchard leaves.
Beyond the barn, O is Orchard. Tree after tree | fruit or nuts growing together.
P? Pitchfork! Long handle, pointed tines | a farm tool we identify from a safe distance.

[Verse 5]
Bell carries to Q — Quail. Low bird, quick feet | pecking under field-edge cover.
R is Rake. Long handle, teeth in a row | adults gather loose material across the ground.
At the field, S is Scarecrow. Coat and hat on a still frame | a crop-side shape meant to discourage birds.
T? Tractor! Engine hum grows near | heavy machine ready to pull or operate farm equipment.

[Verse 6]
Last barn ring, U — Udder. Milk-producing organ beneath animals such as cows, goats, and sheep.
V is Vegetable Basket. Gathered produce settles together | ready to be carried from the field.
By the gate, W is Wagon. Four wheels turn | crops and supplies travel together.
X? X-ray Vet Image! A picture from inside | helping a veterinarian see bones without opening the body.

[Verse 7]
One more ring, Y — Yam. Thick tuber underground | food stored where soil hides it.
Z is Zebu. At the far pen, a shoulder hump rises over sturdy cattle | the final stop on our bell-route.

Every ring reveals what we see!
