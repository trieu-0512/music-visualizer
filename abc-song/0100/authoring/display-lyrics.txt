[Intro]
Recipe read, hands washed, ingredients measured; raw flour and raw egg are not tasting samples.
Our hundredth song follows the baking process all the way to cooling.

[Verse 1]
A — Apple Pie! Fruit filling and pastry crust transform with heat | hot oven and pie dish stay adult-managed.
B ... B ... Brownie! Batter sets into dense crumb during baking | raw batter is not a tasting activity because flour/eggs may carry pathogens.
C? Cookie! Portioned dough spreads/sets/browns with heat | cool before handling.
D is Donut. Can be fried or baked depending recipe | hot oil is especially adult-only.

[Verse 2]
E — Eclair! Choux dough uses steam expansion to create hollow shells | shells cool before filling.
F ... F ... Frosting! Finishing layer belongs on a cooled cake | heat can melt the intended structure.
G? Gingerbread! Spiced dough/batter can become cookie, loaf, or cake | recipe determines structure.
H is Honey Bun. Yeasted sweet dough proofs, bakes, then may receive glaze | sequence matters.

[Chorus]
Plan, make, cool—A-B-C!
Heat belongs with the adult; finishing waits for the bake to settle.

[Verse 3]
I — Icing! Finishing mixture can pipe/spread/pour depending consistency | added after appropriate cooling.
J ... J ... Jelly Donut! Cooked donut receives jelly/jam filling | filling stage follows safe cooking/cooling process.
K? Kolache! Yeasted dough supports a traditional sweet filling | proofing gives the dough time to rise.
L is Lemon Tart. Pastry shell and lemon filling require recipe-specific bake/chill sequence | adult heat management.

[Verse 4]
M — Muffin! Quick batter is combined briefly, portioned, then baked | overworking can change crumb texture.
N ... N ... Nut Bread! Quick/yeast loaf depending recipe with nuts through crumb | nut allergens make label/household awareness important.
O? Orange Cake! Citrus zest/juice can flavor batter or finish | aromatic peel oils add character before/after bake depending recipe.
P is Pie. Crust and filling require recipe-specific assembly and baking | hot steam/filling can burn after oven removal.

[Verse 5]
Q — Queen Cake! Small cake mapping with recipe variation | workflow follows actual recipe rather than the name alone.
R ... R ... Raisin Bread! Raisins distribute through bread dough | yeast fermentation and baking form the loaf.
S? Sprinkles! Decorative finish applied after baking, often onto icing/frosting | finish stage, not oven ingredient by default.
T is Tart. Shallow pastry shell with filling | assembly and baking/chilling vary by filling.

[Chorus]
Plan, make, cool—A-B-C!
Heat belongs with the adult; finishing waits for the bake to settle.

[Verse 6]
U — Ube Roll! Rolled cake uses ube/purple-yam flavor or preparation | ube is Dioscorea alata, not taro; roll/fill after appropriate cooling.
V ... V ... Vanilla Cake! Cake structure sets with heat; vanilla supplies aroma/flavor | cake cools before many finishes.
W? Wholegrain Bread! Includes whole-grain flour components such as bran/germ | dough fermentation/baking produce loaf structure.
X is Xmas Cookie. Holiday-decoration label, not one recipe | cool baked cookie before icing/decorating.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yule Log! Sponge is rolled with filling and decorated as a log | timing keeps the cake flexible enough without unsafe hot handling.
Z ... Z ... Zucchini Bread! Grated zucchini adds water/plant solids to quick-bread batter | bake fully, cool, then slice.
