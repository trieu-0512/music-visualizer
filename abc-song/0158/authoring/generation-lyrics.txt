[Intro]
A receptionist can route a request, an interpreter can carry meaning, a nurse and doctor can coordinate care.
Community help often moves through a team.

[Verse 1]
A — Architect! | Design professional plans buildings/spaces with drawings, codes, structure, accessibility, and collaboration; architect is not the builder operating site tools.
B ... B ... Baker! | Food professional produces breads/pastries using combining, fermentation, shaping, ovens, and food-safety systems.
C? Courier! | Worker transports documents/parcels between locations; route, custody, and delivery confirmation may matter.
D is Doctor. Licensed medical physician diagnoses/treats illness and manages health within training/specialty; clinical procedures require consent and evidence. |

[Verse 2]
E — Electrician! | Trained tradesperson installs/tests electrical systems; live electricity requires isolation, testing, and professional procedures.
F ... F ... Firefighter! | Emergency responder handles fires, rescue, hazards, and prevention; scenes require protective gear, team command, and public distance.
G? Garbage Collector! | Worker collects waste/recycling; moving trucks, compactors, traffic, and sharp waste create safety zones.
H is Helper. General relationship word for someone assisting; it is not one formal profession or fixed skill set. |
