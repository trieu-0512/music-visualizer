[Intro]
Cone says where, lane says through, whistle can say stop, water break says pause.
Shared sports space works when everyone can read the same signals.

C? Cricket Bat! | Flat-bladed bat used by a batter in cricket; ball speed means sport rules and protective equipment matter.
