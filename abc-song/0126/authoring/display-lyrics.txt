[Intro]
Car takes road, train takes rail, helicopter takes sky, bulldozer stays in a work zone.
Things that go do not all share one route.

[Verse 1]
A — ATV! | All-terrain vehicle uses low-pressure tires and straddle seating for off-road travel; it requires trained adult operation, helmet, and approved terrain.
B ... B ... Bulldozer! | Tracked construction machine pushes soil or debris with a large front blade; heavy equipment stays inside controlled work zones.
C? Car! | Road motor vehicle mainly designed to carry people; propulsion can be combustion, electric, hybrid, or other systems.
D is Delivery Van. Enclosed road van carries parcels or goods between stops; loading and parking are part of its service role. |

[Verse 2]
E — Excavator! | Construction machine uses boom, stick, and bucket rotating on an upper structure; it digs rather than simply 'drives'.
F ... F ... Fire Engine! | Emergency vehicle carries firefighters, hoses, pumps, ladders, or equipment; other traffic yields according to local law when emergency signals operate.
G? Golf Cart! | Small low-speed vehicle designed mainly for golf courses or controlled properties; road legality and safety rules vary by location.
H is Helicopter. Rotorcraft gets lift from rotating wings and can hover; it is an aircraft, not a road vehicle. |
