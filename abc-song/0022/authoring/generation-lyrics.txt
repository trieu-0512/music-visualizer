[Intro]
Kneel by one curled leaf; a whole forest begins underneath it.
Seed, stone, root, wing—different lives share the same floor.

[Verse 1]
A — Acorn! Hard shell, little cap | an oak seed resting where leaves collect.
B ... B ... Bear! Broad paws cross the litter | a large mammal passing over the small world below.
C? Cedar! Roots hold beneath the surface | green branches rise far above our ground-level view.
D is Douglas Fir. Tall evergreen, needle-like leaves, hanging cones | roots begin where the forest floor ends.

[Verse 2]
Look up for E — Eagle. Strong wings cross the opening | a high visitor above our low forest map.
F ... F ... Fern! Curled young growth unfurls | divided fronds arch over damp litter.
G? Grass! Narrow leaves rise from rooted bases | a soft green edge where light reaches the floor.
H is Hedgehog. Spines above, small feet below | it moves close to ground cover and can curl for protection.

[Chorus]
Leaf, root, stone—A-B-C!
The forest floor holds stories under every tree.

[Verse 3]
I — Insect! Six legs, three main body parts | tiny movement under bark, leaf, or stem.
J ... J ... Jay! A forest bird drops to the ground, then lifts back to branches | beak and feet working between layers.
K? Kingfisher! Near the river edge, a strong bill points toward water | one bright hunter beside the forest floor.
L is Lynx. Wide quiet paws cross low cover | a wild cat moving without needing a trail.

[Verse 4]
M — Mushroom! Cap rises from damp litter | the visible fruiting body appears above hidden fungal growth.
N ... N ... Newt! Small amphibian near moist leaves | cool cover helps keep its skin from drying.
O? Oak! Deep roots below, broad crown above | and every acorn begins another possible tree.
P is Pine. Needles gather overhead | cones and old needles add new texture to the ground.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quartz Rock! Hard, glassy-looking mineral | a bright edge among dark soil and roots.
R ... R ... River! Water cuts beside the forest floor | carrying sound, moisture, and moving reflections.
S? Squirrel! Quick paws search leaf litter, then climb | one animal moving between ground and branch.
T is Tree. Root below, trunk through, canopy high | one body connecting every forest layer.

[Chorus]
Leaf, root, stone—A-B-C!
The forest floor holds stories under every tree.

[Verse 6]
U — Underbrush! Shrubs and low plants fill the space below taller trees | a thick middle layer above the litter.
V ... V ... Violet! Small leaves and low flowers sit close to the soil | color at knee height.
W? Woodpecker! It works on trunks above the floor | chips, bark, and sound connect our low view to the tree.
X is Xeric Shrub. A shrub adapted to drier conditions | a reminder that not every forest patch holds the same moisture.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Warbler! Small yellow bird among leaves | a moving point of color above the ground layers.
Z ... Z ... Zebra Swallowtail! Striped butterfly wings pass over the low plants | then lift beyond our close-up view.
