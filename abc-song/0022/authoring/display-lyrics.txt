[Intro]
Look down—moss, bark, one curled leaf.
The forest floor has letters underneath.

[Verse 1]
Acorn — that's A. It is the hard-shelled seed of an oak tree, topped by a little cup.
Spot B: Bear! A bear is a large furry mammal with strong legs, broad paws, and an excellent sense of smell.
C brings Cedar. Tree to sky | branches lift up high
D? Douglas Fir! A Douglas fir is a tall evergreen tree with needle-like leaves and hanging cones.

[Verse 2]
Under the trees, here is E with Eagle. Feather light | beak and wings come into sight
Fern — that's F. It unfurls divided green fronds from curled young growth called fiddleheads.
Spot G: Grass! Root down low | leaves reach up and grow
H brings Hedgehog. It has a coat of stiff spines and can curl its body into a tight ball.

[Chorus]

Kingfisher — that's K. Flash and splash | dive for fish, then back in a dash
Spot L: Lynx! A lynx is a furry mammal that walks quietly and uses keen senses to explore its habitat.

[Verse 4]
M brings Mushroom. It is the fruiting body of a fungus, often shaped like a cap on a stalk.

Under the trees, here is O with Oak. Tree to sky | branches lift up high
Pine — that's P. It is an evergreen tree with bundles of needles and woody cones.

[Verse 5]
Spot Q: Quartz Rock! Quartz is a hard mineral that can look glassy or sparkle in rock.

[Verse 6]

Spot V: Violet! A violet is a small low-growing plant with soft purple, blue, white, or yellow flowers.
W brings Woodpecker. Tap-tap tree | woodpecker searches bark carefully
X? Xeric Shrub! A xeric shrub is a woody plant adapted to dry conditions.

[Verse 7]
Under the trees, here is Y with Yellow Warbler. Wing to sky | feathered bird goes gliding by
Zebra Swallowtail — that's Z. It is a butterfly with black-and-white striped wings and long tails.
