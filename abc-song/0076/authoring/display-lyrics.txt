[Intro]
The gorilla may forage; the tiger may sleep; the animal can choose what to do.
A good zoo visit watches without demanding a show.

[Verse 1]
A — Ape! Broad primate category without a tail | grasping hands and flexible shoulders support varied climbing and ground movement.
B ... B ... Bison! Massive grazing bovine with shoulder hump and curved horns | herd space and grazing behavior matter more than a close photo.
C? Cheetah! Slender cat specialized for short high-speed pursuit | resting occupies far more time than sprinting.
D is Dhole. Social Asian wild dog often hunts and communicates in groups | not a domestic dog for petting.

[Verse 2]
E — Elephant! Trunk handles food, water, touch and smell | complex social life needs space, choice, and stimulation.
F ... F ... Flamingo! Long-legged filter feeder often lives socially in flocks | shallow water supports feeding and bathing.
G? Gorilla! Great ape mainly eats plant material | foraging, resting, climbing and social behavior all matter.
H is Hippo. Semi-aquatic mammal spends much daytime in water | extremely dangerous in the wild, always distance-only.

[Chorus]
Watch, read, respect—A-B-C!
Wild animals stay wild, even behind a viewing window.

[Verse 3]
I — Ibex! Wild goat with sure hooves and curved horns | rocky climbing habitat supports natural movement.
J ... J ... Jackal! Wild canid of Africa/Eurasia depending species | scavenging and hunting roles vary.
K? Koala! Australian tree-dwelling marsupial specialized on eucalyptus leaves | long rest helps conserve energy.
L is Lion. Social big cat living in prides in the wild | resting is normal behavior, not laziness for visitors.

[Verse 4]
M — Meerkat! Social mongoose scans, digs, forages and uses burrows | upright lookout is only one behavior.
N ... N ... Nile Crocodile! Large African crocodilian ambush predator | stillness is part of its biology, not an invitation to approach.
O? Okapi! Central African forest relative of giraffe | striped legs and long tongue fit a browsing life.
P is Panda. Giant panda specializes heavily on bamboo | feeding and resting dominate much of its day.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quail! Small ground bird | cover and ground-foraging space matter more than flight display.
R ... R ... Rhino! Large herbivore with keratin horn | viewing distance protects both animal and visitor.
S? Seal! Marine mammal swims with flippers and hauls out to rest | water and dry resting space serve different needs.
T is Tiger. Solitary big cat in the wild | scent, cover, climbing/resting options and privacy support behavior.

[Verse 6]
U — Uakari! Amazonian monkey with short tail and bare face | canopy movement needs complex climbing space.
V ... V ... Vicuna! South American camelid of high Andes | light body and fine coat suit cold dry elevations.
W? Wolf! Social wild canid with large territories in nature | pack behavior cannot be reduced to howling on cue.
X is Xerus. African ground squirrel | digging and alert posture fit open dry habitats.

[Chorus]
Watch, read, respect—A-B-C!
Wild animals stay wild, even behind a viewing window.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Mongoose! Southern African mongoose | small predator forages on ground and uses burrows.
Z ... Z ... Zebu! Domestic cattle type with shoulder hump | human-managed animal, distinct from the wild species around it.
