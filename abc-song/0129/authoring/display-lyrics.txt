[Intro]
A bus carries many people, a pickup carries a load, an electric car changes the powertrain—not the need for a safe road.
Transport choices have purpose, route, and energy.

G? Golf Cart! | Small low-speed vehicle designed mainly for golf courses or controlled properties; road legality and safety rules vary by location.

S? Snowplow! | Vehicle or attachment pushes snow from travel surfaces; operators need visibility and people should stay clear of the blade/discharge.
