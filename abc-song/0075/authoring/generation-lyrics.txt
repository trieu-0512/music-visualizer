[Intro]
Before choosing an animal, read the care sheet and prepare the habitat at home.
A picture can start interest; responsibility decides what happens next.

[Verse 1]
A — Aquarium! Prepare and cycle an appropriate aquatic system before fish arrive | empty setup first, animal later.
B ... B ... Bowl! Basic supply, but owning bowls proves nothing about readiness for a species.
C? Collar! Dog/cat identification or control item may be useful | correct fit comes after species and individual choice.
D is Dog. Long-term commitment includes training, exercise, health care, housing and time | individual needs vary.

[Verse 2]
E — Exercise Wheel! Species-specific enrichment, not a generic sign that any small pet belongs in the same cage.
F ... F ... Fish! Research adult size, schooling/territorial behavior, water parameters and lifespan before choosing a species.
G? Guinea Pig! Social herbivore with hay-centered diet, vitamin C needs and large floor space | plan compatible companionship and vet access.
H is Hamster. Nocturnal burrower needs deep bedding, space and correct wheel | tiny starter cages can be inadequate.

[Verse 3]
I — Iguana! Specialized reptile with large adult size and demanding heat/UVB/diet needs | a serious long-term commitment.
J ... J ... Jingle Ball! Optional toy comes after habitat, nutrition, health and safety planning.
K? Kibble! Food supply must be appropriate to species and life stage | price and storage belong in the ongoing budget.
L is Litter Box. Cat supply needs ongoing cleaning, litter cost and suitable placement | readiness includes routine chores.

[Chorus]
Learn, prepare, commit—A-B-C!
If the lifelong needs do not fit, walking away is good care too.

[Verse 4]
M — Mouse! Research enclosure, social grouping, lifespan, enrichment and veterinary access before adoption.
N ... N ... Nest! Habitat feature may be needed for certain birds/rodents | never collect wild nests or assume every species needs one.
O? Outdoor Kennel! Shelter cannot replace family contact, exercise, climate safety or secure fencing.
P is Perch. Bird setup requires multiple safe perch sizes and flight space | one perch does not make a cage adequate.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quail! Ground bird needs floor space, cover, social planning and appropriate housing | research local rules and welfare before keeping.
R ... R ... Rope Toy! Optional enrichment needs inspection and replacement | budget for wear.
S? Seed Mix! Package on a shelf is not automatically a complete diet | species-specific nutrition research comes first.
T is Turtle. Many turtles live for decades and need large, specialized aquatic/terrestrial setup with heat and UVB | plan adult needs, not hatchling size.

[Verse 6]
U — UV Lamp! For reptiles requiring UVB, choose correct UVB output, placement and replacement schedule | equipment is a recurring responsibility.
V ... V ... Vet Kit! Plan access to a species-experienced veterinarian and budget for preventive or emergency care.
W? Wheel! Correct exercise wheel is one component of a whole habitat | size and surface matter.
X is Xylophone Toy, our supply-picture clue | optional noise toy never outranks species welfare or calm choice.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Canary! Research lifespan, flight space, diet, light cycle and avian-vet access before bringing one home.
Z ... Z ... Zip Carrier! Buy or prepare secure transport before the first trip | carrier is part of emergency and veterinary planning.

[Chorus]
Learn, prepare, commit—A-B-C!
If the lifelong needs do not fit, walking away is good care too.

[Interlude]
Playtime pauses for fresh water and one calm breath. Now remember the pet words.

[Call and Response 1]
Readiness A ... Aquarium! Frame a tank; wave water inside.
Plan B ... Bowl! Cup both hands like a bowl.
Need or supply—C? ... Collar! Trace a collar circle in the air; keep it off your neck.
Decision check D ... Dog! Make one sniffing nose gesture, then a paw step.

[Call and Response 2]
Readiness E ... Exercise Wheel! Trace a wheel; run two finger-steps.
Plan F ... Fish! Swim one hand slowly through the air.
Need or supply—G? ... Guinea Pig! Make a round little body, then two short walking steps.
Decision check H ... Hamster! Puff cheek pouches; run tiny finger-steps.

[Chorus]
Learn, prepare, commit—A-B-C!
If the lifelong needs do not fit, walking away is good care too.

[Call and Response 3]
Readiness I ... Iguana! Trace a long tail; take one low step.
Plan J ... Jingle Ball! Roll a pretend ball; stop on the beat.
Need or supply—K? ... Kibble! Point to pretend food pieces beside the bowl.
Decision check L ... Litter Box! Frame a shallow box shape, then point to it.

[Call and Response 4]
Readiness M ... Mouse! Show whiskers and one long tail line, then scurry twice.
Plan N ... Nest! Cup both hands into a bird nest.
Need or supply—O? ... Outdoor Kennel! Frame a secure kennel outline, then point to the shelter area.
Decision check P ... Perch! Hold one finger like a branch, then perch the other hand.

[Call and Response 5]
Readiness Q ... Quail! Make a plump bird shape, then two ground steps.
Plan R ... Rope Toy! Twist two empty hands like a rope; no real pulling.
Need or supply—S? ... Seed Mix! Dot three tiny pretend seeds beside the bird picture.
Decision check T ... Turtle! Make a shell dome; step slowly once.

[Outro]
List checked; no impulse purchase; every animal stays more important than the display.
A to Z ends with readiness, whether or not a pet comes home.
