[Intro]
Click... spin... the little vane turns.
Our weather station has a letter to learn.

[Verse 1]
Look at A: Anemometer! Spin in the breeze | wind-speed readings come with ease
B — Breeze. A breeze is a light wind you may feel moving across your skin or leaves.
Under the changing sky, C meets Coat. Coat wrapped round | warm outer layer when cold comes down
D ... D ... Drizzle! Drizzle is very light rain made of small drops.

[Verse 2]
What did E find? Earmuffs! Ears tucked warm | padded cups for winter storm
Look at F: Fog! It is a cloud close to the ground that makes distant things harder to see.
G — Gauge. A gauge has a scale, pointer, or display that shows a measurement.
Under the changing sky, H meets Hail. It is made of balls or lumps of ice that fall from storm clouds.

[Chorus]
Weather station—A-B-C!
Measure, look, and sing with me!

[Verse 3]
I ... I ... Ice! Cold and bright | frozen water catches light
What did J find? Jet Stream! It is a fast-moving band of air high in the atmosphere.
Look at K: Kelvin Thermometer! Kelvin scale | temperature numbers tell the tale
L — Low Cloud. A low cloud forms close to the ground compared with higher cloud layers.

[Verse 4]
Under the changing sky, M meets Mittens. Hands tucked in | fingers share the warmth within
N ... N ... Nimbus Cloud! Nimbus is a weather word associated with rain-bearing clouds.
What did O find? Overcast Sky! Clouds spread wide | one gray layer side to side
Look at P: Pressure Gauge! It shows how much pressure is present in air or another fluid.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilt. Stitch by stitch | warm layered fabric, soft and rich
Under the changing sky, R meets Rainbow. A rainbow appears when light is separated into colors by water droplets in the air.
S ... S ... Sun! Sun up high | daylight warms us from the sky
What did T find? Thermometer! It measures temperature.

[Chorus]
Weather station—A-B-C!
Measure, look, and sing with me!

[Verse 6]
Look at U: Updraft! Air goes high | rising current climbs the sky
V — Vane. A weather vane turns to show the direction the wind comes from.
Under the changing sky, W meets Wind. Whoosh and bend | moving air from end to end
X ... X ... Xylophone! A xylophone is our X-word helper: a row of tuned bars makes different notes when struck.

[Verse 7]
What did Y find? Yellow Sun! Picture sun high | bright yellow circle in the sky
Look at Z: Zephyr! It is a soft, gentle breeze.
