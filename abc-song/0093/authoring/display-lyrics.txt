[Intro]
Raspberry bruises easily; horseradish root is firm; xoconostle needs careful adult preparation.
Market handling starts by noticing structure.

[Verse 1]
A — Apricot! Soft stone fruit with central pit | ripe fruit bruises more easily than a root vegetable.
B ... B ... Banana! Peel protects soft flesh but bruises under pressure | curve and peel identify without squeezing.
C? Cherry! Small stone fruit with stem and hard pit | choking/pit hazards mean adult preparation for young children.
D is Date. Oval date-palm fruit, often sold fresh or dried | one elongated stone inside.

[Verse 2]
E — Eggplant! Smooth-skinned berry with leafy calyx | firm but pressure can bruise.
F ... F ... Fig! Very soft ripe fruit with delicate skin and many tiny internal structures | gentle-support card.
G? Guava! Firm-to-soft tropical fruit with many seeds | aroma/shape help ID without pressing.
H is Horseradish. Pungent root used as condiment after grating | adult prep because grating is sharp and vapors can irritate.

[Chorus]
Look, support, separate—A-B-C!
Soft, leafy, firm, or spiny: handle the picture by its clues.

[Verse 3]
I — Indian Fig! Prickly-pear cactus fruit | tiny glochids can remain on skin, so adult-safe handling/preparation only.
J ... J ... Jackfruit! Very large multiple fruit with thick rind and sticky latex | adult cutting/prep.
K? Kale! Leafy greens with broad/curled leaves | keep leaves supported rather than crushed.
L is Leek. Long layered leaf sheaths often trap soil | washing/prep happens before cooking.

[Verse 4]
M — Melon! Large rind-covered fruit | support with two hands if real, but song uses picture-only sorting.
N ... N ... Nectarine! Smooth-skinned stone fruit | peach relative without fuzzy skin.
O? Okra! Ridged green pod, often tapered | pod structure identifies it.
P is Peach. Soft fuzzy stone fruit | gentle handling prevents bruising.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quince! Firm aromatic pome fruit, usually cooked before eating because raw flesh is hard/astringent in many cultivars.
R ... R ... Raspberry! Aggregate berry-like fruit made of many drupelets | highly delicate handling.
S? Strawberry! Aggregate accessory fruit with surface achenes | soft-fruit card.
T is Turnip. Firm swollen root/hypocotyl with leafy tops | root-vegetable handling.

[Chorus]
Look, support, separate—A-B-C!
Soft, leafy, firm, or spiny: handle the picture by its clues.

[Verse 6]
U — Ugli Fruit! Branded Jamaican tangelo with rough loose-looking citrus peel | appearance is not spoilage by itself.
V ... V ... Vanilla Bean! Cured vanilla orchid fruit sold as dark flexible pod | aroma/spice item, used in small amounts.
W? Watercress! Tender leafy aquatic/semi-aquatic herb sold in bunches | leaf/stem card.
X is Xoconostle. Sour Opuntia cactus fruit | spines/glochids make it adult-prep only.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yam! Firm starchy tuber | species differ; cook appropriately rather than eating unknown raw tubers.
Z ... Z ... Zucchini! Tender summer squash fruit with soft edible skin | firm-fruit vegetable-use card.
