[Intro]
Empty basket, cool morning, orchard rows ahead.
Rustle, lift, carry—the harvest day begins.

[Verse 1]
Empty basket, A — Apple! Round fruit twists from the branch | first harvest weight in our hands.
B is Bucket. Handle lifts, open top waits | water or gathered things can travel inside.
C? Chicken! Scratch and peck beside the orchard path | quick feet stir dry earth.
D ... D ... Duck! At the pond, webbed feet paddle | harvest day keeps moving past the water.

[Verse 2]
Gather clue E — Egg. Smooth oval shell | set carefully, never tossed.
F is Fence. Post after post | it marks where field ends and path begins.
G? Goat! Cloven hooves at the gate | a curious browser reaches for leaves.
H — Hay. Cut, dried, stacked | golden feed saved after the field is cleared.

[Chorus]
Fill the basket—A-B-C!
Harvest day grows full by Z!

[Verse 3]
Next load, I — Irrigation Pump. Water pushed through hose and pipe helped crops reach harvest.
J ... J ... Jug! Handle, narrow mouth | liquid pours in a steady stream.
K? Kid Goat! Quick hooves circle the busy yard | a young goat curious about every lift and step.
L is Ladder. Rung over rung | adults use it to reach high places; children watch safely.

[Verse 4]
Midday break, M — Milk. Pale food produced by mammals | common in dairy-farm life.
N ... N ... Nest. Twigs cradle eggs or chicks | a different kind of gathering place.
O? Orchard! Tree after tree | this is where many fruit harvests begin.
P is Pitchfork. Long handle, pointed tines | adult farm tool, seen and named from a safe distance.

[Verse 5]
Fuller day, Q — Quilt. Stitch after stitch | soft layers come together as one warm blanket.
R ... R ... Rake! Teeth in a row | adults pull loose plant material into a pile.
S? Sheep! Woolly fleece in the field | grazing while harvest work passes nearby.
T is Tractor. Big wheels carry the work onward | pulling or powering heavy equipment.

[Chorus]
Fill the basket—A-B-C!
Harvest day grows full by Z!

[Verse 6]
Last loads, U — Utility Cart. Tools and supplies ride together from job to job.
V ... V ... Vegetable Basket! This one really fills | gathered produce settles side by side.
W? Wheat! Tall grain heads hold the seeds | grain later ground to make flour.
X is Xylophone—our porch-side X helper | tuned bars turn the harvest rhythm into notes.

[Verse 7]
Near the bottom, Y — Yam. Thick underground tuber | harvest comes up from soil, not down from a branch.
Z ... Z ... Zebu. Shoulder hump above sturdy cattle | one last farm clue as the full basket heads home.
