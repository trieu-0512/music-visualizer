Walking art-pop at 90 BPM with guitar, soft bass, woodblock and marimba; give each category a distinct two-note motif. Keep the mix dry around category words and thin the ending into four separated motifs. Opening stays sparse. Round 1 teaches with a narrow stepwise melody; Round 2 retrieval leaves a response gap. Arrangement builds lightly into the chorus, then thins. Duck backing vocals and percussion around every target-word. End with a clean cadence.
