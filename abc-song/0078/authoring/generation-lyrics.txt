[Intro]
Forest, desert, wetland, mountain, ocean—some animals fit one strongly, others cross several.
Habitat is a relationship, not a single permanent box.

[Verse 1]
A — Ape! Apes occupy African/Asian forests and, for some, mosaics of woodland/grassland | broad category needs species detail.
B ... B ... Baboon! Many baboons use savanna, woodland and rocky habitats | adaptable ground-traveling primates.
C? Camel! Dromedary and Bactrian camels suit arid environments | broad padded feet and fat-storing humps support dry-land life.
D is Deer. Deer species occupy forests, grasslands, wetlands and tundra edges | not one habitat box.

[Verse 2]
E — Elephant! African and Asian elephants use forests, savannas, grasslands and wetlands depending species/population.
F ... F ... Flamingo! Shallow saline/alkaline lakes, lagoons and wetlands provide filter-feeding habitat.
G? Gorilla! Central African forest great ape | dense vegetation and fruit/leaf resources define the core habitat.
H is Hyena. Species range from savanna and scrub to desert/woodland | not only open grassland.

[Verse 3]
I — Iguana! Mostly tropical American lizards using trees, ground, islands or rocky coast depending species.
J ... J ... Jaguar! Forest, wetland, scrub and grassland can all support jaguars | strong swimmer as well as land predator.
K? Koala! Australian eucalyptus woodland/forest specialist | tree food makes habitat unusually specific.
L is Lemur. Madagascar primates occupy rainforest, dry forest, spiny forest and more | island diversity creates many habitat fits.

[Verse 4]
M — Meerkat! Southern African arid/open habitats | burrows help manage heat and predation.
N ... N ... Nyala! Southern African antelope favors dense woodland/thickets near water | cover matters.
O? Ostrich! Open African savanna and semi-arid habitats | running body fits wide ground space.
P is Panda. Giant panda lives in cool mountain forests with abundant bamboo | specialized forest-mountain overlap.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quokka! Southwestern Australian marsupial using dense vegetation and island/mainland habitats | not desert stereotype.
R ... R ... Red Panda! Himalayan temperate mountain forest with bamboo understory | forest plus elevation.
S? Seal! Marine/coastal mammal uses ocean for feeding and land/ice for haul-out depending species.
T is Tapir. Tropical forest and wet habitats in Central/South America or Southeast Asia depending species | water often nearby.

[Verse 6]
U — Urial! Dry mountain slopes, steppe and rocky terrain across Central/South Asia.
V ... V ... Vulture! Habitat follows food, cliffs/trees for roosting, and soaring conditions | open landscapes often useful.
W? Walrus! Arctic/sub-Arctic marine mammal tied to shallow seas, coasts and sea ice | not temperate zoo-water generic.
X is Xenopus. African clawed frog genus lives fully aquatic in freshwater | pond/stream system, not dry land.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yak! Domestic/wild yak adapted to high Tibetan Plateau and Himalayan-region cold uplands | elevation and cold define the card.
Z ... Z ... Zebra! African grasslands and open woodlands depending species | grazing habitat closes the matrix.
