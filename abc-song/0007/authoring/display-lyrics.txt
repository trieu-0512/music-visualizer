[Intro]
Paddle left. Pause. What's round the bend?
A new letter waits where the water turns.

[Verse 1]
Launch at A: Alligator! Bank-side rest | then a quiet glide comes next.
B ... B ... Beaver! Branch by branch, flat tail paddling as sharp teeth cut wood.
Catfish — that's C. Whisker-like barbels feel around its mouth as it swims.
D — Dam. A wall holds water back and changes the current's path.

[Verse 2]
First bend—what's waiting at E? Egret! Stand and scan | long legs pause in shallow water.
At the bank, F: Fishing Rod! Long pole, fishing line, and reel—three parts easy to spot.
G ... G ... Goose! Glide wide | paddles on water, then walks grassy banks.
Heron — that's H. Hold still | then one long-legged step through the shallows.

[Verse 3]
I — Insect. Six in line | six legs, three main body parts in the design.
What's waiting at J? Jetty! A structure reaches from the shore out over water.
Next reach, K: Kayak! On the track | double paddle forward and back.
L ... L ... Lake! A large body of water held inside surrounding land.

[Chorus]
Paddle, pause—what's round the bend?
Say the word, then paddle again.

[Verse 4]
Minnow — that's M. Quick little flick | small fish darts through shallow water.
N — Nest. Rest in the nest | bird-built place for eggs and chicks.
What's waiting at O? Oar! Row as we go | long blade pulls water to move a boat.
Paddle to P: Pike! Long freshwater fish waiting still among water plants.

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Quillwort! Spiky little leaves make this wet-place plant easy to spot.
Around another bend, R is River. Bend after bend | water runs downhill through a channel.
S — Salamander. Long body, moist skin, four legs, and a tail behind.
What's waiting at T? Trout! Tail flick | cool-water fish pushes against the current.

[Verse 6]
Paddle to U: Umbrella Sedge! Green rays spread | leaf-like spokes above the stem.
V ... V ... Valley! Low between hills, often giving running water a way through.
Waterfall — that's W. High to low | rushing water drops from rock below.
X — X-ray Tetra. A small freshwater fish with a silvery body and partly transparent middle.

[Chorus]
Paddle, pause—what's round the bend?
Say the word, then paddle again.

[Verse 7]
Near the last turn, what's waiting at Y? Yellow Water Lily! Yellow in light | broad leaves float, flower bright.
Paddle to Z: Zooplankton! Tiny drifters move wherever the water carries them.

C ... C ... Catfish! Wiggle fingers like whiskers, then swim.
Ready for D? ... Dam! Hold two palms together like a wall.

[Call and Response 2]

Paddle to F ... Fishing Rod! Pretend to hold a fishing rod safely.
G ... G ... Goose! Glide one hand across the water.
Ready for H? ... Heron! Hold two fingers tall, then take one slow step.

[Call and Response 3]
I ... Insect! Make tiny walking fingers.
Paddle to J ... Jetty! Slide one hand straight out over imaginary water.
K ... K ... Kayak! Paddle left, paddle right.
Ready for L? ... Lake! Spread both hands wide and calm.

[Call and Response 4]
M ... Minnow! Dart one finger gently side to side.
Paddle to N ... Nest! Cup both hands like a nest.
O ... O ... Oar! Row one slow stroke.
Ready for P? ... Pike! Hold still, then make one quick fish-swim.

[Chorus]
Paddle, pause—what's round the bend?

[Call and Response 5]
Q ... Quillwort! Spread fingers upward like spiky leaves.
Paddle to R ... River! Move one hand in a winding path.
S ... S ... Salamander! Walk two fingers, then wiggle a tail.
Ready for T? ... Trout! Flick one hand gently like a tail.

[Call and Response 6]
U ... Umbrella Sedge! Open fingers like a tiny green umbrella.
Paddle to V ... Valley! Dip both hands down between two hills.
W ... W ... Waterfall! Let both hands tumble gently downward.
Ready for X? ... X-ray Tetra! Make a gentle fish-swim with one hand.

[Call and Response 7]
Y ... Yellow Water Lily! Hold one flat palm, then open a yellow flower.
Paddle to Z ... Zooplankton! Let fingertips drift slowly with the current.

[Outro]
