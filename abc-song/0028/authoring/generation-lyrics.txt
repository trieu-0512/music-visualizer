[Intro]
One thing darts. One thing climbs. One thing barely moves at all.
The jungle writes paths through the green.

[Verse 1]
A — Anteater! Long snout leads, sturdy legs follow | searching along the ground for ants and termites.
B ... B ... Banana! Huge leaves sway, fruit hangs | the plant anchors the scene while animals move around it.
C? Ceiba Tree! Trunk rises, branches spread | one still giant that gives many moving paths a place to cross.
D is Dragonfly. Four wings dart and hover | quick turns stitch bright lines over wet green.

[Verse 2]
E — Elephant! Slow feet carry a huge body | flexible trunk curls, reaches, lifts, and drinks.
F ... F ... Fern! Fronds unfurl rather than run | growth is the motion of this quiet target.
G? Gecko! Toe pads cling | body pauses on bark, then slips to a new hold.
H is Howler Monkey. Branches sway under a climbing body | loud calls travel farther than the animal moves.

[Verse 3]
I — Insect! Six legs crawl, wings may buzz or dart | tiny bodies make many kinds of paths.
J ... J ... Jaguar! Spotted cat steps through cover with powerful quiet motion | we watch only from a safe distance.
K? Kinkajou! Feet grip branches, tail balances | night movement curls through the canopy.
L is Liana. Woody vine climbs by growing along supports | a slow motion written into the forest itself.

[Chorus]
Slither, climb, glide—A-B-C!
Different bodies move through green differently.

[Verse 4]
M — Macaw! Broad wings beat, then glide | a flash of color crossing open air.
N ... N ... Nest! Twigs stay still in a cup | movement comes and goes around this sheltered center.
O? Ocelot! Low spotted cat steps through dense cover | quiet body, quick change of direction.
P is Parrot. Feet grip, beak helps climb | short branch movements before the wings take over.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quetzal! Long feathers trail behind flight | a green-bright bird path between branches.
R ... R ... Rattan! Long climbing stems bend and weave | plant growth makes a slow spiral through the forest.
S? Snake! Muscles send curves down a long scaled body | observe the slither without approaching.
T is Tapir. Sturdy legs step through low vegetation | short flexible snout reaches leaves ahead.

[Verse 6]
U — Umbrella Leaf! Broad surface nods under rain | a plant-motion made by water and wind.
V ... V ... Vine! Climb, trail, curl | stems borrow support instead of building a trunk.
W? Water Vine! Green stem stretches through light | a living line crossing other movement paths.
X is Xenops. Small bird creeps along bark and branches | searching surfaces for insects.

[Chorus]
Slither, climb, glide—A-B-C!
Different bodies move through green differently.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Butterfly! Broad wings flutter from plant to plant | light motion near flowers and leaves.
Z ... Z ... Zebra Longwing! Long dark wings striped pale yellow | smooth gliding closes the jungle choreography.
