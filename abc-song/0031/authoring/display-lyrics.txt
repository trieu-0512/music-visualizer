[Intro]
Step... step... whose track is that?
A desert letter crossed the sand.

[Verse 1]
A — Agave! Spikes in a ring | thick desert leaves spread from the spring
B ... B ... Bighorn Sheep! A bighorn sheep is a mountain and desert sheep whose adults can carry heavy curved horns.
C is for Cactus. Spines in the sun | cactus stores water when rain is done
What does D bring? Desert Tortoise! A desert tortoise carries a hard shell and shelters from extreme heat in burrows.

[Verse 2]
Across the warm sand, E meets Eagle. Feather light | beak and wings come into sight
F — Fox! It has pointed ears, a long bushy tail, and quiet paws for moving through its habitat.
G ... G ... Gila Monster! A Gila monster is a heavy-bodied desert lizard with bead-like scales and patterned skin.
H is for Hawk. It is a keen-eyed bird of prey with strong wings and grasping feet.

[Verse 3]
What does I bring? Ibex! Hooves go slow | sure-foot steps along below
Across the warm sand, J meets Joshua Tree. It is a desert plant with stiff pointed leaves on branching trunks.
K — Kestrel! Feather light | beak and wings come into sight
L ... L ... Lizard! A lizard has a long body, four legs in most species, scales, and a tail.

P — Prickly Pear! It is a cactus with flat pads and colorful fruit.

Across the warm sand, T meets Tortoise. It is a reptile with scales that moves close to the ground or through branches.

W is for Wren. Feather light | beak and wings come into sight
What does X bring? Xerus! A xerus is an African ground squirrel that lives in open dry country.

[Verse 7]

Z — Ziziphus Shrub! It is a hardy thorny plant that can bear small round fruits.
