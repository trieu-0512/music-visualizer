[Intro]
A tiny seed wakes under the soil.
Up comes a leaf—what letter comes too?

[Verse 1]
A — Apple Tree! Roots down low | branches reach and grow
B ... B ... Bean! A bean plant can climb or bush out, making pods that hold the beans inside.
C is for Carrot. Carrot down low | leafy top above where orange roots grow
What does D bring? Dirt! Garden dirt is soil—a mix of mineral particles and organic material where roots can grow.

[Verse 2]
In the garden, E meets Eggplant. An eggplant plant grows broad leaves and glossy purple, white, or striped fruit.
F — Flower! It is the blooming part of a plant, often carrying petals around its reproductive center.
G ... G ... Garden Gate! A garden gate swings open and closed to make an entrance through a fence.
H is for Hoe. It is a long-handled garden tool used by adults to shape or loosen soil; children only identify it safely.

[Chorus]
Grow, grow—A-B-C!
Garden words sprout up with me!

[Verse 3]
What does I bring? Ivy! Climb and wind | ivy leaves trail close behind
In the garden, J meets Jug. It is a container with a handle and a narrow opening for pouring liquid.
K — Kneeling Pad! It is a soft cushion that protects knees during low garden work.
L ... L ... Ladybug! A ladybug is a small creature that can be noticed by its legs, body shape, or movement.

[Verse 4]
M is for Mulch. It covers soil to help hold moisture and reduce weeds.
What does N bring? Nasturtium! Nasturtium is a flowering plant with round leaves and bright blooms.
In the garden, O meets Onion. It forms a layered bulb underground with green leaves above the soil.
P — Pea! A pea plant makes pods with round peas lined up inside.

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Quail! Quail on the trail | ground bird with a little tail
R is for Rose. It opens layers of petals on a woody stem that often carries prickles.
What does S bring? Sunflower! Face the light | tall stem, yellow petals bright
In the garden, T meets Tomato. A tomato plant carries round fruit on branching green stems.

[Verse 6]
U — Umbrella Plant! It has leaf clusters that spread like little umbrellas.
V ... V ... Vegetable Bed! A vegetable bed is a prepared patch of soil where food plants grow together.
W is for Watering Can. It has a handle and spout for pouring water gently onto plants.
What does X bring? Xylophone! A xylophone is our X-word helper: a row of tuned bars makes different notes when struck.

[Chorus]
Grow, grow—A-B-C!
Garden words sprout up with me!

[Verse 7]
In the garden, Y meets Yarrow. Bloom in view | petals open fresh with dew
Z — Zinnia! It is a bright garden flower with layered petals around its center.
