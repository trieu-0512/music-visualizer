[Intro]
Under the soil, a root reaches where we cannot see.
Above it, one green shape begins the garden map.

[Verse 1]
A — Apple Tree! Roots hold below | woody branches lift leaves, blossoms, and fruit into the light.
B ... B ... Bean! Seed wakes low, stem climbs high | pods later line up beans inside.
C is for Carrot. Green above, orange below | the thick root stores food under the soil.
D? Dirt! Crumb, grain, dark mix | garden soil gives roots room to spread and take in water.

[Verse 2]
Up through E — Eggplant. Broad leaves spread | glossy fruit hangs beneath the green.
F is Flower. Bud to bloom | petals open around the flower's center.
G ... G ... Garden Gate! It does not grow; it swings | one clear doorway into the beds.
H? Hoe! Adult hands may loosen or shape soil with it | children learn the tool from a safe distance.

[Chorus]
Down below, up we grow—A-B-C!
Root to leaf, the garden opens for me!

[Verse 3]
I is Ivy. Curl and climb | trailing stems hold leaves as they reach along a support.
J ... J ... Jug! Handle steady, narrow mouth | a pour can carry water where roots need it.
K? Kneeling Pad! Soft under knees | a cushion for low garden work, not part of the plant.
L — Ladybug! Tiny beetle on a leaf | six legs crawl beneath spotted wing covers.

[Verse 4]
M is Mulch. Layer on soil | it helps moisture stay and makes weed growth harder.
N ... N ... Nasturtium! Round leaves spread low | bright flowers lift above them.
O? Onion! Green leaves rise while layer wraps layer | the bulb thickens underground.
P — Pea! Tendril, stem, pod | round peas wait in a row inside.

[Verse 5]
At the bed edge, Q is Quail. Quick feet pass low | a small ground bird pecks and moves on.
R ... R ... Rose! Woody stem, prickles, folded petals | the bloom opens layer by layer.
S? Sunflower! Root down, stem tall | a broad flower head rises toward the light.
T is Tomato. Branching green stems | round fruit gathers weight beneath the leaves.

[Verse 6]
U ... U ... Umbrella Plant! Leaf clusters fan overhead | little green umbrellas above the pot.
V? Vegetable Bed! Soil held ready in one patch | roots, stems, leaves, and food plants share the space.
W — Watering Can! Spout tips low | a gentle shower returns water to the soil below.
By the path, X is Xylophone—our X-word helper | tuned bars rise and fall like a tiny garden scale.

[Chorus]
Down below, up we grow—A-B-C!
Root to leaf, the garden opens for me!

[Verse 7]
Y ... Y ... Yarrow! Flat clusters gather many tiny flowers | light heads above divided leaves.
Z? Zinnia! Layered petals ring the center | one last bloom opens at the top of our garden map.

[Interlude]
Roots stay hidden for one quiet beat—can the garden words rise back up?
