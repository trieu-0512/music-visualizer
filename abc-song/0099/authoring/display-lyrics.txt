[Intro]
Two pastries can look alike and contain different fillings, nuts, dairy, eggs, wheat, or other ingredients.
At the sweet shop, the label matters more than a guess from the glass.

[Verse 1]
A — Apple Pie! Pastry crust with apple filling | recipe may include wheat, butter/dairy, spices or alternatives, so label decides allergens.
B ... B ... Bread! Flour-water baked staple with many formulas | wheat is common, but ingredients vary.
C? Cookie! Broad baked category | shape cannot tell whether nuts, eggs, dairy, gluten or other allergens are present.
D is Donut. Fried or baked dough product, ring or filled | filling/coating changes ingredients.

[Verse 2]
E — Egg Tart! Egg-custard filling is explicit in the name | pastry ingredients still vary.
F ... F ... Fruitcake! Dense cake containing dried/candied fruit, often nuts in some recipes | ingredient card matters.
G? Glaze! Thin finish may be sugar-water, dairy-based, chocolate, or other formula | appearance cannot guarantee ingredients.
H is Hot Cross Bun. Spiced yeasted bun marked with a cross; recipes often include dried fruit | label confirms exact formula.

[Verse 5]
Q — Queen Cake! Traditional small cake name with recipe variations | display card should define local product rather than assume.
R ... R ... Roll! Shape/portion word across breads and pastries | ingredients depend on product.
S? Sprinkles! Tiny decorative pieces | colors and ingredient bases vary, so decoration is not an allergen label.
T is Toast. Bread browned by heat | all ingredient information comes from the original bread.
