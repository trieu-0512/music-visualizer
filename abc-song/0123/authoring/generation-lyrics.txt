[Intro]
A ball rebounds, a paddle pushes water, a bicycle turns pedal work into wheel motion.
Active play also needs control, protection, water, and rest.

[Verse 1]
A — Athlete! | Person who trains or participates in physical sport; skill develops through practice, recovery, coaching, and appropriate challenge.
B ... B ... Ball! | Round or oval sport object depending game; material, size, bounce, and rules vary widely.
C? Cricket Bat! | Flat-bladed bat used by a batter in cricket; ball speed means sport rules and protective equipment matter.
D is Dumbbell. Handheld resistance weight; load and technique must fit age, strength, and coaching rather than copying adult lifting. |

[Verse 2]
E — Exercise Mat! | Padded surface cushions floor exercises; thickness and grip should fit the activity.
F ... F ... Football! | Ball used in football codes; size, shape, and rules differ between association football, American football, rugby codes, and others.
G? Goggles! | Eye protection or swim eyewear; fit and sport determine whether they block water, wind, debris, or impact.
H is Helmet. Protective headgear designed for a specific activity; correct fit and certification/type matter. |

Q — Quoits Ring! | Ring tossed toward a peg or target; soft age-appropriate versions reduce impact risk.
R ... R ... Relay Baton! | Light tube passed between relay runners in an exchange zone; successful handoff matters more than throwing it.
S? Soccer Ball! | Round association-football ball kicked, passed, or headed under game rules; size varies by age/group.
T is Trampoline. Elastic-bed apparatus returns energy to a jumper; one-at-a-time rules, guarding, and trained supervision reduce serious injury risk. |
