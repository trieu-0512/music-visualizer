[Intro]
A blanket can add warmth, a sofa adds seating, a towel dries, clear floors and safe appliances add comfort too.
Cozy is a system, not a pile of soft things.

[Verse 1]
A — Alarm Clock! | Timepiece with alarm function signals a set time; it does not know whether a person has slept enough.
B ... B ... Blanket! | Flexible textile layer provides warmth/comfort; loose bedding should suit age and sleep-safety context.
C? Cupboard! | Enclosed storage furniture with shelves/doors; contents determine whether child locks or high storage are needed.
D is Door. Movable barrier opens/closes a passage; hinges and closing edges are pinch points. |

[Verse 2]
E — Envelope! | Folded paper container carries letters/small flat items; it belongs to storage/mail, not furniture.
F ... F ... Fan! | Device moves air with rotating blades or blower; guards and electrical safety matter, fingers stay away.
G? Glass! | Drinking vessel or transparent material depending context; breakable glassware needs careful adult judgment around children.
H is Hanger. Clothing support hangs garments from a rail/hook; wire/plastic shapes are not toys. |

[Chorus]
Soft, light, clear—A-B-C!
Comfort works when the room stays safe to use.

[Verse 3]
I — Iron! | Heated appliance presses fabric; hot soleplate, steam, and cord make it adult-only.
J ... J ... Jug! | Handled container pours liquid; material and contents determine weight, breakage, or heat hazards.
K? Kettle! | Appliance/vessel heats water; boiling water, steam, and hot surfaces are adult-only.
L is Laundry Basket. Container gathers/carries laundry; it is not a ride-on toy and loads can block visibility. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mirror! | Reflective surface forms an image; securely mounted/nonbreakable designs reduce injury risk.
N ... N ... Napkin! | Cloth/paper wipes hands/mouth during meals; it is not a substitute for washing when cleaning is needed.
O? Ottoman! | Low upholstered footrest/seat; storage ottomans may have lids/hinges needing finger safety.
P is Picture. Image/photo/artwork represents a scene or subject; it is a visual item rather than a functional appliance. |

[Chorus]
Soft, light, clear—A-B-C!
Comfort works when the room stays safe to use.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilt! | Layered stitched bedcover/textile; warmth and sleep suitability depend on age/context.
R ... R ... Refrigerator! | Insulated appliance keeps food cold; doors, shelves, and food storage have safety rules and it is never a hiding place.
S? Sofa! | Upholstered multi-seat furniture for sitting/resting; arms/back are not climbing platforms.
T is Towel. Absorbent textile dries skin or surfaces; wet towels should be hung/spread to dry rather than left on floors. |

[Verse 6]
U — Utensil Drawer! | Kitchen drawer stores eating/prep tools; sharp utensils must be secured away from children.
V ... V ... Vacuum! | Powered appliance removes dust/debris by airflow; cords, moving brush, and electricity require supervised use.
W? Wardrobe! | Tall clothing-storage cabinet; anchoring and door/drawer safety can prevent tip/pinch hazards.
X is Xylophone. Tuned-bar musical instrument; deliberate home-object comparison item, not furniture. |

[Chorus]
Soft, light, clear—A-B-C!
Comfort works when the room stays safe to use.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Cushion! | Cushion with yellow cover; color changes appearance, not its support function.
Z ... Z ... Zen Garden! | Small arranged sand/stone garden used for visual/tactile contemplation; tiny stones may be choking hazards for young children.
