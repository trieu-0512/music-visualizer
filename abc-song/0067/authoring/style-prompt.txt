Crisp diction. Open with penguin/ostrich/woodpecker contrasts at 82 BPM in gentle 6/8 with nylon guitar, pizzicato strings, soft brush and flute. Melody narrow. Arrangement changes by function: airy for flight, fluid for penguin, dry for cavity/ground nests. Round 1 teaches feather and nest diversity. Chorus feather-shelter-care. Round 2 uses soft hand shapes; never mime taking feathers or nests. Duck targets. End with a quiet untouched-nest cadence.
