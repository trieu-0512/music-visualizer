[Intro]
Astronaut near. Jupiter far. Galaxy farther than one Solar System can hold.
We zoom outward without confusing size, distance, and category.

[Verse 1]
A — Astronaut! Trained person works in spacecraft or space environments | human scale starts the zoom.
B ... B ... Booster! Launch stage or motor adds thrust during part of ascent | hardware larger than a person, tiny beside a planet.
C? Comet! Small icy body can grow a coma and tails near the Sun | Solar System object, not a star.
D is Dwarf Planet. Rounded body orbiting the Sun without clearing its orbital neighborhood | one Solar System category.

[Verse 2]
E — Eclipse! Alignment blocks some or all light from one body as seen from another | geometry event, not an object.
F ... F ... Fuel Tank! Stores propellant for a vehicle | mission hardware, not a celestial body.
G? Galaxy! Vast gravitational system of stars, gas, dust, dark matter and more | our Solar System occupies only a tiny place inside one galaxy.
H is Helmet. Crew protective equipment | foreground object before the astronomical zoom widens.

[Verse 3]
I — Io! One of Jupiter's large moons | extremely active volcanically because of tidal heating.
J ... J ... Jupiter! Fifth planet from the Sun and largest planet in our Solar System | giant scale after Io.
K? Kuiper Belt! Region beyond Neptune containing many icy small bodies | farther out than the major planets.
L is Lunar Rover. Wheeled vehicle used by Apollo astronauts on the Moon | surface hardware at moon scale.

[Chorus]
Near, far, farther—A-B-C!
One universe holds scales too large for one parade line.

[Verse 4]
M — Moon! Earth's natural satellite | smaller than Earth and orbiting it.
N ... N ... Neptune! Eighth major planet from the Sun | outer-planet scale before the Kuiper Belt.
O? Observatory! Facility built for astronomical observing | returns our zoom to human-made instruments.
P is Probe. Robotic spacecraft sent to collect data | tool that can travel far without becoming the thing it studies.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quarter Moon! Half the visible lunar disk is illuminated at this phase | 'quarter' describes orbital phase position.
R ... R ... Rover! Mobile surface vehicle explores ground on another world | hardware at local surface scale.
S? Satellite! Object in orbit around another body | can be natural like a moon or artificial like a spacecraft.
T is Telescope. Collects radiation from distant sources | bridge between human scale and cosmic scale.

[Verse 6]
U — Universe! All space-time, matter, energy and cosmic contents we can describe | widest scale in the song.
V ... V ... Venus! Second planet from the Sun | rocky planet smaller than the Universe by an almost unimaginable factor.
W? World! Everyday broad term for a planet or inhabited place | not a strict scale category.
X is Xenon Thruster. Electric thruster can ionize xenon and accelerate ions for efficient propulsion | small hardware enabling long spacecraft journeys.

[Chorus]
Near, far, farther—A-B-C!
One universe holds scales too large for one parade line.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yoke! Control-interface shape used in some vehicles | mapping helper, not a universal spacecraft standard.
Z ... Z ... Zero-gravity Chair! Reclining chair changes body posture | does not make a person weightless or simulate orbital free fall.
