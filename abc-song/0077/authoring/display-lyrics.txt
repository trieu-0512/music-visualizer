[Intro]
Antelope in Africa—then Emu moves the map to Australia, and Red Panda pulls it toward Asia.
This is a world wildlife ride, not one safari road.

[Verse 1]

C? Cheetah! Mostly African with a tiny Iranian remnant population | open habitat supports high-speed hunting.
D is Dhole. Asian wild dog of forests and grasslands | geography shifts the map east.

H is Hippo. Sub-Saharan African semi-aquatic mammal | river/lake habitat interrupts the dry-road cliché.

[Verse 3]

K? Kangaroo! Australian marsupial | hopping movement moves the route firmly to Australia.
L is Lemur. Primate native to Madagascar | island geography is essential to the clue.

W? Wolf! Gray wolves range across parts of North America, Europe and Asia | large territory, not safari-only.
