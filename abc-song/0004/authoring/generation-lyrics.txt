[Intro]
Morning light slides over the water.
A little sail lifts—our letters are moving.

[Verse 1]
On our way, A meets Anchor. Down, down, around | anchor drops toward the ground
Boat — that's our B. A boat floats on water and carries people or things.
What waits at C? Crab! Across the sand | sideways steps along the land
D — Dolphin. A dolphin surfaces to breathe and swims in smooth arcs.

[Verse 2]
E is for Estuary. River to sea | fresh and salt mix naturally
On our way, F meets Flipper. It is a broad limb that helps a sea animal swim.
Grouper — that's our G. Round all around | wide mouth near the ground
What waits at H? Hermit Crab! It carries a borrowed shell over its soft body.

[Verse 3]
I — Island. Land ringed by sand | water all around the land
J is for Jetty. It sticks out into the water from the shore.
On our way, K meets Kayak. Paddle the track | forward and back
Lobster — that's our L. A lobster walks on the seafloor with claws held out front.

[Chorus]
Sail and see from A to Z!
Every letter finds a clue!

[Verse 4]
What waits at M? Manatee! Slow in the sea | paddle flippers moving free
N — Nautilus. A nautilus swims with a coiled shell divided into many chambers.
O is for Octopus. Squeeze with ease | eight arms bend as they please
On our way, P meets Pelican. It scoops fish with a long bill and throat pouch.

[Verse 5]
Queen Angelfish — that's our Q. Bright in the light | yellow-blue colors shine just right
What waits at R? Ray! It glides through water with wide fins that look like wings.
S — Seal. Sweep and spin | flippers pull the seal back in
T is for Tidepool. It holds seawater in a rocky hollow when the tide goes out.

[Verse 6]
On our way, U meets Urchin. Round on the ground | stiff spines point all around
Violet Sea Snail — that's our V. A violet sea snail is a small purple-blue ocean snail that can float near the surface.
What waits at W? Whale! Whale with a tail | rises to breathe along the trail
X — X-ray Fish. An X-ray fish has a partly see-through body that can reveal tiny bones.

[Verse 7]
Y is for Yellowfin Tuna. Zoom through blue | yellow fins flash into view
On our way, Z meets Zooplankton. Tiny drifting zooplankton ride along with moving water.

[Chorus]
Sail and see from A to Z!
Every letter finds a clue!

[Interlude]
The sail turns home; now the clues return from memory.

[Call and Response 1]
Softly, A ... Anchor! Point down slowly, like an anchor dropping.
B? ... Boat! Row two slow strokes.
Show C ... Crab! Take two tiny sideways finger-steps.
D ... Dolphin! Make one smooth diving arc with your hand.

[Call and Response 2]
Softly, E ... Estuary! Bring two hands together like waters meeting.
F? ... Flipper! Sweep one hand like a flipper.
Show G ... Grouper! Make a slow fish-swim with one hand.
H ... Hermit Crab! Curl one hand like a shell, then make tiny sideways steps.

[Call and Response 3]
Softly, I ... Island! Draw a little circle in the air.
J? ... Jetty! Slide one hand straight out over imaginary water.
Show K ... Kayak! Paddle left, paddle right.
L ... Lobster! Make two gentle claw shapes.

[Call and Response 4]
Softly, M ... Manatee! Push both hands slowly through the water.
N? ... Nautilus! Trace a spiral with one finger.
Show O ... Octopus! Wiggle your fingers like octopus arms.
P ... Pelican! Make one gentle scooping motion.

[Chorus]
Sail and see from A to Z!
Every letter finds a clue!

[Call and Response 5]
Softly, Q ... Queen Angelfish! Glide two hands like bright fins.
R? ... Ray! Glide both hands wide like wings.
Show S ... Seal! Sweep both hands gently like flippers.
T ... Tidepool! Make a little bowl with both hands.

[Call and Response 6]
Softly, U ... Urchin! Spread fingers into a round spiky shape.
V? ... Violet Sea Snail! Trace a tiny spiral, then float your hand.
Show W ... Whale! Glide both hands wide, then lift for a breath.
X ... X-ray Fish! Make a gentle fish-swim with one hand.

[Call and Response 7]
Softly, Y ... Yellowfin Tuna! Make one smooth, speedy fish-swim.
Z? ... Zooplankton! Let fingertips drift slowly with the current.

[Chorus]
Sail and see from A to Z!
Every letter finds a clue!

[Outro]
Sail and see—our clues are through.
A to Z came into view!
