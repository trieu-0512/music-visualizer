[Intro]
Morning light slides over the water.
A little sail lifts—our letters are moving.

[Verse 1]
On our way, A meets Anchor. Down through water | heavy metal helps the boat stay still.
Small waves spill around B — Boat | a floating carrier moving over blue.
From blue shallows, C is Crab | sideways feet stitch tracks across sand.
Beyond the strand, D — Dolphin | rises for air, then curves back through the sea.

[Verse 2]
E is for Estuary. Sea meets river | fresh and salty waters join and flow.
In that flow, F meets Flipper | a broad limb sweeps water back to make a swimmer go.
Where swimmers go, G is Grouper | stout reef body, wide mouth waiting in shade.
In reef shade, H — Hermit Crab | soft body carries a borrowed shell.

[Verse 3]
I — Island. Water all around | land rises inside a blue ring.
Along that ring, J is Jetty | reaching from the shore out over water.
Over water, K meets Kayak | narrow boat, double paddle forward and back.
Looking back, L — Lobster | claws ahead, walking on the seafloor.

[Chorus]
Sail and see from A to Z!
Every letter finds a clue!

[Verse 4]
What waits at M? Manatee! Warm-water swimmer | paddle-like flippers moving slow and free.
Moving free, N — Nautilus | coiled shell divided into chambers as it swims.
Where it swims, O is Octopus | eight arms, soft body, squeezing through tight spaces under the sea.
Above the sea, P meets Pelican | long bill and throat pouch scoop fish from the water.

[Verse 5]
Queen Angelfish — that's our Q. Yellow and blue glow | reef colors bright below.
In that glow, R is Ray | wide fins glide through water like wings.
Under gliding wings, S — Seal | flippers sweep, then the seal rests on rock or shore.
At the shore, T is Tidepool | rocky hollow keeps seawater when the tide goes out.

[Verse 6]
When tide goes out, U meets Urchin | round sea animal covered in stiff spines.
Past the spines, V — Violet Sea Snail | tiny purple-blue snail floating near the surface.
At the surface, W is Whale | giant marine mammal rising to breathe.
After that breath, X — X-ray Fish | translucent little body lets us partly see through.

[Verse 7]
Through the water, Y is Yellowfin Tuna | yellow-tinted fins flash fast through blue.
Through the blue, Z meets Zooplankton | tiny drifters ride wherever moving water flows.

[Chorus]
Sail and see from A to Z!
Every letter finds a clue!

[Interlude]
The sail turns home; now the clues return from memory.

[Call and Response 1]
Softly, A ... Anchor! Point down slowly, like an anchor dropping.
B? ... Boat! Row two slow strokes.
Show C ... Crab! Take two tiny sideways finger-steps.
D ... Dolphin! Make one smooth diving arc with your hand.

[Call and Response 2]
Softly, E ... Estuary! Bring two hands together like waters meeting.
F? ... Flipper! Sweep one hand like a flipper.
Show G ... Grouper! Make a slow fish-swim with one hand.
H ... Hermit Crab! Curl one hand like a shell, then make tiny sideways steps.

[Call and Response 3]
Softly, I ... Island! Draw a little circle in the air.
J? ... Jetty! Slide one hand straight out over imaginary water.
Show K ... Kayak! Paddle left, paddle right.
L ... Lobster! Make two gentle claw shapes.

[Call and Response 4]
Softly, M ... Manatee! Push both hands slowly through the water.
N? ... Nautilus! Trace a spiral with one finger.
Show O ... Octopus! Wiggle your fingers like octopus arms.
P ... Pelican! Make one gentle scooping motion.

[Chorus]
Sail and see from A to Z!
Every letter finds a clue!

[Call and Response 5]
Softly, Q ... Queen Angelfish! Glide two hands like bright fins.
R? ... Ray! Glide both hands wide like wings.
Show S ... Seal! Sweep both hands gently like flippers.
T ... Tidepool! Make a little bowl with both hands.

[Call and Response 6]
Softly, U ... Urchin! Spread fingers into a round spiky shape.
V? ... Violet Sea Snail! Trace a tiny spiral, then float your hand.
Show W ... Whale! Glide both hands wide, then lift for a breath.
X ... X-ray Fish! Make a gentle fish-swim with one hand.

[Call and Response 7]
Softly, Y ... Yellowfin Tuna! Make one smooth, speedy fish-swim.
Z? ... Zooplankton! Let fingertips drift slowly with the current.

[Chorus]
Sail and see from A to Z!
Every letter finds a clue!

[Outro]
Sail and see—our clues are through.
A to Z came into view!
