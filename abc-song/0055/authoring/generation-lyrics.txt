Black Hole answers B. A black hole is a region where gravity is so strong that even light cannot escape once it is inside.
past the quiet stars, what belongs to C? Capsule! A space capsule is a compact spacecraft section built to carry crew, cargo, or experiments.
D—short beat—Dish Antenna. A dish antenna focuses radio waves so a spacecraft or ground station can send and receive signals.

[Verse 2]
Hear E; see Earth. Earth, blue and round | ocean, land, and air are found
F is here; Flag is why. It is a marked piece of fabric used as a symbol; astronauts have carried flags on missions.
Galaxy answers G. A galaxy is a huge system of stars, gas, dust, and dark matter held together by gravity.
past the quiet stars, what belongs to H? Helmet! A space helmet protects an astronaut's head and helps provide a safe breathing environment inside the suit system.

[Verse 3]
I—short beat—International Space Station. Station circles round | orbiting lab above the ground
Hear J; see Jet Pack. A spacecraft maneuvering pack can use small thrusters to help an astronaut move in space during specially planned operations.
K is here; Krypton Tank is why. Krypton stored just right | gas can feed an ion-thruster flight
Lunar Rover answers L. A lunar rover is a vehicle designed to travel across the Moon's surface.

[Verse 4]
past the quiet stars, what belongs to M? Moon! Moon in view | sunlight makes its phases new
N—short beat—Nebula. A nebula is a cloud of gas and dust in space, sometimes linked to star birth or the remains of stars.
Hear O; see Observatory. It is a place equipped for observing the sky with telescopes or other instruments.
P is here; Probe is why. A space probe is an uncrewed spacecraft sent to collect information about space or another world.

[Verse 5]
Quasar answers Q. Quasar in view | space clue coming through
past the quiet stars, what belongs to R? Rover! It is a mobile robot or vehicle designed to explore the surface of another world.
S—short beat—Spacesuit. A spacesuit provides pressure, oxygen support, temperature control, and protection for an astronaut outside a spacecraft.
Hear T; see Telescope. A telescope collects light or other signals to help us study distant objects.

[Verse 6]
U is here; Uranus is why. Uranus rolls by | sideways tilt turns under distant sky
Venus answers V. Venus is a rocky planet covered by a thick atmosphere and very hot surface conditions.
past the quiet stars, what belongs to W? White Dwarf! It is the hot, dense remnant core left after a Sun-like star sheds its outer layers.
X—short beat—X-ray Telescope. An X-ray telescope detects X-rays from hot and energetic objects in space.
