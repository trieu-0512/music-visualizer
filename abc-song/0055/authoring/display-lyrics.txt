P is here; Probe is why. A space probe is an uncrewed spacecraft sent to collect information about space or another world.

Hear T; see Telescope. A telescope collects light or other signals to help us study distant objects.

X—short beat—X-ray Telescope. An X-ray telescope detects X-rays from hot and energetic objects in space.
