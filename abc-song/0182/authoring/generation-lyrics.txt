[Intro]
Alarm can mark wake time, evening book can mark a transition, but neither can measure how tired a body feels.
Clocks organize time; people still notice needs.

[Verse 1]
A — Alarm Clock! | Timepiece can signal a planned wake/event time; an alarm does not measure whether someone got enough sleep.
B ... B ... Bed! | Furniture supports sleep/rest; age-appropriate safe sleep setup matters, especially for infants/young children.
C? Comb! | Toothed grooming tool separates/arranges hair; clean personal combs reduce sharing of scalp contaminants.
D is Door. Movable barrier opens/closes a passage; hinge and closing edge are pinch points. |

[Verse 2]
E — Evening Book! | Book used during an evening routine; reading can cue transition without guaranteeing sleep.
F ... F ... Face Towel! | Small towel dries face; personal clean towels reduce cross-contamination and eye irritation.
G? Glass! | Drinking vessel can be breakable; age/material/context determine whether an adult should handle it.
H is Hairbrush. Bristled/pinned grooming tool detangles/styles hair; gentle technique avoids pulling and personal brushes should stay clean. |

[Chorus]
Time, cue, check—A-B-C!
Read the clock and the situation together.

[Verse 3]
I — Ice Water! | Cold drinking water with ice; small hard ice pieces can be choking risks for young children.
J ... J ... Juice! | Drink made from fruit/vegetable juice; amount, added sugar, and dental context matter, and water is still a core hydration drink.
K? Key! | Lock-opening object; small keys can be choking hazards and access keys stay with responsible users.
L is Lamp. Electrical light source; stable placement, hot bulbs, cords, and outlets need safe setup. |

[Verse 4]
M — Milk! | Nutrient-containing drink/food; type and suitability depend on age, allergies/intolerance, diet, and household guidance.
N ... N ... Nightlight! | Low-level light aids orientation at night; outlet/cord placement remains an electrical safety issue.
O? Outfit! | Set of clothes worn together; choice can reflect weather, activity, comfort, culture, or preference.
P is Plate. Flat dish holds food; material and food temperature determine safe handling. |

[Chorus]
Time, cue, check—A-B-C!
Read the clock and the situation together.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quiet Book! | Soft/activity or reading book for calm independent/shared use; quiet is an option, not a punishment.
R ... R ... Robe! | Loose garment worn over sleepwear/clothes; belts/loose fabric need snag awareness.
S? Soap! | Cleaning substance helps water remove oils/dirt; avoid eyes/mouth and use the product intended for the task.
T is Toothbrush. Brush cleans tooth surfaces with toothpaste/water as appropriate; it is personal and should not be shared. |

[Verse 6]
U — Uniform! | Standardized clothing for school/work/team; it identifies context but not a person's ability or worth.
V ... V ... Vitamins! | Micronutrients needed in small amounts; supplements are not candy and should only be given by responsible adults according to need/guidance.
W? Washcloth! | Small absorbent cloth supports washing skin/surfaces; use clean cloth and appropriate soap/water.
X is X-shaped Timer. Project timer with X-like visual form; a timer shows elapsed/remaining time but cannot decide readiness by itself. |

[Chorus]
Time, cue, check—A-B-C!
Read the clock and the situation together.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yawn! | Involuntary behavior associated with sleepiness and changes in alertness; a yawn alone does not prove bedtime or illness.
Z ... Z ... Zip Pajamas! | Pajamas closed with a zipper; slider/fabric can catch, so zip slowly and ensure comfortable fit.
