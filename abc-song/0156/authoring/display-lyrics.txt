[Intro]
An architect plans, builder builds, librarian helps find information, firefighter responds with a trained team.
Helping can be professional, volunteer, technical, creative, or public service.

C? Chef! | Professional cook plans/prepares food and manages kitchen processes; hot/sharp equipment and food safety require trained work.

[Verse 2]

G? Garbage Collector! | Worker collects waste/recycling; moving trucks, compactors, traffic, and sharp waste create safety zones.
