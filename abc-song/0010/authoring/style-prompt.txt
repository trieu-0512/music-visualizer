Bright clear adult lead with friendly walking-story delivery and crisp dry diction. Easy 4/4 campfire-folk preschool pulse at 89 BPM with acoustic guitar, light footstep stomps, shaker, glockenspiel and soft bass; narrow melody with ballad-like cadences. Open with two audible walking steps and a stream-side discovery line. Round 1 uses a flexible ABCB walking-ballad idea: in many four-letter groups the second and fourth lines rhyme or near-rhyme while the first and third remain free narrative, but never force the scheme when a scientific fact or rare word needs cleaner language. Let stream/gleam, side/stride, light/sight, low/show and bright/night provide natural closures; elsewhere accept slant rhyme or no rhyme. Chorus uses the stream/seen rhyme as a compact step-and-name hook. Round 2 strips to soft stomps and bass, leaves one real beat after the letter cue, then confirms the object and one congruent gesture. Mute stomps and backing vocals on target-word attacks; glockenspiel answers afterward. End by slowing the steps and resolving on the final hook without extra spoken praise.
