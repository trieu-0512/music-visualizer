[Intro]
Someone prepares food, someone packs, someone washes, someone rests—and roles can change from day to day.
Families and households do not need one fixed job chart.

[Verse 1]
A — Alarm Clock! | Timepiece can signal a planned wake/event time; an alarm does not measure whether someone got enough sleep.
B ... B ... Bed! | Furniture supports sleep/rest; age-appropriate safe sleep setup matters, especially for infants/young children.
C? Comb! | Toothed grooming tool separates/arranges hair; clean personal combs reduce sharing of scalp contaminants.
D is Dinner. Evening/main meal in many routines; timing, foods, and family structure vary widely. |

[Verse 2]
E — Eggs! | Food with shell, white, and yolk; raw/undercooked egg can carry pathogens, so hygiene and cooking guidance matter.
F ... F ... Fork! | Tined eating utensil; use seated/calmly and choose age-appropriate size.
G? Getting-dressed Chart! | Visual sequence supports remembering clothing steps; it is a prompt, not a rule that every outfit uses every item.
H is Hand Soap. Soap formulated for handwashing; lather/rinse helps remove dirt and microbes when used properly. |

[Chorus]
Share, plan, rest—A-B-C!
Choose tasks by age, ability, time, and care.

[Verse 3]
I — Ice Water! | Cold drinking water with ice; small hard ice pieces can be choking risks for young children.
J ... J ... Jacket! | Outer layer adds warmth/weather protection depending material; choose it for conditions, not simply because it appears on a chart.
K? Kettle! | Appliance/vessel heats water; boiling water, steam, and hot surfaces are adult-only.
L is Lunchbox. Container carries a meal/snack; food temperature and hygiene depend on contents and storage. |

[Verse 4]
M — Milk! | Nutrient-containing drink/food; type and suitability depend on age, allergies/intolerance, diet, and household guidance.
N ... N ... Nightlight! | Low-level light aids orientation at night; outlet/cord placement remains an electrical safety issue.
O? Outfit! | Set of clothes worn together; choice can reflect weather, activity, comfort, culture, or preference.
P is Plate. Flat dish holds food; material and food temperature determine safe handling. |

[Chorus]
Share, plan, rest—A-B-C!
Choose tasks by age, ability, time, and care.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilt! | Layered stitched textile provides warmth; sleep suitability depends on age and context.
R ... R ... Robe! | Loose garment worn over sleepwear/clothes; belts/loose fabric need snag awareness.
S? Slippers! | Soft indoor footwear; traction and fit matter, especially on stairs or smooth floors.
T is Toothbrush. Brush cleans tooth surfaces with toothpaste/water as appropriate; it is personal and should not be shared. |

[Verse 6]
U — Uniform! | Standardized clothing for school/work/team; it identifies context but not a person's ability or worth.
V ... V ... Vitamins! | Micronutrients needed in small amounts; supplements are not candy and should only be given by responsible adults according to need/guidance.
W? Water Bottle! | Personal container carries drinking water; keeping it clean and not sharing mouthpieces supports hygiene.
X is Xylophone. Tuned-bar musical instrument; deliberate routine-set comparison item, not a required morning chore. |

[Chorus]
Share, plan, rest—A-B-C!
Choose tasks by age, ability, time, and care.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yawn! | Involuntary behavior associated with sleepiness and changes in alertness; a yawn alone does not prove bedtime or illness.
Z ... Z ... Zipper! | Interlocking teeth/slider closes clothing/bags; move slowly to avoid catching skin or fabric.
