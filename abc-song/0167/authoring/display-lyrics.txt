[Intro]
Check weather and activity first; socks usually come before shoes, but not every garment belongs in every outfit.
Getting dressed is a set of choices, not one fixed costume.

[Verse 1]
A — Apron! | Protective garment covers front of clothing during cooking/craft/work; it is not weather outerwear.
B ... B ... Boots! | Footwear extending above ankle; waterproofing, insulation, safety toe, or fashion role depends design.
C? Coat! | Outer garment provides warmth/weather protection; material and length vary.
D is Denim Jacket. Jacket made mainly from denim; durable woven cotton construction gives structure but not waterproofing by itself. |

[Verse 2]
E — Espadrilles! | Light shoes traditionally with rope/jute-style soles and fabric uppers; suited to dry casual conditions more than wet rough terrain.
F ... F ... Fleece! | Soft synthetic or wool-like insulating fabric/garment; traps air for warmth but may not block wind/rain alone.
G? Gloves! | Separate-finger hand coverings provide warmth, grip, protection, or hygiene depending type.
H is Hat. Head covering provides shade, warmth, protection, or style depending type. |

[Chorus]
Choose, layer, check—A-B-C!
Wear what fits the day and the body.

[Verse 3]
I — Infinity Scarf! | Looped scarf has no loose ends but can still snag; remove around machinery/climbing hazards.
J ... J ... Jacket! | Hip/waist-length outer garment; insulation/weather resistance varies widely.
K? Knee Socks! | Socks extend to near knee for warmth, uniform, sport, or style; fit should not restrict circulation.
L is Lace-up Shoes. Shoes secured with laces; tied laces prevent trips and maintain fit. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mittens! | Hand covering groups fingers together for warmth; reduced finger dexterity is the tradeoff.
N ... N ... Necktie! | Long fabric accessory knotted at neck; should not be worn where loose neckwear could catch in machinery/play equipment.
O? Overall! | One-piece or bib garment covers torso/legs for work, protection, or style; exact meaning varies.
P is Poncho. Loose outer covering with opening for head; rain ponchos shed water but loose fabric needs care near wind/machinery. |

[Chorus]

[Verse 5]
Q — Quarter Socks! | Short socks rising above ankle but below crew height; naming varies by brand/region.
R ... R ... Running Shoes! | Athletic shoes designed for running with cushioning/traction/support features that vary by model and runner.
S? Socks! | Foot layers absorb moisture, reduce friction, and add warmth; clean dry fit supports comfort.
T is T-shirt. Light knit shirt with short sleeves in common form; works as base/single layer depending weather. |

[Verse 6]
U — Uniform! | Standardized clothing identifies school/work/team; it does not determine skill or personality.
V ... V ... Vest! | Sleeveless garment adds layer, visibility, or protection depending design; ordinary fashion vest is not PPE.
W? Wool Hat! | Hat made partly/fully from wool; wool fibers can insulate even when slightly damp.
X is Xmas Sweater. Holiday-themed sweater label describes decoration/seasonal style, not fabric or warmth standard. |

[Verse 7]
Y — Yoga Pants! | Stretch trousers designed for movement/comfort; not weather-specific by name alone.
Z ... Z ... Zori Sandals! | Traditional Japanese thong sandals with flat sole and straps; materials/forms vary historically and today.
