[Intro]
Cold may call for insulation, rain for water resistance, warm dry weather for lighter layers—but the exact garment construction matters.
Check the weather before matching the clothes.

[Verse 1]
A — Apron! | Protective garment covers front of clothing during cooking/craft/work; it is not weather outerwear.
B ... B ... Belt! | Strap fastens around waist to hold clothing or accessories; fit should be comfortable, not tight.
C? Cap! | Close-fitting/brimmed headwear depending style; baseball-style cap shades face but not full sun exposure.
D is Dress. One-piece garment covering torso and extending below waist; length/material/style vary. |

[Verse 2]
E — Earmuffs! | Pads cover outer ears for warmth or hearing protection depending design; winter earmuffs do not replace certified hearing protectors.
F ... F ... Flip-flops! | Open sandals held by toe/foot straps; easy airflow but little toe/heel protection or traction for rough activity.
G? Gloves! | Separate-finger hand coverings provide warmth, grip, protection, or hygiene depending type.
H is Hat. Head covering provides shade, warmth, protection, or style depending type. |
