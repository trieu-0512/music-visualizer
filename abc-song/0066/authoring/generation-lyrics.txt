Spot B: Budgie! A budgie is a small parrot with a curved bill and two toes facing forward and two backward.
C brings Cardinal; Cardinal at the seed | stout cone bill is built for the feed
A clue for D: Dove. A dove has a small head, rounded body, and a soft cooing call.

Finch—that is our F. It is a small bird often recognized by a compact body and seed-cracking cone-shaped bill.
Spot G: Goldfinch! Goldfinch small and bright | cone bill, yellow-dark wings in sight
H brings Heron; It is a long-legged wader with a long neck and spear-like bill for catching prey.

[Verse 3]
A clue for I: Ibis. Ibis probes down | curved bill searches soft wet ground
through the dawn branches, here comes Junco for J. It is a small ground-feeding sparrow relative with a short cone-shaped bill.
Kiwi—that is our K. Kiwi on the ground | tiny wings, strong legs stepping round
Spot L: Lark! A lark is a ground bird with walking feet and, in many species, a strong aerial song.

A clue for N: Nest. A nest is a structure birds may build or use to hold eggs and shelter chicks; it is not a bird itself.
through the dawn branches, here comes Owl for O. It is a bird with forward-facing eyes and soft flight feathers; many species are active at night.
Parrot—that is our P. It has a strong hooked bill and gripping feet with two toes forward and two backward.

[Verse 5]
Spot Q: Quetzal! Quetzal tail flows | forest colors flash wherever it goes
R brings Robin; It is a ground-hopping songbird with a slim bill used to pick small foods.
A clue for S: Sparrow. A sparrow is a small bird with a compact body and a short cone-shaped bill suited to seeds.
through the dawn branches, here comes Toucan for T. It is a tropical bird known for its very large, lightweight-looking colorful bill.

Spot V: Veery! A veery is a small thrush with warm brown upperparts and faint spotting on the upper chest.
W brings Woodpecker; Woodpecker on a tree | tapping bill and gripping feet are clues to see
A clue for X: Xenops. A xenops is a small tropical American bird that searches bark and branches for insects.
