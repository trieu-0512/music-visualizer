[Intro]
Chug-chug, wheel-turn—the gate rolls by.
We won't stand still; each stop has a word.

[Verse 1]
Engine starts; A is Apron. Cloth across the front | helping work clothes stay cleaner while the route begins.
B — Barn! Wide door, high roof | our rolling route begins beside the shelter.
C? Chicken! Scratch-scratch near the wheel track | feet rake soil while the beak pecks small bits.
D ... D ... Duck! Pond-side paddle | broad bill dips as the tractor rolls past water.

[Verse 2]
First row, E — Egg. Smooth oval shell | still in the nest while wheels keep turning.
F is Fence. Post, gap, post | a boundary traces the field beside our path.
G? Grain! Tiny seed becomes a load | wheat, oats, or corn ready for the bin.
H — Hen! Soft cluck by the coop | an adult female chicken that can lay eggs.

[Chorus]
Chug, turn, stop—then on we roll!
A to Z around the farm we go!

[Verse 3]
Next stop, I — Ice Chest. Cool box, lid shut | food and drinks stay chilled during the workday.
J ... J ... Jar! Wide mouth, firm sides | a lidded container carried carefully from shelf to table.
K? Kid Goat! Two quick hoofbeats beside the track | young goat trots, pauses, looks back.
L is Ladder. Rung after rung | adult climbing tool parked safely by the barn.

[Verse 4]
Halfway round, M — Mule. Steady hooves, strong body | a working animal that can carry or pull.
N ... N ... Nest! Twigs curl into a cup | a bird-built place for eggs and chicks.
O? Orchard! The tractor leaves the yard for rows of fruit or nut trees.
P is Pig. Snout down, soil up | rooting beside the pen as wheels roll on.

[Verse 5]
Farmhouse stop, Q — Quilt. Stitch after stitch | layered cloth folded warm and thick.
R ... R ... Rake! Teeth in a row | an adult tool gathers loose material from the ground.
S? Scarecrow! Arms out in the crop row | a still field figure built to discourage birds.
T is Trough. Long open container | feed or water waits where farm animals can reach it.

[Chorus]
Chug, turn, stop—then on we roll!
A to Z around the farm we go!

[Verse 6]
At the animal yard, U — Udder. Milk-producing organ beneath animals such as cows, goats, and sheep.
V ... V ... Vegetable Basket! Harvest gathered together | one carry from field toward the farm table.
W? Wheat! Tall grain heads ripple beside the track | seeds later ground to make flour.
X is X-ray Vet Image. The route pauses at animal care | an inside view of bones helps the vet check what eyes cannot see.

[Verse 7]
Near the porch, Y — Yam. A thick underground tuber stores food below the leaves.
Last row, Z — Zucchini! Long green squash under broad leaves | the tractor trail ends where harvest grows.

[Chorus]
Chug, turn, stop—then on we roll!
A to Z around the farm we go!
