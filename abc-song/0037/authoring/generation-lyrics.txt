[Intro]
Low light lies blue in the snow while one dark wing crosses it.
Polar color is quiet until contrast teaches us where to look.

D is Dovekie. Black-and-white seabird | strong contrast makes a tiny bird readable against sea and sky.

[Verse 2]
E — Ermine! Small weasel may wear white winter fur in cold regions | dark eyes and nose remain clear points.

[Verse 4]
M — Musk Ox! Dark shaggy coat stands heavy against snow | long hair makes a broad silhouette.
N ... N ... Northern Fulmar! Gray-white seabird glides over steel-blue water | soft colors, strong wing shape.
O? Owl! Round head and wings can appear as pale or dark shapes depending on species | silhouette matters more than glitter.
P is Puffin. Black-and-white body with a colorful bill | one small patch of strong color near cold water.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quartz! Hard mineral catches low-angle light with a glassy flash | a stone clue, not living glow.
R ... R ... Ringed Seal! Pale ring patterns mark a gray coat | circles break up the body against ice and sea.
S? Snowy Owl! Pale feathers blend with snow | dark eyes give the face its clearest contrast.
T is Tundra. Low plants, snow, rock, and long sky | a wide background where tiny color differences matter.

[Verse 6]
U — Umiak! Traditional open Arctic boat | dark hull shape can sit clearly against pale shore or water.
V ... V ... Vole! Small ground mammal stays low | little dark movement against snow can reveal it before detail does.
W? Walrus! Brown heavy body, pale tusks | warm earth color against cold blue-gray sea.
X is X-ray Fish. Partly transparent body can reveal internal structures | an unusual light-and-body clue far from Arctic camouflage.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow-billed Loon! Dark-and-pale northern bird | the yellow bill becomes a small strong color at the end of day.
Z ... Z ... Zoarcid Fish! Eel-shaped cold-water fish | soft underwater light follows the long body into blue shadow.
