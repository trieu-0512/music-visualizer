[Intro]
Aquarium water belongs in one zone; litter belongs far from the food bowl; a quiet bed belongs somewhere else.
A good pet home is arranged around the animal, not the alphabet.

[Verse 1]
A — Aquarium! Fish habitat needs adequate size, filtration, temperature and water quality | decoration comes after biology.
B ... B ... Bunny! Domestic rabbit needs large exercise space, hay, hiding and litter options | not a tiny cage corner.
C? Cat! Home needs clean litter area, scratch surfaces, vertical/rest spaces, play and resources distributed to reduce stress.
D is Doghouse. Outdoor shelter can be one weather-protected rest spot | family contact and broader care still happen beyond it.

[Verse 2]
E — Ear Cleaner! Only pet-safe product used when appropriate and directed by a veterinarian | never pour random household liquid into ears.
F ... F ... Feather Toy! Cat toy can mimic prey movement | supervised play prevents swallowing strings or detached feathers.
G? Guinea Pig! Needs roomy enclosure, hay, vitamin-C-appropriate nutrition, hiding places, companionship with compatible guinea pigs, and floor time.
H is Hamster. Deep bedding and hiding zones support burrowing | nocturnal rest needs a quiet daytime location.

[Chorus]
Place, clean, quiet—A-B-C!
Right care item, right species, right part of home.

[Verse 3]
I — ID Tag! Identification information helps return lost pets | tag complements, not replaces, secure management and microchip where appropriate.
J ... J ... Jumping Toy! Enrichment item must fit species and avoid unsafe heights, entrapment, or loose pieces.
K? Kitten! Growing cat needs kitten-appropriate nutrition, litter setup, socialization, play, sleep, vaccination and veterinary care.
L is Litter Box. Toilet area for cats should be accessible, regularly scooped, and generally kept away from food and water.

[Verse 4]
M — Mouse! Pet mice need secure ventilated enclosure, bedding, hides, nesting material, food and enrichment | social needs depend on sex and individual management.
N ... N ... Nail Clipper! Grooming tool belongs in adult-guided care | knowing nail anatomy matters more than speed.
O? Outdoor Kennel! Secure outdoor area can provide temporary exercise or shelter | not a substitute for social contact, climate safety, and indoor care.
P is Perch. Bird perches should vary in diameter and texture and allow clean foot placement | cage layout must preserve flight/movement space.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilt Bed! Washable soft rest zone can comfort some pets | choose material that cannot be dangerously shredded or swallowed.
R ... R ... Rope Toy! Tug or climbing toy must match species, supervision, fiber condition, and chewing behavior.
S? Scratching Post! Place where cat naturally wakes, travels, or marks | sturdy positioning turns furniture conflict into a better option.
T is Turtle. Habitat can require water zone, basking zone, heat, filtration and UVB depending species | setup is a system, not a bowl.

[Chorus]
Place, clean, quiet—A-B-C!
Right care item, right species, right part of home.

[Verse 6]
U — Undercoat Brush! Coat-specific grooming tool removes loose undercoat | overbrushing or wrong tools can irritate skin.
V ... V ... Vet Kit! At-home kit should support basic safe transport/records and veterinarian-recommended supplies | it does not replace diagnosis or treatment.
W? Water Bowl! Fresh water station stays clean and accessible | placement should reduce contamination.
X is X-ray Vet Image. Diagnostic image made by veterinary equipment | interpretation belongs to trained veterinary professionals.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Canary! Bird home needs horizontal flight room, safe perches, balanced nutrition and predictable rest | cage is habitat, not decoration.
Z ... Z ... Zip Carrier! Ventilated secure transport zone | familiar bedding or calm training can reduce travel stress.

[Interlude]
Playtime pauses for fresh water and one calm breath. Now remember the pet words.
