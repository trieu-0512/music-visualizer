[Intro]
First find the problem, then measure and check; only then can a trained adult choose the repair.
Repair begins with diagnosis, not with the loudest tool.

[Verse 1]
A — Axe! | Sharp edged chopping tool for wood; professional/adult-only and never a preschool action target.
B ... B ... Brick! | Masonry unit laid in courses with mortar or other systems; load-bearing use follows structural design.
C? Cement Mixer! | Rotating drum combines cement, aggregate, and water into concrete mix; moving machinery stays in restricted work zone.
D is Drill. Rotating tool makes holes or drives bits; powered drill is adult/professional equipment here. |

[Verse 2]
E — Excavator! | Boom, stick, and bucket dig and move material; rotating upper body creates a large exclusion zone.
F ... F ... Forklift! | Industrial truck raises loads on forks; only trained operators use it and it never carries casual riders.
G? Goggles! | Eye protection shields against dust/chips/splashes when rated for the hazard; fit and standard matter.
H is Hammer. Handled striking tool drives/removes fasteners or shapes material; real tool use requires suitable age, eye protection, and supervision. |

[Chorus]
Notice, measure, check—A-B-C!
Fix follows evidence.

[Verse 3]
I — Impact Driver! | Powered rotary impact tool drives screws/fasteners with high torque; adult/professional operation in this preschool set.
J ... J ... Joint! | Connection where two building parts meet; joint design controls alignment, load transfer, sealing, or movement.
K? Keyhole Saw! | Narrow pointed hand saw cuts small openings/curves; sharp teeth make it adult-only here.
L is Level. Tool compares a surface to horizontal/vertical using vial, laser, or electronic sensing. |

[Verse 4]
M — Measuring Tape! | Flexible graduated tape measures length; hook/zero position and straight alignment affect accuracy.
N ... N ... Nail! | Metal fastener driven into material; sharp point and hammer use keep it adult-managed.
O? Oil Can! | Container/applicator delivers lubricant to machinery; contents and maintenance belong to adults.
P is Plywood. Engineered sheet made from bonded wood veneers with alternating grain directions for dimensional strength. |

[Chorus]
Notice, measure, check—A-B-C!
Fix follows evidence.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quick Clamp! | Clamp rapidly holds workpieces together; pinch points require careful adult positioning.
R ... R ... Rivet! | Permanent/semi-permanent fastener deforms to clamp materials together; installation uses specialized tools.
S? Shovel! | Long-handled blade lifts or moves soil/gravel; jobsite use requires space and adult control.
T is Toolbox. Container organizes and transports tools; closed storage keeps sharp tools inaccessible. |

[Verse 6]
U — Utility Cart! | Small wheeled work cart carries materials/tools; load and route must stay controlled.
V ... V ... Vise! | Bench tool clamps work securely between jaws; fingers stay clear of closing jaws.
W? Wheelbarrow! | Single/front-wheel hand cart moves loose material; balanced load and clear route reduce tipping.
X is Xylophone. Tuned-bar musical instrument; deliberate non-construction X comparison target. |

[Chorus]
Notice, measure, check—A-B-C!
Fix follows evidence.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Vest! | High-visibility garment improves wearer visibility; it does not replace hard hat, barriers, or other PPE.
Z ... Z ... Zinc Screw! | Steel screw may be zinc-coated for corrosion protection; coating does not change the need for correct size/driver.
