[Intro]
Row... row... ready on the rail?
Call the letters, tell the tale!

[Verse 1]
A ... A ... Angelfish! Fins flare bright | reef-blue swimmer catching light.
B — Boat! Hull floats steady, carrying people and gear.
From the deck, C finds Crab—sideways feet stitch little tracks across wet sand.
D is for Dolphin—surface, breathe, then arc and dive back out of sight.

[Verse 2]
Crew calls E? Eel! Long body bends | slipping through reef cracks as currents go.
F ... F ... Fish! Fins steer while gills draw oxygen from the water.
G — Grouper! Stout reef fish, wide mouth waiting near rocky shade.
From the deck, H finds Hermit Crab—soft body sheltered in a borrowed shell wherever it goes.

[Chorus]
Row and say, A-B-C!
Sea and boat words ride with me!

[Verse 3]
I is for Isopod. Plate after plate | many little legs hurry in a row.
Crew calls J? Jellyfish! Soft bell pulses while trailing tentacles drift behind.
K ... K ... Kayak! A double paddle turns the narrow boat through water.
L — Lagoon! Shallow water rests apart from open sea, calm and low.

[Verse 4]
From the deck, M finds Mussel. Two dark halves | closed shell holds tight on stone.
N is for Nautilus—coil after coil, its chambered shell travels as it swims.
Crew calls O? Oyster! Rough two-part shell closes around the soft animal inside.
P ... P ... Pelican! Long bill dips; throat pouch scoops a fish, then wings carry it home.

[Verse 5]
Q — Queen Angelfish! Yellow and blue glow | bright reef colors turning slow.
From the deck, R finds Reef—a rocky or coral home full of cracks and hiding places.
S is for Seahorse—upright swimmer, curled tail holding tight to a plant.
Crew calls T? Tidepool! A rocky hollow keeps seawater when the tide goes low.

[Chorus]
Row and say, A-B-C!
Sea and boat words ride with me!

[Verse 6]
U ... U ... Urchin! Round on the ground | stiff sea spines point all around.
V — Vessel! A boat or ship built to travel on water.
From the deck, W finds Wave—surface water rising, cresting, falling on its way.
X is for X-ray Fish. Partly see-through body | some tiny bones can be found.

[Verse 7]
Crew calls Y? Yellow Tang! Yellow in view | flat oval reef fish gliding through blue.
Z ... Z ... Zebra Shark! Young sharks wear dark zebra-like bands; a long tail trails behind.

[Chorus]
Row and say, A-B-C!
Sea and boat words ride with me!

[Interlude]
Oars rest for one breath; now the crew calls back.

[Call and Response 1]
Crew calls A ... Angelfish! Glide two hands like fins.
B ... B ... Boat! Row two slow strokes.
Ready, C? ... Crab! Take two tiny sideways finger-steps.
D ... Dolphin! Make one smooth diving arc with your hand.

[Call and Response 2]
Crew calls E ... Eel! Wiggle one hand in a long curve.
F ... F ... Fish! Swish one hand like a fish tail.
Ready, G? ... Grouper! Make a slow fish-swim with one hand.
H ... Hermit Crab! Curl one hand like a shell, then make tiny sideways steps.

[Call and Response 3]
Crew calls I ... Isopod! Walk fingertips in a tiny row.
J ... J ... Jellyfish! Pulse your fingers softly like a drifting bell.
Ready, K? ... Kayak! Paddle left, paddle right.
L ... Lagoon! Make a quiet bowl shape with both hands.

[Call and Response 4]
Crew calls M ... Mussel! Close two palms like a shell.
N ... N ... Nautilus! Trace a spiral with one finger.
Ready, O? ... Oyster! Close two hands like a shell.
P ... Pelican! Make one gentle scooping motion.

[Chorus]
Row and say, A-B-C!
Sea and boat words ride with me!

[Call and Response 5]
Crew calls Q ... Queen Angelfish! Glide two hands like bright fins.
R ... R ... Reef! Make a little hiding arch with your hands.
Ready, S? ... Seahorse! Curl one finger like a seahorse tail.
T ... Tidepool! Make a little bowl with both hands.

[Call and Response 6]
Crew calls U ... Urchin! Spread fingers into a round spiky shape.
V ... V ... Vessel! Rock both hands gently like a boat.
Ready, W? ... Wave! Roll one hand up and down.
X ... X-ray Fish! Make a gentle fish-swim with one hand.

[Call and Response 7]
Crew calls Y ... Yellow Tang! Make a flat oval with your hands, then glide.
Z ... Z ... Zebra Shark! Glide one hand slowly like a long shark tail.

[Chorus]
Row and say, A-B-C!
Sea and boat words ride with me!

[Outro]
Oars come home—A-B-C!
Sea-word crew, we named the sea!
