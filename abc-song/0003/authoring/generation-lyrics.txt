[Intro]
Row... row... ready on the rail?
Call the letters, tell the tale!

[Verse 1]
A ... A ... Angelfish! Glide with the tide | tall fins open wide
B — Boat! A boat floats on water and carries people or things.
From the deck, C finds Crab. Across the sand | sideways steps along the land
D is for Dolphin. A dolphin surfaces to breathe and swims in smooth arcs.

[Verse 2]
Crew calls E? Eel! Slide with the tide | long body side to side
F ... F ... Fish! It swims with fins and breathes through gills.
G — Grouper! Round all around | wide mouth near the ground
From the deck, H finds Hermit Crab. It carries a borrowed shell over its soft body.

[Chorus]
Row and say, A-B-C!
Sea and boat words ride with me!

[Verse 3]
I is for Isopod. Small and low | many little legs in a row
Crew calls J? Jellyfish! It drifts with a soft bell-shaped body and trailing tentacles.
K ... K ... Kayak! Paddle the track | forward and back
L — Lagoon! A lagoon is shallow water separated from the open sea.

[Verse 4]
From the deck, M finds Mussel. Shell, can you tell? | two dark halves hold well
N is for Nautilus. A nautilus swims with a coiled shell divided into many chambers.
Crew calls O? Oyster! Shell, can you tell? | rough two halves hold well
P ... P ... Pelican! It scoops fish with a long bill and throat pouch.

[Verse 5]
Q — Queen Angelfish! Bright in the light | yellow-blue colors shine just right
From the deck, R finds Reef. It is a rocky or coral habitat with many hiding places.
S is for Seahorse. Curl and twirl | tail grips plants in a whirl
Crew calls T? Tidepool! It holds seawater in a rocky hollow when the tide goes out.

[Chorus]
Row and say, A-B-C!
Sea and boat words ride with me!

[Verse 6]
U ... U ... Urchin! Round on the ground | stiff spines point all around
V — Vessel! A vessel is a boat or ship made to travel on water.
From the deck, W finds Wave. Rise, surprise | crest and fall before our eyes
X is for X-ray Fish. An X-ray fish has a partly see-through body that can reveal tiny bones.

[Verse 7]
Crew calls Y? Yellow Tang! Yellow in view | flat oval body glides through blue
Z ... Z ... Zebra Shark! It is a long-tailed shark whose young have zebra-like dark bands.

[Chorus]
Row and say, A-B-C!
Sea and boat words ride with me!

[Interlude]
Oars rest for one breath; now the crew calls back.

[Call and Response 1]
Crew calls A ... Angelfish! Glide two hands like fins.
B ... B ... Boat! Row two slow strokes.
Ready, C? ... Crab! Take two tiny sideways finger-steps.
D ... Dolphin! Make one smooth diving arc with your hand.

[Call and Response 2]
Crew calls E ... Eel! Wiggle one hand in a long curve.
F ... F ... Fish! Swish one hand like a fish tail.
Ready, G? ... Grouper! Make a slow fish-swim with one hand.
H ... Hermit Crab! Curl one hand like a shell, then make tiny sideways steps.

[Call and Response 3]
Crew calls I ... Isopod! Walk fingertips in a tiny row.
J ... J ... Jellyfish! Pulse your fingers softly like a drifting bell.
Ready, K? ... Kayak! Paddle left, paddle right.
L ... Lagoon! Make a quiet bowl shape with both hands.

[Call and Response 4]
Crew calls M ... Mussel! Close two palms like a shell.
N ... N ... Nautilus! Trace a spiral with one finger.
Ready, O? ... Oyster! Close two hands like a shell.
P ... Pelican! Make one gentle scooping motion.

[Chorus]
Row and say, A-B-C!
Sea and boat words ride with me!

[Call and Response 5]
Crew calls Q ... Queen Angelfish! Glide two hands like bright fins.
R ... R ... Reef! Make a little hiding arch with your hands.
Ready, S? ... Seahorse! Curl one finger like a seahorse tail.
T ... Tidepool! Make a little bowl with both hands.

[Call and Response 6]
Crew calls U ... Urchin! Spread fingers into a round spiky shape.
V ... V ... Vessel! Rock both hands gently like a boat.
Ready, W? ... Wave! Roll one hand up and down.
X ... X-ray Fish! Make a gentle fish-swim with one hand.

[Call and Response 7]
Crew calls Y ... Yellow Tang! Make a flat oval with your hands, then glide.
Z ... Z ... Zebra Shark! Glide one hand slowly like a long shark tail.

[Chorus]
Row and say, A-B-C!
Sea and boat words ride with me!

[Outro]
Oars come home—A-B-C!
Sea-word crew, we named the sea!
