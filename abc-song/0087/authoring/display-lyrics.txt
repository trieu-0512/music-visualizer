[Intro]
A cup measures volume; a timer measures time; a rolling pin changes shape.
Cooking words become clearer when we ask what changes and what gets measured.

[Verse 1]
A — Apron! Clothing barrier helps keep splashes off clothes | protection, not a measurement tool.
B ... B ... Bowl! Holds ingredients while they are combined | container volume depends on the bowl.
C? Cup! Can be a drinking vessel or standardized measuring cup | context decides the meaning.
D is Donut. Dough changes through combining, shaping and cooking | finished food, not one measurement.

[Verse 2]
E — Egg Timer! Measures an interval of time | it does not measure egg temperature or doneness directly.
F ... F ... Frying Pan! Transfers stovetop heat to food | hot adult-only stage.
G? Grater! Sharp-edged holes shred food into smaller pieces | adult-only picture tool here.
H is Honey. Viscous sweet ingredient flows slowly | volume/mass can be measured rather than guessed.

[Verse 3]
I — Ice Cream! Frozen emulsion/foam whose texture depends on freezing and churning | finished food illustrates phase/texture change.
J ... J ... Jar! Container can store ingredients | capacity is not automatically one standard measure.
K? Kettle! Heats water toward boiling | hot water and steam require adult handling.
L is Lemon. Cutting and squeezing can release acidic juice | child only observes sharp-tool steps.

[Chorus]
Measure, wait, change—A-B-C!
Different tools measure different things.

[Verse 4]
M — Mixer! Mechanical blending combines ingredients and can incorporate air | powered appliance stays adult-operated.
N ... N ... Napkin! Cleanup item | no measurement role.
O? Orange! Citrus fruit with segments | juice volume varies from fruit to fruit.
P is Pancake. Batter changes from fluid to set structure with heat | pan/heat adult-managed.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quart Cup! In US customary measure, one quart equals four cups | label the system rather than pretending all countries use it.
R ... R ... Rolling Pin! Mechanical pressure flattens dough | shape change without cutting.
S? Spoon! May stir or serve; a measuring spoon is standardized, an ordinary spoon is not necessarily.
T is Toast. Bread browns through dry heat and chemical reactions | toaster heat remains adult-managed.

[Verse 6]
U — Utensil! General word for a kitchen tool | function must be named more specifically before use.
V ... V ... Vanilla! Flavor comes from cured vanilla-orchid pods or extracts | tiny quantities can strongly affect aroma.
W? Waffle! Batter sets in a heated patterned iron | hot appliance creates the grid.
X is X-shaped Cookie Cutter. Presses an X outline through rolled dough | shape tool, not a measuring device.

[Chorus]
Measure, wait, change—A-B-C!
Different tools measure different things.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yam! Tuber can soften as starch-rich tissue cooks | heat changes texture.
Z ... Z ... Zester! Removes thin colored outer citrus peel rich in aromatic oils | sharp adult-only tool.
