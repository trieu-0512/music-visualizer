[Intro]
Some jobs can be shared; sharp blades, hot pans and powered tools stay with the adult.
Cooking together means matching the task to the helper.

[Verse 1]
A — Apron! Clothing protection anyone can wear | clean hands come first.
B ... B ... Bowl! Cool bowl can support age-appropriate stirring/pouring | check material/weight.
C? Cutting Board! Stable prep surface | sharp cutting on it remains adult work here.
D is Dish. Serving/cleanup item | children may carry only safe, cool, unbreakable dishes as appropriate.

[Verse 2]
E — Egg Timer! Timekeeping can be a child-friendly job | timer reminder never replaces adult doneness/safety judgment.
F ... F ... Frying Pan! Hot stove tool | adult-only.
G? Grapes! Fruit can be washed with help | adult prepares safely for young children's choking risk.
H is Hot Pot. Very hot shared cooking setup | adult-managed and physically separated from children.

[Verse 3]
I — Ice Tray! Cool-water filling can be a supervised child task | freezer access still follows household safety.
J ... J ... Juicer! Manual safe designs may allow help; powered/sharp juicers stay adult-operated.
K? Knife! Sharp blade | adult-only, no pretend passing of real knives.
L is Ladle. Cool empty ladle can be identified; hot soup serving stays adult-managed.

[Chorus]
Ask, share, pass—A-B-C!
Right task, right hands, one kitchen team.

[Verse 4]
M — Mixer! Powered moving beaters | adult-operated unless age/tool instructions explicitly support supervised use.
N ... N ... Noodles! Ingredient/food | children can count or portion cool noodles while adults manage boiling water.
O? Oven! High heat | adult-only zone.
P is Pan. Cool pan can be named; heated pan adult-only.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quiche! Finished dish | adults manage oven; child can help count or name cool ingredients.
R ... R ... Rice! Dry grains can be measured with supervision; hot pot/rice cooker remains adult-managed.
S? Spatula! Cool spatula may be safe for stirring; hot-pan turning stays adult task.
T is Tongs. Cool food-serving tongs may suit supervised child use | hot grill/pan tongs do not.

[Verse 6]
U — Utensil! General category | safety depends on exact tool, temperature and task.
V ... V ... Vegetable Peeler! Sharp blade | adult-only in this preschool workflow.
W? Waffle! Finished food can be plated after cooling | waffle iron adult-only.
X is X-shaped Cookie Cutter. Pressing cool dough may be age-appropriate with a safe cutter | adult checks edges/material.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yam! Ingredient can be washed; cutting/hot cooking stays adult work.
Z ... Z ... Zester! Sharp edge | adult-only.

[Chorus]
Ask, share, pass—A-B-C!
Right task, right hands, one kitchen team.
