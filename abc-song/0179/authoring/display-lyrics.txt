[Intro]
Anger or jealousy can show a problem; a pause and a voice can help explain what happened.
Repair is more than a forced sorry or smile.

[Verse 1]
A — Angry Face! | Anger cue; one face cannot show the whole reason or intensity.
B ... B ... Brave Badge! | Courage symbol; bravery can include asking help, speaking up, or declining unsafe risk.
C? Caring Card! | Prompt to check needs or offer help; ask before touching or solving.
D is Delight Star. Delight symbol; the feeling is possible, never required. |

[Verse 2]
E — Emotion Cards! | Cards help name/discuss feelings; they are vocabulary tools, not emotion tests.
F ... F ... Feelings Wheel! | Chart groups emotion words for reflection; categories are not fixed scientific boxes.
G? Gentle Hands! | Cue for safe hands; gentleness includes respecting space and permission.
H is Heart. Care/love symbol here, not the biological heart. |

[Chorus]
Pause, say, repair—A-B-C!
Care listens before it fixes.

[Verse 3]
I — Inside Voice! | Cue for quieter indoor volume; feelings and needs can still be said.
J ... J ... Jealous Face! | Jealousy cue; having the feeling is different from acting harmfully.
K? Kind Hands! | Cue for safe helpful hands; kindness also includes words, listening, boundaries, or help.
L is Love Letter. Written care message; respect privacy and recipient comfort. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mood Meter! | Self-reflection chart for feeling/energy; not a diagnosis.
N ... N ... Nervous Face! | Nervousness cue; a face alone cannot measure experience.
O? Optimism Sun! | Hopeful metaphor; optimism does not promise problems disappear.
P is Proud Star. Pride symbol for effort, growth, identity, or achievement; no comparison is required. |

[Chorus]
Pause, say, repair—A-B-C!
Care listens before it fixes.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quiet Corner! | Optional low-stimulation pause space; not punishment or forced isolation.
R ... R ... Relaxation Pillow! | Comfort object for rest/pause; it cannot guarantee relaxation or block breathing.
S? Smile! | Smile can express many social/emotional states; it does not prove happiness or need to be demanded.
T is Tired Face. Tiredness cue; many causes are possible and rest/support may help. |

[Verse 6]
U — Upset Face! | Upset cue; ask what is happening instead of guessing from a face.
V ... V ... Voice Card! | Prompt to communicate needs, feelings, questions, or boundaries.
W? Worry Box! | Optional worry-note activity; it does not erase worry or replace needed support.
X is Xylophone Calm Toy. Soft music toy may settle some people; others may prefer quiet. |

[Chorus]
Pause, say, repair—A-B-C!
Care listens before it fixes.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yoga Card! | Optional comfortable movement/breathing prompt; range stays pain-free.
Z ... Z ... Zigzag Breath Card! | Visual breathing pace; return to normal breathing if uncomfortable or dizzy.
