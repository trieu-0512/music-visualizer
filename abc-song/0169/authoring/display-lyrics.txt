[Intro]
Wet raincoat dries before storage, boots pair together, soft knitwear may fold, dresses can hang.
Wardrobe care begins after wearing.

[Verse 1]
A — Apron! | Protective garment covers front of clothing during cooking/craft/work; it is not weather outerwear.
B ... B ... Boots! | Footwear extending above ankle; waterproofing, insulation, safety toe, or fashion role depends design.
C? Cap! | Close-fitting/brimmed headwear depending style; baseball-style cap shades face but not full sun exposure.
D is Dress. One-piece garment covering torso and extending below waist; length/material/style vary. |

[Verse 2]
E — Espadrilles! | Light shoes traditionally with rope/jute-style soles and fabric uppers; suited to dry casual conditions more than wet rough terrain.
F ... F ... Fleece! | Soft synthetic or wool-like insulating fabric/garment; traps air for warmth but may not block wind/rain alone.
G? Gown! | Long garment; can mean formal dress, nightwear, medical gown, or robe depending context.
H is Hoodie. Sweatshirt/jacket with attached hood; drawstrings can be snag hazards in some contexts. |

[Chorus]
Clean, dry, store—A-B-C!
Put clothes away in the condition they need.

[Verse 3]
I — Indoor Shoes! | Footwear reserved for indoor surfaces; keeps outdoor dirt separate and often emphasizes light comfort.
J ... J ... Jeans! | Denim trousers with sturdy woven fabric and seams; fit should allow normal movement.
K? Knit Cap! | Soft knitted close-fitting hat retains warmth around head; fiber and thickness affect insulation.
L is Leggings. Stretch close-fitting trousers; flexible for movement but warmth/weather protection depends fabric. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mittens! | Hand covering groups fingers together for warmth; reduced finger dexterity is the tradeoff.
N ... N ... Nightgown! | Loose sleepwear garment; fit/fabric should suit sleep and fire-safety guidance.
O? Overcoat! | Long outer coat worn over other clothes for cold-weather insulation.
P is Pajamas. Sleepwear designed for comfort; fabric, fit, and fire-safety standards vary by market/age. |

[Chorus]
Clean, dry, store—A-B-C!
Put clothes away in the condition they need.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilted Jacket! | Jacket stitches insulation between layers; quilting holds filling in place and creates warmth.
R ... R ... Raincoat! | Outer layer designed to resist rain; waterproof/breathable performance depends material and seams.
S? Scarf! | Long neck fabric adds warmth/style; loose ends require care around machinery/play equipment.
T is Trainers. UK/common term for athletic sneakers; purpose ranges casual wear to sport-specific models. |

[Verse 6]
U — Uniform! | Standardized clothing identifies school/work/team; it does not determine skill or personality.
V ... V ... Velcro Shoes! | Shoes using hook-and-loop straps for closure; fit still matters even when fastening is easy.
W? Wool Hat! | Hat made partly/fully from wool; wool fibers can insulate even when slightly damp.
X is Xmas Sweater. Holiday-themed sweater label describes decoration/seasonal style, not fabric or warmth standard. |

[Chorus]
Clean, dry, store—A-B-C!
Put clothes away in the condition they need.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yoga Pants! | Stretch trousers designed for movement/comfort; not weather-specific by name alone.
Z ... Z ... Zori Sandals! | Traditional Japanese thong sandals with flat sole and straps; materials/forms vary historically and today.
