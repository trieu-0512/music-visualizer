[Intro]
Choose what the task needs, use only the safe materials, then clean and return them.
Classroom action includes stopping and resetting.

[Verse 1]
A — Alphabet Chart! | Chart displays letter forms and sequence; it can support recognition without replacing sound, writing, or word practice.
B ... B ... Backpack! | School bag; two fitted shoulder straps help spread a sensible load.
C? Calculator! | Electronic or mechanical tool performs entered calculations; correct keys still depend on choosing the right mathematical operation.
D is Desk. Work surface supports books, writing, and materials; clear space helps separate active work from stored supplies. |

[Verse 2]
E — Eraser! | Rubber-like material lifts many graphite marks from paper; it does not erase every kind of ink or damage-free mark.
F ... F ... Folder! | Folded cover keeps loose papers together and separates subjects or projects.
G? Glue! | Adhesive joins materials after it spreads and sets; classroom glue should be non-toxic and used only as directed, never tasted.
H is Highlighter. Translucent marker emphasizes selected text while leaving words visible; highlighting everything defeats its purpose. |

[Chorus]
Choose, do, clean—A-B-C!
The activity is not finished until the tools are safe and stored.

[Verse 3]
I — Ink! | Colored liquid or paste carries pigment or dye to a surface and can stain, so lids and careful handling matter.
J ... J ... Jigsaw Map! | Map puzzle breaks a geographic picture into interlocking pieces; matching shape and location rebuilds the whole.
K? Knowledge Card! | Project card holds a fact, clue, or concept; it is not one standardized product.
L is Library Book. Shared book is borrowed for a period and returned so another reader can use the same copy. |

[Verse 4]
M — Marker! | Fiber- or felt-tip writing tool feeds ink to a broad or fine point; cap care helps keep many markers from drying out.
N ... N ... Notebook! | Bound pages keep related notes or drawings in order and make earlier work easy to revisit.
O? Origami Paper! | Light square folding paper; classic origami builds forms mainly with creases.
P is Paintbrush. Bristles or synthetic fibers carry paint and release it with strokes; brush shape changes the kind of mark. |

[Chorus]
Choose, do, clean—A-B-C!
The activity is not finished until the tools are safe and stored.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quiz Card! | Prompt checks recall or understanding; a miss shows what to practice next.
R ... R ... Reading Book! | Book selected for reading practice or enjoyment; text difficulty and purpose matter more than the cover.
S? Stapler! | Tool bends metal staples through stacked sheets; staple points and the closing mechanism require adult or teacher management for preschoolers.
T is Tape. Flexible strip carries adhesive on one or both sides to join or hold materials temporarily or permanently. |

[Verse 6]
U — USB Drive! | Flash-memory device stores files; supervised computer use prevents loss or mishandling.
V ... V ... Vocabulary Card! | Card links a word to meaning, example, picture, or pronunciation.
W? Workbook! | Practice book leaves spaces for learners to write, draw, solve, or respond directly on the page.
X is Xylophone. Tuned-bar percussion instrument; shorter matching bars generally sound higher. |

[Chorus]
Choose, do, clean—A-B-C!
The activity is not finished until the tools are safe and stored.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Pencil! | A pencil with a yellow-painted body still writes through its graphite core; body color does not change the writing mechanism.
Z ... Z ... Zero Card! | Card shows 0: no counted items in some contexts and a place-holding digit in others.
