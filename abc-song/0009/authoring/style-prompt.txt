Calm close adult lead with crisp diction, natural breath and minimal vibrato. Slow 3/4 preschool nature sway at 82 BPM with kalimba, soft shaker, airy pad used sparingly and gentle bass; narrow mostly stepwise melody. Open with a tiny wetland sound-image—drip, hush, distant wing motion—then enter the first clue. Round 1 should feel closer to free verse than to a nursery rhyme: use silence, sensory images, assonance, consonance, and only occasional internal rhyme or delayed closure. A line may answer a sound two or three lines later; many lines should not rhyme at all. Do not add filler merely to close a rhyme. Rare names such as Vallisneria and X-ray Tetra are intentionally plain and speech-clear. Chorus stays calm and memorable instead of becoming hyperactive. Round 2 becomes nearly percussion-free with about two beats of recall space before each answer. Keep all target words dry and forward, with no harmony on their attack. End by thinning to solo kalimba and one soft final refrain line.
