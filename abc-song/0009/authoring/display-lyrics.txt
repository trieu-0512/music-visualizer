[Intro]
Drip... hush... wings over the reeds.
Morning wakes one letter at a time.

[Verse 1]
Alligator — our A. Glide and hide | by the riverside
At wetland dawn, B meets Beaver. A beaver paddles with a flat tail and builds with branches.
C — Canoe. Canoe in view | paddle through blue
What belongs to D? Duck! A duck paddles on top of the water and dips its beak below.

[Verse 2]
E ... E ... Egret. Stand in the sand | long legs step across the land
Fishing Rod — our F. It has a long pole, line, and reel for fishing.
At wetland dawn, G meets Green Algae. Green scene | soft patches grow between
H — Hydrilla. It is a submerged water plant with leafy stems.

[Verse 3]
What belongs to I? Iris! Wet-ground sight | iris flower opens bright
J ... J ... June Bug. It is a shiny beetle that can fly on warm evenings.
Kingfisher — our K. Flash and splash | dive for fish, then back in a dash
At wetland dawn, L meets Lily Pad. A lily pad floats flat on the water like a green plate.

[Chorus]
Wetland wonders—A-B-C!
Reeds and wings and water sing!

[Verse 4]
M — Minnow. Go with the flow | tiny fish darts to and fro
What belongs to N? Newt! A newt is a small amphibian with four legs and a long tail.
O ... O ... Otter. Side to side | float, turn, dive and glide
Pond — our P. It is a small body of still water where plants and animals live.

[Verse 5]
At wetland dawn, Q meets Quail. Quail on the trail | ground bird with a little tail
R — Reed. It grows tall and straight at the water's edge.
What belongs to S? Swan! Glide beside | long curved neck, wings spread wide
T ... T ... Tadpole. It starts life in water with a tail and later grows legs.

[Verse 6]
Umbrella Sedge — our U. Green rays spread | umbrella shape overhead
At wetland dawn, V meets Vallisneria. Vallisneria is a ribbon-leaved freshwater plant that grows underwater.
W — Waterfall. Water falls tall | rushing sheet by rocky wall
What belongs to X? Xiphophorus! Xiphophorus is a group of small freshwater fish that includes swordtails and platies.

[Verse 7]
Y ... Y ... Yellow Water Lily. Yellow in the light | broad leaves float, flower bright
Zooplankton — our Z. Tiny drifting zooplankton ride along with moving water.

[Chorus]
Wetland wonders—A-B-C!
Reeds and wings and water sing!
