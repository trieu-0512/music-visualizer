[Intro]
Drip... hush... wings over the reeds.
Morning wakes one letter at a time.

[Verse 1]
Alligator — our A. Still by the bank, then one quiet glide through brown-green water.
At wetland dawn, B meets Beaver. Flat tail cuts a small V through the reflected sky.
C — Canoe. Paddle dips softly; rings widen behind the blade.
What belongs to D? Duck! Dip, then drip | broad bill lowers while webbed feet paddle.

[Verse 2]
E ... E ... Egret. Long legs hold still in the shallows while the water moves around them.
Fishing Rod — our F. Long pole, line, and reel make a quiet silhouette beside the water.
At wetland dawn, G meets Green Algae. Green sheen | soft patches tint water and wet surfaces.
H — Hydrilla. Leafy stems stay below the surface and move with the current.

[Verse 3]
What belongs to I? Iris! Morning light | bright flower opens near wet ground.
J ... J ... June Bug. A shiny beetle lifts into the warm evening air.
Kingfisher — our K. Flash and splash | quick dive for a fish, then stillness on a perch.
At wetland dawn, L meets Lily Pad. Flat green leaf rests on the water like a plate.

[Chorus]
Wetland wonders—A-B-C!
Reeds and wings and water sing!

[Verse 4]
M — Minnow. Quick flick | small fish darts through the shallow edge.
What belongs to N? Newt! Four legs, long tail, small amphibian moving between damp land and water.
O ... O ... Otter. Float, turn, dive; the smooth body redraws the surface.
Pond — our P. Still water holds reflections, plants, insects, and animal life.

[Verse 5]
At wetland dawn, Q meets Quail. Peck, step | small ground bird moving through low cover.
R — Reed. Tall stem leans, straightens, then leans again at the water's edge.
What belongs to S? Swan! Long neck curves while the body glides over calm water.
T ... T ... Tadpole. Tail first, legs later—the little swimmer is changing as it grows.

[Verse 6]
Umbrella Sedge — our U. Green rays spread from the top of the stem like a small open umbrella.
At wetland dawn, V meets Vallisneria. Long ribbon-like leaves grow underwater in freshwater.
W — Waterfall. White fall | water drops past dark rock and breaks into spray.
What belongs to X? Xiphophorus! A freshwater fish group that includes swordtails and platies.

[Verse 7]
Y ... Y ... Yellow Water Lily! Yellow in light | broad leaves float, flower bright.
Zooplankton — our Z. Tiny drifters move with the water, almost too small to notice one by one.

[Chorus]
Wetland wonders—A-B-C!
Reeds and wings and water sing!
