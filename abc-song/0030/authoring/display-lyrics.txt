[Intro]
Dark water moves under a roof of leaves.
We follow one slow bend without pretending the whole rainforest lives in the river.

[Verse 1]
A — Anaconda! Heavy scaled body near wet bank and water | a low curve beside the moving current.
B ... B ... Boa! Thick-bodied snake can coil on branches | one dry perch above the river route.
C? Ceiba Tree! Huge trunk rises from bank-side forest | crown disappears into the roof over our water.
D is Dragonfly. Wings hover over the surface | quick turns catch reflected light at the first bend.

[Verse 2]
E — Elephant! Large feet stay on land | trunk can reach, smell, touch, and drink near water's edge.
F ... F ... Fern! Divided fronds lean from damp ground | quiet green at the edge of the current.
G? Gorilla! Powerful primate remains on forest ground and bank-side cover | not every river clue belongs in water.
H is Hornbill. Large bill crosses overhead | broad wings move from one bank of green to the other.

[Chorus]
Bank, bend, flow—A-B-C!
Water below, green overhead, onward we go.

[Verse 3]
I — Iguana! Long tail lies along a branch or bank-side perch | spines make a clear silhouette above reflection.
J ... J ... Jungle Vine! Climbing stem drops through the green roof | one line nearly touching the river view.
K? Kinkajou! Night-active branch traveler | gripping feet move above the route, not through the current.
L is Liana. Woody climber loops from trunk to trunk | its curve repeats in the water below.

[Verse 4]
M — Monkey! Branch grip, swing, pause | movement overhead follows the river bend beneath.
N ... N ... Nest! Twig cup held above the corridor | we pass quietly and leave it undisturbed.
O? Ocelot! Spotted cat moves through lower bank-side cover | quick shadow between roots and leaves.
P is Palm. Crown of broad leaves leans toward open light | reflection breaks into green pieces on the water.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quetzal! Bright bird crosses a canopy gap | color flashes above a darker river mirror.
R ... R ... Rattan! Climbing palm weaves long flexible stems | tangled bank-side lines follow us downstream.
S? Sloth! Slow body hangs beneath a branch | current moves faster below than the animal moves above.
T is Toucan. Huge bill perches over the corridor | one bold shape where canopy opens.

[Verse 6]
U — Uakari! Short-tailed rainforest monkey moves among branches | another overhead clue beyond the next bend.
V ... V ... Vine! Stem climbs, trails, and crosses supports | green lines frame the river without becoming water plants.
W? Water Vine! Long green stem threads through humid forest | a living line near our last shaded bend.
At the river camp, X is Xenops. A small tropical bird searches bark and branches for insects.

[Chorus]
Bank, bend, flow—A-B-C!
Water below, green overhead, onward we go.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Anaconda! Large yellowish snake with dark blotches | a wet-habitat curve seen from respectful distance.
Z ... Z ... Zingiber Plant! Leafy stems rise from underground rhizomes | tropical green waiting near the landing.

[Interlude]
Let the boat-glide stop for one beat—what river-corridor clues return?
