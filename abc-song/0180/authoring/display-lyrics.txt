[Intro]
Mood Meter helps name, timer helps make waiting visible, quiet corner offers space, voice helps communicate.
A feelings toolbox works only when the tool matches the need.

[Verse 1]
A — Angry Face! | Anger cue; one face cannot show the whole reason or intensity.
B ... B ... Bashful Bunny! | Character suggests bashfulness; it is a discussion cue, not a diagnosis.
C? Caring Card! | Prompt to check needs or offer help; ask before touching or solving.
D is Delight Star. Delight symbol; the feeling is possible, never required. |

[Verse 2]
E — Excited Eyes! | Excitement cue; wide eyes have many meanings, so ask rather than assume.
F ... F ... Friendship Bracelet! | Friendship symbol; exchanging one is optional and does not measure friendship quality.
G? Gratitude Jar! | Optional gratitude notes can record appreciated moments without dismissing difficult feelings.
H is Happy Face. Happiness cue; people show happiness differently and smiles have many meanings. |

[Verse 3]
I — Inside Voice! | Cue for quieter indoor volume; feelings and needs can still be said.
J ... J ... Jealous Face! | Jealousy cue; having the feeling is different from acting harmfully.
K? Kind Hands! | Cue for safe helpful hands; kindness also includes words, listening, boundaries, or help.
L is Lonely Face. Loneliness cue; someone can feel lonely even around others. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mood Meter! | Self-reflection chart for feeling/energy; not a diagnosis.
N ... N ... Nervous Face! | Nervousness cue; a face alone cannot measure experience.
O? Okay Sign! | Okay symbol varies by culture; verbal confirmation can be clearer.
P is Patience Timer. Timer makes waiting visible; frustration need not vanish. |

[Chorus]

[Verse 5]
Q — Quiet Corner! | Optional low-stimulation pause space; not punishment or forced isolation.
R ... R ... Respect Badge! | Respect symbol; boundaries, listening, fairness, and safety matter more than obedience.
S? Smile! | Smile can express many social/emotional states; it does not prove happiness or need to be demanded.
T is Thank-you Card. Written gratitude; appreciation works best when specific and sincere. |

[Verse 6]
U — Understanding Heart! | Symbol for perspective-taking; understanding does not require agreement or accepting harm.
V ... V ... Valued Badge! | Reminder that a person matters; worth is not earned by winning or cheerfulness.
W? Warm Hug Card! | Hug prompt only when both want it; care never requires touch.
X is XOXO Card. X/O affection symbols in some cultures; physical affection always requires consent. |

[Verse 7]
Y — Yoga Card! | Optional comfortable movement/breathing prompt; range stays pain-free.
Z ... Z ... Zigzag Breath Card! | Visual breathing pace; return to normal breathing if uncomfortable or dizzy.
