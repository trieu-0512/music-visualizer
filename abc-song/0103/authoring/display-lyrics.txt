[Intro]
Desk first, library later, art after that, then the zipper pouch closes near the end.
A school day changes jobs as the hours move.

[Verse 1]
A — Art Paper! | Drawing/craft paper; thickness and surface vary by medium.
B ... B ... Book! | Pages can carry stories, facts, images, or instructions; contents tell the purpose.
C? Calculator! | Electronic or mechanical tool performs entered calculations; correct keys still depend on choosing the right mathematical operation.
D is Desk. Work surface supports books, writing, and materials; clear space helps separate active work from stored supplies. |

[Verse 2]
E — Eraser! | Rubber-like material lifts many graphite marks from paper; it does not erase every kind of ink or damage-free mark.
F ... F ... Folder! | Folded cover keeps loose papers together and separates subjects or projects.
G? Globe! | Spherical Earth model reduces many shape distortions of flat maps, while remaining tiny-scale.
H is Hole Punch. Lever tool cuts small holes through paper; moving metal parts can pinch, so preschool handling belongs with an adult or teacher. |

[Verse 3]
I — Index Card! | Small stiff card gives limited writing space for one note, label, example, or study prompt.
J ... J ... Journal! | Record collects thoughts, observations, or events over time.
K? Knowledge Card! | Project card holds a fact, clue, or concept; it is not one standardized product.
L is Library Book. Shared book is borrowed for a period and returned so another reader can use the same copy. |

[Verse 4]
M — Map! | Place model uses symbols, direction, scale, and selected features.
N ... N ... Notebook! | Bound pages keep related notes or drawings in order and make earlier work easy to revisit.
O? Origami Paper! | Light square folding paper; classic origami builds forms mainly with creases.
P is Pencil. Writing tool usually contains a graphite-and-clay core, not metallic lead; pressure changes line darkness. |

[Verse 5]
Q — Quiz Card! | Prompt checks recall or understanding; a miss shows what to practice next.
R ... R ... Reading Book! | Book selected for reading practice or enjoyment; text difficulty and purpose matter more than the cover.
S? Scissors! | Two pivoting blades cut material by shearing; preschool use requires suitable blunt-tip tools and adult supervision.
T is Textbook. Instructional book organizes a subject into explanations, examples, images, and often practice material. |

[Verse 6]
U — Uniform! | Standardized clothing identifies a school or group; it is part of school routine, not a learning tool by itself.
V ... V ... Vocabulary Card! | Card links a word to meaning, example, picture, or pronunciation.
W? Workbook! | Practice book leaves spaces for learners to write, draw, solve, or respond directly on the page.
X is X-axis Graph. Horizontal Cartesian axis usually carries x-values paired with vertical y-values. |
