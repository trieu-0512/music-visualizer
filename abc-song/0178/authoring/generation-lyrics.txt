[Intro]
Quiet can help one person, soft music another, a hug only when wanted, and tiredness may simply need rest.
A calming tool is an option, not a command.

[Verse 1]
A — Affection Heart! | Heart card represents care; affection can be words, time, help, or consented touch.
B ... B ... Brave Badge! | Courage symbol; bravery can include asking help, speaking up, or declining unsafe risk.
C? Caring Card! | Prompt to check needs or offer help; ask before touching or solving.
D is Delight Star. Delight symbol; the feeling is possible, never required. |

[Verse 2]
E — Excited Eyes! | Excitement cue; wide eyes have many meanings, so ask rather than assume.
F ... F ... Friendship Bracelet! | Friendship symbol; exchanging one is optional and does not measure friendship quality.
G? Gentle Hands! | Cue for safe hands; gentleness includes respecting space and permission.
H is Heart. Care/love symbol here, not the biological heart. |

[Chorus]
Notice, choose, check—A-B-C!
If one tool does not fit, choose another or ask for help.

[Verse 3]
I — Interest Card! | Prompt represents curiosity or engagement; interest can change by activity, time, and person.
J ... J ... Joy Jar! | Joy notes recall enjoyable moments without requiring constant happiness.
K? Kind Hands! | Cue for safe helpful hands; kindness also includes words, listening, boundaries, or help.
L is Lonely Face. Loneliness cue; someone can feel lonely even around others. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mad Face! | Anger cue; the feeling is normal while unsafe actions still need limits.
N ... N ... Nervous Face! | Nervousness cue; a face alone cannot measure experience.
O? Okay Sign! | Okay symbol varies by culture; verbal confirmation can be clearer.
P is Proud Star. Pride symbol for effort, growth, identity, or achievement; no comparison is required. |

[Chorus]
Notice, choose, check—A-B-C!
If one tool does not fit, choose another or ask for help.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quiet Corner! | Optional low-stimulation pause space; not punishment or forced isolation.
R ... R ... Respect Badge! | Respect symbol; boundaries, listening, fairness, and safety matter more than obedience.
S? Sharing Box! | Turn-taking container; sharing respects ownership, safety, and boundaries.
T is Tired Face. Tiredness cue; many causes are possible and rest/support may help. |

[Verse 6]
U — Upset Face! | Upset cue; ask what is happening instead of guessing from a face.
V ... V ... Valued Badge! | Reminder that a person matters; worth is not earned by winning or cheerfulness.
W? Warm Hug Card! | Hug prompt only when both want it; care never requires touch.
X is Xylophone Calm Toy. Soft music toy may settle some people; others may prefer quiet. |

[Chorus]
Notice, choose, check—A-B-C!
If one tool does not fit, choose another or ask for help.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Feelings Chart! | Color-coded feelings chart; yellow only means what this chart key defines.
Z ... Z ... Zen Cushion! | Seated quiet-practice cushion; comfort is individual and Zen is not a generic calm-down command.
