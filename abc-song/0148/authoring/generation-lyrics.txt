[Intro]
Measure and level first, clamp before fastening, move material only when the path is clear.
Building order can prevent mistakes.

[Verse 1]
A — Auger! | Rotating helical bit drills holes in soil or material; powered augers are professional tools with entanglement hazards.
B ... B ... Bolt! | Externally threaded fastener commonly works with nut or threaded hole; diameter/pitch/grade must suit the joint.
C? Cement Mixer! | Rotating drum combines cement, aggregate, and water into concrete mix; moving machinery stays in restricted work zone.
D is Drill. Rotating tool makes holes or drives bits; powered drill is adult/professional equipment here. |

[Verse 2]
E — Excavator! | Boom, stick, and bucket dig and move material; rotating upper body creates a large exclusion zone.
F ... F ... Forklift! | Industrial truck raises loads on forks; only trained operators use it and it never carries casual riders.
G? Gravel! | Loose stone aggregate supports drainage, base layers, concrete mixes, or landscaping depending size/use.
H is Hard Hat. Protective helmet reduces head injury from some impacts/falling objects; correct type/fit matters. |

[Verse 3]
I — I-beam! | Structural beam with I-shaped cross-section places material far from center to resist bending efficiently.
J ... J ... Jackhammer! | Powered percussive demolition tool breaks concrete/asphalt; noise, vibration, debris, and weight require trained workers/PPE.
K? Keyhole Saw! | Narrow pointed hand saw cuts small openings/curves; sharp teeth make it adult-only here.
L is Level. Tool compares a surface to horizontal/vertical using vial, laser, or electronic sensing. |

N ... N ... Nail! | Metal fastener driven into material; sharp point and hammer use keep it adult-managed.
O? Oil Can! | Container/applicator delivers lubricant to machinery; contents and maintenance belong to adults.
P is Pliers. Hand tool grips, bends, or cuts depending jaw design; pinch/cut edges require supervised adult use. |

[Verse 5]
Q — Quick Clamp! | Clamp rapidly holds workpieces together; pinch points require careful adult positioning.
R ... R ... Rivet! | Permanent/semi-permanent fastener deforms to clamp materials together; installation uses specialized tools.
S? Saw! | Toothed cutting tool; hand and powered saws are adult/professional tools in this activity.
T is Trowel. Hand tool spreads mortar/concrete/plaster or works soil depending shape; construction versions are adult tools. |

[Verse 6]
U — Utility Knife! | Retractable/replaceable sharp blade cuts sheet materials; adult-only.
V ... V ... Vise! | Bench tool clamps work securely between jaws; fingers stay clear of closing jaws.
W? Wheelbarrow! | Single/front-wheel hand cart moves loose material; balanced load and clear route reduce tipping.
X is X-brace. Diagonal members cross in an X to resist racking/lateral movement in a frame. |

Y — Yardstick! | Rigid three-foot measuring stick in US customary measure; it measures length, not level/plumb.
Z ... Z ... Zip Tie! | Plastic cable tie locks through a ratcheting head to bundle items; cut tails can be sharp and loops must never restrain a person.
