[Intro]
Paw print, wing mark, flipper trail.
Snowy wildlife leaves a letter behind.

[Verse 1]
Look at A: Arctic Fox! White in the light | thick fur warm in Arctic white
B — Bear. A bear is a large furry mammal with strong legs, broad paws, and an excellent sense of smell.
Across the snow and ice, C meets Cod. Blue below | ocean swimmer on the go
D ... D ... Dog Sled! A dog sled rides on long runners over snow and can be pulled by a trained team of sled dogs.

[Verse 2]
What did E find? Eider! Feather light | beak and wings come into sight
Look at F: Fur Seal! It swims with strong flippers and has a thick coat of fur.
G — Goose. Glide wide | grassy banks on either side
Across the snow and ice, H meets Harp Seal. It is a northern seal whose adults have a dark harp-shaped marking on the back.

[Verse 3]
I ... I ... Iceberg! Iceberg bright | most stays hidden out of sight
What did J find? Jellyfish! It drifts with a soft bell-shaped body and trailing tentacles.
Look at K: Kittiwake! Feather light | beak and wings come into sight
L — Lynx. A lynx is a furry mammal that walks quietly and uses keen senses to explore its habitat.

[Verse 4]
Across the snow and ice, M meets Musk Ox. Hooves on ground | steady little steps go round
N ... N ... Narwhal! A narwhal is a whale that can have one long spiral tusk.
What did O find? Owl! Owl in the night | round eyes watching, wings in flight
Look at P: Puffin! It is a black-and-white seabird with a colorful bill and strong wings for both flight and underwater swimming.

[Verse 5]
Q — Qajaq. Qajaq is the Greenlandic word behind kayak, a narrow skin-covered boat traditionally used in Arctic waters.
Across the snow and ice, R meets Ringed Seal. It is a small Arctic seal with pale ring-shaped markings on its coat.
S ... S ... Seal! Sweep and spin | flippers pull the seal back in
What did T find? Tundra! It is cold open land where trees are scarce and low plants grow.

[Verse 6]
Look at U: Upland Goose! Perch, then fly | quick wingbeats cross the sky
V — Vole. A vole is a small mammal that moves close to the ground and searches its habitat for food or shelter.
Across the snow and ice, W meets Whale. Whale with a tail | rises to breathe along the trail
X ... X ... Xylophone! A xylophone is our X-word helper: a row of tuned bars makes different notes when struck.

[Verse 7]
What did Y find? Yellow-billed Loon! Wing to sky | feathered bird goes gliding by
Look at Z: Zoarcid Fish! It is an eel-shaped fish from a family often called eelpouts.
