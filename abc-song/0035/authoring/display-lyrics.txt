[Intro]
At first it is only a blue line under palms.
Then shade, water, tracks, and green tell us why this place matters.

O? Oasis! Water and vegetation gather inside dry country | the central idea of the whole route.
P is Palm. Crown of broad leaves throws shade | green reflection trembles where fronds meet water.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quail! Small ground bird uses cover near the greener edge | quick feet stay low beneath taller shapes.
R ... R ... Rattlesnake! Tail rattle and scaled body | venomous wildlife keeps its space, and we keep ours.
S? Sand! Tiny grains continue right to the oasis edge | dry texture beside cool reflection.
T is Tortoise. Hard shell and sturdy feet | slow desert life may use shade and suitable water without becoming an oasis-only animal.

[Verse 6]
U — Umbrella Thorn! Spreading thorny crown makes a patch of shade | dry-edge green beyond the palms.
V ... V ... Vulture! Broad wings soar over open country | a distant scavenger linked to the wider ecosystem, not the water alone.
W? Wadi! Usually dry channel can carry water after rain | the oasis story widens into water that appears and disappears.
X is Xerus. African ground squirrel of dry open country | quick movement at the drier edge of our oasis map.
