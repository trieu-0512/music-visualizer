[Intro]
Fresh water first. Habitat check second. Play comes after the animal's needs are met.
Pet care is a routine, not a pile of toys.

[Verse 1]
A — Angelfish! Tropical freshwater fish needs suitable tank volume, stable water quality, heat, and compatible tankmates | bowl-sized living is not enough.
B ... B ... Bowl! Food or water dish must suit the animal and stay clean | one bowl cannot replace a proper fish aquarium.
C? Cat! Needs food, fresh water, litter care, scratching outlets, play, rest, and veterinary care | independence is not no-care.
D is Doghouse. Outdoor shelter can provide shade and weather protection | it never replaces exercise, companionship, water, and safe indoor care where appropriate.

[Verse 2]
E — Exercise Wheel! Hamster wheel should be solid-surfaced and large enough for a natural running posture | tiny bent-back wheels are not enrichment.
F ... F ... Fish! Different species need different tank size, temperature, filtration, water chemistry, and social setup.
G? Grooming Brush! Coat-appropriate brushing can remove loose hair and detect tangles | gentle technique depends on species and coat type.
H is Hamster. Needs secure spacious habitat, deep bedding, hiding places, suitable wheel, food and water | many hamsters are solitary by nature.

[Chorus]
Water, food, shelter—A-B-C!
Care fits the species, every day.

[Verse 3]
I — Iguana! Reptile needs species-appropriate heat gradient, diet, humidity, space, and UVB exposure | care is specialized.
J ... J ... Jingle Ball! Toy may enrich play if size and construction are safe | inspect for loose parts and swallowing hazards.
K? Kibble! Dry prepared food can be part of a balanced diet when formulated for the species and life stage | portion still matters.
L is Leash. Properly fitted leash and collar or harness help manage a dog outdoors | training and supervision matter too.

[Verse 4]
M — Meal Bowl! Portion goes in the dish; nutritional balance comes from the food plan | a full bowl is not automatically the right amount.
N ... N ... Nail Clipper! Nail trimming needs correct tool and knowledge of the quick | adult or veterinary/grooming guidance prevents injury.
O? Octopus Toy! Shape is playful, but safety depends on material, size, seams, and the pet using it | inspect toys regularly.
P is Puppy. Young dog needs socialization, training, sleep, vaccines and veterinary care, suitable food, and safe exercise | play is only one part.

[Chorus]
Water, food, shelter—A-B-C!
Care fits the species, every day.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilt Bed! Soft resting place can add comfort | bedding must be washable and safe from chewing or loose threads for that animal.
R ... R ... Rabbit! Needs unlimited grass hay as a major diet component, leafy greens, water, space, hiding, and safe chewing opportunities.
S? Scratching Post! Gives cats an appropriate surface to scratch, stretch, and mark | stable height and placement improve use.
T is Turtle. Care varies by species; aquatic turtles need appropriate water, basking, filtration, heat, diet, and UVB.

[Verse 6]
U — UV Lamp! Reptile care often requires a suitable UVB source, correctly placed and replaced on schedule | not every lamp labeled UV is appropriate.
V ... V ... Vest Harness! Harness must fit the species and body securely without rubbing or restricting breathing or movement.
W? Water Bowl! Fresh clean water needs regular replacement | some species also need other hydration or aquatic-habitat systems.
X is Xylophone Toy, our picture clue | sound toys should be pet-safe and never forced on animals that find noise stressful.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Canary! Small songbird needs spacious flight-oriented cage, perches, balanced diet, bathing opportunity, light-dark routine, and social/environmental enrichment.
Z ... Z ... Zip Carrier! Secure ventilated carrier makes transport safer | introduce it calmly before emergency or vet travel.
