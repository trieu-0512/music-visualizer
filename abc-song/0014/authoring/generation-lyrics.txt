[Intro]
First gold touches the fence before the yard is loud.
We follow the light as the farm wakes.

[Verse 1]
Dawn touches A — Apple. Round fruit hangs among leaves beaded with morning light.
B is Barn. Roofline turns gold first | shelter still quiet beneath it.
C? Chicken! Scratch in the soft yard | small pecks begin as the morning shadows shorten.
D — Duck. First light trembles on pond water | webbed feet paddle through it.

[Verse 2]
E ... E ... Ewe. Wool catches pale light | an adult female sheep walking out with the flock.
F is Farmer. Morning jobs begin | checking animals, land, and crops as the day opens.
G? Grain! Dew catches the field | wheat, oats, or corn all give us small harvested seeds.
H — Hen. Soft cluck in the coop | an adult female chicken that can lay eggs.

[Verse 3]
Sun climbs; I is Irrigation Pump. Water moves through hose and pipe toward the crops.
J ... J ... Jug. Handle steady, narrow mouth | tip it and liquid pours in one clear stream.
K? Kid Goat! Quick hooves cross a stripe of sun | curious ears turn toward every sound.
L is Lamb. Small hooves, soft fleece | a young sheep warm in the widening light.

[Chorus]
Sunrise gold—soft and slow.
A-B-C—the whole farm starts to glow.

[Verse 4]
At noon, M — Milk. Pale food from mammals | part of the daily work on a dairy farm.
N ... N ... Nest. Twigs hold a shaded cup | eggs or chicks rest inside.
O? Ox! Strong shoulders under bright noon | trained cattle can pull heavy loads.
P is Pig. Snout searches cool soil | stout body steps into the shade.

[Verse 5]
Afternoon, Q — Quilt. Stitch after stitch | layered cloth waits folded for cooler hours.
R ... R ... Rooster! Morning crow is gone | bright comb still catches the sun.
S? Sheep! Fleece catches the long light | grazing slows near day's end.
T is Trough. Long open shape holds feed or water as evening chores begin.

[Verse 6]
Low sun finds U — Udder. Milk-producing organ beneath animals such as cows, goats, and sheep.
V ... V ... Vet Kit. Simple animal-check tools packed together | ready when a veterinarian needs them.
W? Wagon! Four wheels cross long shadows | carrying crops or supplies toward the barn.
X is X-ray Vet Image. Bones show in an inside view | one quiet clue for animal care.

[Chorus]
Sunrise gold—soft and slow.
A-B-C—the whole farm starts to glow.

[Verse 7]
Last light, Y — Yam. Thick tuber stays hidden in soil while leaves darken above.
Z ... Z ... Zebu. Shoulder hump in silhouette | cattle standing calm as daylight leaves the field.
