Intimate warm adult lead, minimal vibrato and close diction. Open 6/8 sunrise folk at 84 BPM with nylon guitar, mandolin taps, soft shaker, tiny glockenspiel and warm bass. The arranging principle is daylight: sparse dawn, slightly fuller morning, warm noon center, then thinning afternoon and evening. Round 1 is image-led free verse with sparse end rhyme; use assonance, held vowels and silence instead of forcing couplets. Long factual lines may take two bars, while short light-images can land in one. Chorus stays gentle—sunrise gold, soft and slow—without a big pop lift. Round 2 uses two supportive beats of recall space and restrained gestures; no bell or percussion attack on target onset. Technical animal-care terms stay speech-clear. End with longer guitar decay and the image of shadows fading, not spoken praise or a repeated high-energy hook.
