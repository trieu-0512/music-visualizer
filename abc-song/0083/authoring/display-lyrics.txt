[Intro]
Boa scales keep water in; frog skin must stay moist; axolotl gills wave outside the body.
Skin tells a habitat story.

[Verse 1]
A — Axolotl! Aquatic salamander retains feathery external gills | soft permeable amphibian skin stays underwater.
B ... B ... Boa! Keratinized reptile scales reduce water loss and protect the body | dry to the touch in normal conditions.
C? Crocodile! Tough scales and bony scutes armor a semi-aquatic reptile | water habitat does not mean amphibian skin.
D is Dart Frog. Moist amphibian skin supports gas/water exchange | bright color in many species warns predators.

[Verse 2]
E — Eastern Newt! Amphibian skin changes with aquatic/terrestrial life stages | moisture remains important.
F ... F ... Frog! Mucus helps keep permeable skin moist | healthy 'slime' is functional biology.
G? Garter Snake! Smooth/keeled scale patterns vary by species | scales protect a limbless reptile.
H is Hellbender. Giant aquatic salamander with folded skin increasing surface area | fully amphibian despite wrinkled appearance.

L is Leopard Gecko. Granular/tubercled scales and eyelids distinguish this gecko | dry-land reptile.

[Verse 4]

O? Ornate Box Turtle! Scales cover limbs while shell encloses trunk | reptile surfaces combine keratin and bone.
P is Python. Smooth overlapping scales cover a muscular snake | shedding replaces the outer skin layer.

[Verse 5]
Q — Queen Snake! North American semi-aquatic snake that often eats freshly molted crayfish | reptile scales remain dry-skin adaptation.
R ... R ... Red-eyed Tree Frog! Moist skin and adhesive toe pads support arboreal amphibian life.
S? Salamander! Permeable moist skin makes many species sensitive to dehydration and contaminants.
T is Turtle. Shell is bony skeleton covered by scutes in many species | not a loose external object.

V ... V ... Viper! Scaled venomous snake | surface pattern helps camouflage but does not make handling safe.
W? Wood Frog! Amphibian survives seasonal freezing of much body water in winter | moist skin returns to active spring life.
X is Xenosaurus. Knob-scaled lizard | textured reptile skin fits rocky crevices.
