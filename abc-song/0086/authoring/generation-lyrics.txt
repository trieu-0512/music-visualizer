[Intro]
Tap the table: one-two—kitchen picture beat!
We name the safe picture clues; real hot and sharp tools stay with grown-ups.

[Verse 1]
A—Apple! Apple round and bright | skin and core are picture clues in sight
B ... B ... Blender. A blender is a powered appliance with fast-moving blades used by a grown-up to mix or puree food.
Cup steps in for C. It is a small open container used for drinking or measuring.
What does D reveal? Donut! A donut is a baked or fried pastry often shaped like a ring or filled round.

[Verse 2]
across the tabletop picture beat, E meets Egg. Egg, oval shell | white and yolk are tucked in well
F—Fork! It is an eating utensil with several narrow tines.
G ... G ... Grapes. Grapes are small round or oval fruits that grow in bunches on vines.
Honey steps in for H. It is a thick sweet liquid made by honeybees from flower nectar.

K—Knife! A kitchen knife has a sharp blade for cutting food and is a grown-up tool in this preschool picture activity.
L ... L ... Ladle. A ladle is a long-handled spoon with a deep bowl for serving liquids.

[Verse 4]
Muffin steps in for M. Muffin in its cup | rounded top is rising up
What does N reveal? Napkin! A napkin is a piece of cloth or paper used to wipe hands or the mouth during a meal.
across the tabletop picture beat, O meets Oven. It is an enclosed appliance that cooks food with heat; a grown-up operates it because surfaces can become very hot.
P—Pan! It is a cooking vessel with a broad bottom; heated pans are handled by grown-ups.

across the tabletop picture beat, T meets Toast. It is bread browned by dry heat.

U—Under-counter Drawer! Drawer under counter slides | storage space is tucked inside
V ... V ... Vegetable Peeler. A vegetable peeler has a sharp slot blade that removes thin strips of peel and is used with adult supervision.
Waffle steps in for W. Waffle on display | little grid marks cross it every way
What does X reveal? Xylophone! A xylophone is a musical instrument, not a kitchen tool; its bars make pitched notes when struck.

Z—Zester! It has small sharp edges that remove thin fragrant pieces of citrus peel; a grown-up handles it here.
