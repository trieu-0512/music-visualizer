Hot, sharp or powered equipment is described, never operated by the child. Use mixed entry families—direct hit, repeated-letter pickup, object-first reveal, question-answer and small narrative turn—without cycling them mechanically A-B-C-D. Use small melodic surprises—pickup, held note, half-bar rest, call-and-answer, octave color or rhythmic displacement—sparingly so the catalog feels authored, not randomized.
