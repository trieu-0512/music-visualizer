[Intro]
Apple can sit in our picture space; knife, oven, blender, peeler and zester need a different rule.
Before naming the action, we name the safety category.

[Verse 1]
A — Apple! Fruit with skin, flesh, core and seeds | food clue, not a tool.
B ... B ... Blender! Powered appliance spins sharp blades inside a closed jar | adult-operated and kept off during picture play.
C? Cup! Open container for drinking or measuring | ordinary handling item when unbreakable and suitable.
D is Donut. Fried or baked sweet pastry | food category, with portion/allergy context rather than a tool action.

[Verse 2]
E — Egg! Food with shell, white and yolk | raw egg handling requires hygiene and cooking safety.
F ... F ... Fork! Tined eating utensil | table tool, used calmly rather than waved.
G? Grapes! Fruit growing in bunches | for young children whole grapes can be a choking hazard and should be prepared appropriately by an adult.
H is Honey. Sweet food made by bees from nectar | not for infants under 12 months because of botulism risk.

[Chorus]
Cold, hot, sharp—A-B-C!
Safe hands know which tools belong with grown-ups.

[Verse 3]
I — Ice Tray! Mold freezes water into cubes | cold item, with small ice pieces still needing age-appropriate supervision.
J ... J ... Juicer! Manual or powered tool separates juice | powered/sharp designs belong with adults.
K? Knife! Sharp cutting blade | adult-only in this preschool activity; point to the picture, never mime cutting with an object.
L is Ladle. Deep-bowled serving spoon | safe only when contents are not hot.

[Verse 4]
M — Muffin! Baked food with soft crumb | food clue, not proof that every muffin has the same ingredients.
N ... N ... Napkin! Cloth or paper for wiping hands/mouth | no heat or blade risk.
O? Oven! Enclosed high-heat appliance | adult-only; hot door, racks, pans and steam can burn.
P is Pan. Cooking vessel | harmless when cool, burn hazard when heated.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quart Cup! US liquid measure equal to four US cups | measurement tool, not a universal international kitchen unit.
R ... R ... Rice! Grain cooked with water | food safety includes proper cooking and storage.
S? Spoon! Bowl-shaped utensil for scooping or stirring | safe empty-hand mimic only.
T is Toast. Bread browned by heat | food arrives after the hot appliance step is finished by an adult.

[Chorus]
Cold, hot, sharp—A-B-C!
Safe hands know which tools belong with grown-ups.

[Verse 6]
U — Under-counter Drawer! Storage space | sharp tools may be secured inside, out of children's reach.
V ... V ... Vegetable Peeler! Sharp slot blade removes peel | adult-guided or adult-only depending age; picture-only here.
W? Waffle! Cooked batter food with grid surface | hot waffle iron stays adult-only.
X is Xylophone. Musical instrument, deliberately not a kitchen tool | X mapping helper keeps categories honest.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yam! Starchy tuber | must be prepared/cooked appropriately; not the same plant as sweet potato.
Z ... Z ... Zester! Sharp-edged tool removes thin citrus zest | adult-only picture target.

[Interlude]
The picture table goes quiet; hands are empty. Now remember the kitchen words.
