[Intro]
Apartment is private home, library is shared learning space, hospital is healthcare, bus stop is a waiting point.
A town map makes more sense when we ask what each place is for.

[Verse 1]
A — Apartment! | Private dwelling unit inside a larger building; hallways/elevators may be shared while the home itself is private.
B ... B ... Bus Stop! | Designated place where buses pick up/drop off passengers; riders wait back from traffic and check route information.
C? Crosswalk! | Marked pedestrian crossing; users still look/listen and follow signals because paint does not physically stop vehicles.
D is Department Store. Large retail store organized into multiple merchandise departments; it is a commercial place, not a public service office. |

[Verse 2]
E — Elevator! | Lift transports people/goods vertically between floors; doors, capacity, and emergency controls require normal safety behavior.
F ... F ... Fire Station! | Base for firefighters, engines, rescue equipment, and training; emergency bays must stay clear.
G? Garage! | Building or bay stores/repairs vehicles; active repair areas can contain moving cars, tools, chemicals, and are not play spaces.
H is Hospital. Healthcare facility provides diagnosis/treatment, emergency, inpatient, or specialist services; access and roles vary by department. |

K? Kindergarten! | Early-childhood education setting focused on learning, play, routines, and social development; age structure varies by country.
L is Library. Public/school institution lends or provides access to books, media, information, and study space; borrowing has return rules. |

[Verse 5]
Q — Queue! | Ordered line of people waiting for service; it organizes turns rather than being a place/building itself.
R ... R ... Restaurant! | Business prepares and serves meals/drinks; kitchen areas are staff work zones while dining areas serve customers.
S? School! | Education institution with classrooms, staff, shared facilities, and safety/arrival routines.
T is Taxi. Passenger-for-hire road service; legal licensing, fares, and pickup rules vary by place. |
