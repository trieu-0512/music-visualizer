[Intro]
Outer ear gathers sound, retina detects light, tongue helps taste, hand carries touch signals.
A sense is a pathway, not one isolated body part.

[Verse 1]
A — Arm! | Upper limb from shoulder through hand in everyday use; bones, joints, muscles, nerves and vessels coordinate movement.
B ... B ... Belly! | Everyday word for abdomen, containing abdominal wall and many digestive/urinary/reproductive organs; outside shape does not reveal health by itself.
C? Cheek! | Side of face contains skin, fat, muscles, and part of the cheekbone; cheek movement contributes to expression and chewing.
D is Digestive System. Organ system breaks food into absorbable nutrients and moves waste onward; mouth, stomach, intestines, liver, pancreas and other organs contribute different steps. |

[Verse 2]
E — Elbow! | Joint between upper arm and forearm mainly bends/straightens and helps forearm rotation through nearby joints.
F ... F ... Foot! | Body structure with many bones, joints, muscles, tendons and sensory receptors; it supports weight, balance and walking.
G? Gum! | Gingiva is soft tissue around teeth that helps seal/protect tooth-supporting structures; healthy gums still need regular oral care.
H is Hand. Body part with palm, fingers and thumb supports touch, grasping, communication, and many fine movements. |

[Chorus]
Sense, signal, brain—A-B-C!
Follow information farther than the surface.

[Verse 3]
I — Incisor! | Front cutting tooth with a relatively sharp edge; humans normally have incisors in upper and lower jaws.
J ... J ... Jaw! | Upper and lower jaw support teeth; the mandible is the movable lower jaw used in chewing and speech.
K? Knee! | Large joint connecting thigh and lower leg; bones, cartilage, menisci, ligaments, tendons and muscles share load and movement.
L is Lips. Soft tissue around mouth opening supports speech, eating, expression and touch. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mouth! | Opening and oral cavity begin digestion and support taste, chewing, swallowing, speech and breathing pathways.
N ... N ... Nail! | Hard keratin plate protects the tips of fingers/toes and supports fine touch; nail health cannot diagnose the whole body by itself.
O? Outer Ear! | Visible pinna plus outer ear canal collect/direct sound toward eardrum; hearing continues through middle/inner ear and brain.
P is Pupil. Opening at center of iris lets light into eye; it appears black and changes size as iris muscles respond. |

[Chorus]
Sense, signal, brain—A-B-C!
Follow information farther than the surface.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quick Pulse! | Pulse is a rhythmic pressure wave caused by each heartbeat; a quicker rate can follow activity, emotion, fever, medicines or illness, so context matters.
R ... R ... Retina! | Light-sensitive neural tissue lines back of eye and converts light into nerve signals sent toward brain.
S? Shoulder! | Region links arm to trunk through several joints, muscles and tendons, giving a wide range of motion.
T is Tongue. Muscular organ helps move food, taste, speak and swallow; taste receptors are distributed across the tongue rather than strict taste zones. |

[Verse 6]
U — Uvula! | Small soft-tissue projection hanging from soft palate; it contributes to swallowing/speech functions but is not the sole gag structure.
V ... V ... Voice! | Sound begins when airflow makes vocal folds vibrate, then throat, mouth and nose shape resonance and speech.
W? Wrist! | Joint region links forearm to hand through multiple small carpal bones, ligaments and tendons.
X is Xiphoid Bone. Xiphoid process is the small lower part of sternum; it can be cartilaginous when young and ossifies variably with age. |

[Chorus]
Sense, signal, brain—A-B-C!
Follow information farther than the surface.

[Verse 7]
Y — Y-shaped Bone Model! | Project teaching model shaped like Y; it demonstrates a bone/model concept but is not proof that a normal human bone universally has this shape.
Z ... Z ... Zinc Bandage! | Project label for a zinc-containing dressing, such as zinc-oxide bandage used in some medical care; selection/application belongs to trained healthcare guidance.
