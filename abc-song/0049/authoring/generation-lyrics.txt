[Intro]
Whoosh for wind, tap for rain, hush for snow.
Three weather rhythms—and the letters go.

[Verse 1]
Under the changing sky, A finds Anemometer. Spin in the breeze | wind-speed readings come with ease
B — Boots. Boots cover the feet and ankles; weather boots help keep feet dry, warm, or protected.
What waits at C? Coat! Coat wrapped round | warm outer layer when cold comes down
D is for Drizzle. Drizzle is very light rain made of small drops.

[Verse 2]
Eye of Storm — our E. Calm at the core | storm winds circle round once more
Under the changing sky, F finds Frost. It is a thin layer of ice crystals that can form on cold surfaces.
G — Gust. Gust goes rush | quick strong wind, then back to hush
What waits at H? Humidity Meter! It measures how much water vapor is in the air.

[Verse 3]
I is for Ice. Cold and bright | frozen water catches light
Jet Stream — our J. It is a fast-moving band of air high in the atmosphere.
Under the changing sky, K finds Kelvin Thermometer. Kelvin scale | temperature numbers tell the tale
L — Lightning. Lightning is a bright electrical flash in a storm; we watch storms safely from indoors.

[Chorus]
Wind, rain, snow—A-B-C!
Weather changes—sing with me!

[Verse 4]
What waits at M? Mittens! Hands tucked in | fingers share the warmth within
N is for Nimbus Cloud. Nimbus is a weather word associated with rain-bearing clouds.
Overcoat — our O. Long coat on | extra warmth when cold comes on
Under the changing sky, P finds Puddle. It is a small shallow pool of water on the ground.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilt. Stitch by stitch | warm layered fabric, soft and rich
What waits at R? Raincoat! It uses water-resistant material to help keep clothing dry in rain.
S is for Sun. Sun up high | daylight warms us from the sky
Thunder — our T. It is the sound made by air rapidly expanding around lightning.

[Verse 6]
Under the changing sky, U finds Umbrella. Open it wide | raindrops slide from side to side
V — Vapor. Water vapor is water in its invisible gas form in the air.
What waits at W? Windsock! Fill and fly | wind direction meets our eye
X is for X-band Radar. X-band radar uses short radio waves to detect things such as rain or moving objects.

[Chorus]
Wind, rain, snow—A-B-C!
Weather changes—sing with me!

[Verse 7]
Yellow Sun — our Y. Picture sun high | bright yellow circle in the sky
Under the changing sky, Z finds Zephyr. It is a soft, gentle breeze.
