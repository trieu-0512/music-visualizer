[Intro]
Wake, wash, dress, eat, pack—then the order can change when the day changes.
A routine is a memory path, not a rulebook carved in stone.

[Verse 1]
A — Apron! | Protective garment covers clothing during cooking/craft/cleaning; it does not replace handwashing or safe tool use.
B ... B ... Bath! | Washing body in tub/shower; young children need close adult supervision around water and safe water temperature.
C? Comb! | Toothed grooming tool separates/arranges hair; clean personal combs reduce sharing of scalp contaminants.
D is Door. Movable barrier opens/closes a passage; hinge and closing edge are pinch points. |

[Verse 2]
E — Eggs! | Food with shell, white, and yolk; raw/undercooked egg can carry pathogens, so hygiene and cooking guidance matter.
F ... F ... Fork! | Tined eating utensil; use seated/calmly and choose age-appropriate size.
G? Getting-dressed Chart! | Visual sequence supports remembering clothing steps; it is a prompt, not a rule that every outfit uses every item.
H is Hairbrush. Bristled/pinned grooming tool detangles/styles hair; gentle technique avoids pulling and personal brushes should stay clean. |

J ... J ... Jacket! | Outer layer adds warmth/weather protection depending material; choose it for conditions, not simply because it appears on a chart.
K? Kettle! | Appliance/vessel heats water; boiling water, steam, and hot surfaces are adult-only.
L is Lunchbox. Container carries a meal/snack; food temperature and hygiene depend on contents and storage. |

[Verse 5]
Q — Quiet Book! | Soft/activity or reading book for calm independent/shared use; quiet is an option, not a punishment.
R ... R ... Routine Chart! | Visual list helps remember repeated tasks; routines can flex when needs or schedules change.
S? Soap! | Cleaning substance helps water remove oils/dirt; avoid eyes/mouth and use the product intended for the task.
T is Toothbrush. Brush cleans tooth surfaces with toothpaste/water as appropriate; it is personal and should not be shared. |

W? Washcloth! | Small absorbent cloth supports washing skin/surfaces; use clean cloth and appropriate soap/water.
X is Xylophone. Tuned-bar musical instrument; deliberate routine-set comparison item, not a required morning chore. |
