[Intro]
Cluck-cluck, hoof-step—morning at the gate!
The barn wakes up, and the letters don't wait.

[Verse 1]
A — Apron! It covers the front of clothes and helps keep them cleaner during messy work.
B ... B ... Bucket! A bucket has a handle and a deep open top for carrying things such as water or feed.
C is for Cow. Cow says moo | slow farm steps come into view
What does D bring? Donkey! A donkey has long ears, sturdy hooves, and a strong body used for carrying or pulling loads.

[Verse 2]
By the barn, E meets Egg. Egg in the nest | oval shell at rest
F — Fence! It makes a boundary around a field, yard, or animal area.
G ... G ... Grain! Grain is the small seed harvested from crops such as wheat, oats, or corn.
H is for Hay. It is cut and dried grass or other plants stored as animal feed.

[Chorus]
Good morning, farm—A-B-C!
Barnyard words for you and me!

[Verse 3]
What does I bring? Irrigation Pump! An irrigation pump moves water through pipes or hoses so crops can be watered.
By the barn, J meets Jug. It is a container with a handle and a narrow opening for pouring liquid.
K — Kid Goat! Step by step | sturdy legs keep moving yet
L ... L ... Lamb! A lamb is a young sheep with a soft woolly coat and small hooves.

[Verse 4]
M is for Mule. Hooves on ground | steady little steps go round
What does N bring? Nectarine! Nectarine is a plant food that grows from a crop, vine, shrub, or tree before it is picked.
By the barn, O meets Ox. Hooves go slow | sure-foot steps along below
P — Pig! It has a strong snout for rooting and a stout body carried on four hooved feet.

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Quail! Quail on the trail | ground bird with a little tail
R is for Rooster. It is an adult male chicken with a noticeable comb and a loud crow.
What does S bring? Scarecrow! A scarecrow is a human-shaped figure placed in a field to discourage birds from feeding on crops.
By the barn, T meets Tractor. It is a powerful farm vehicle built to pull or operate heavy equipment.

[Chorus]
Good morning, farm—A-B-C!
Barnyard words for you and me!

[Verse 6]
U — Utility Cart! It carries tools, feed, or supplies around a farm or garden.
V ... V ... Vet Kit! A vet kit holds simple tools a veterinarian uses to check animal health.
W is for Wagon. Wagon on the way | four wheels carry things today
What does X bring? Xylophone! A xylophone is our X-word helper: a row of tuned bars makes different notes when struck.

[Verse 7]
By the barn, Y meets Yam. It stores food in a thick underground tuber.
Z — Zebu! It is a type of cattle with a noticeable hump over its shoulders.

[Interlude]
The barnyard settles for one breath—now call the words back.
