[Intro]
Gate latch clicks; one rooster sends the morning wide.
Hoofbeat, cluck, bucket-ring—the farm wakes sound by sound.

[Verse 1]
A — Apron! Cloth across the front | ready for splashes, crumbs, and farm-day mess.
B ... B ... Bucket! Handle up, hollow down | water or feed rides from place to place.
C is for Cow. Slow hoof, soft moo | grass goes in, steady chewing all morning through.
D? Donkey! Long ears turn toward the gate | sturdy hooves carry a strong body down the lane.

[Verse 2]
Near the coop, E is Egg. Smooth oval shell | small and still in a nest of straw.
F — Fence! Post after post | a clear edge keeps field and animal space apart.
G ... G ... Grain! Tiny seeds in a golden handful | wheat, oats, or corn begin this small.
H is for Hay. Cut, dried, stacked | summer grass becomes feed for another day.

[Chorus]
Cluck, creak, hoofbeat—A-B-C!
Morning wakes in sound from A to Z!

[Verse 3]
I? Irrigation Pump! Water wakes in the pipe | pushed through hose or line toward thirsty crops.
J — Jug! Handle in one hand, narrow mouth above | tip it gently and the liquid pours.
K ... K ... Kid Goat! Quick little hooves, curious ears | a young goat testing every step.
L is for Lamb. Soft fleece, small hooves | a young sheep stays close to the flock.

[Verse 4]
M? Mule! Strong legs, steady pace | a sturdy hoofed animal that can carry or pull farm loads.
N — Nectarine! Smooth skin, round fruit | ripening warm on a tree before it is picked.
O ... O ... Ox! Slow pull, strong shoulders | trained cattle can move heavy farm loads.
P is for Pig. Snout to soil | rooting, sniffing, stepping on four hooved feet.

[Verse 5]
Q? Quail! Low in the grass | quick feet, small pecks, then a sudden burst of wing.
R — Rooster! Comb catches sunrise red | one bright crow carries over the yard.
S ... S ... Scarecrow! Sleeves spread wide in the field | a human-shaped watcher meant to discourage hungry birds.
T is for Tractor. Big wheels, low rumble | built to pull or power heavy farm work.

[Chorus]
Cluck, creak, hoofbeat—A-B-C!
Morning wakes in sound from A to Z!

[Verse 6]
U? Utility Cart! Tools, feed, supplies aboard | a small carrier rolling between jobs.
V — Vet Kit! Simple checking tools packed together | helpers for a veterinarian caring for animals.
W ... W ... Wagon! Four wheels follow the path | crops or supplies ride together.
At the porch, X is Xylophone—our X-word helper | tuned bars answer the farm rhythm note by note.

[Verse 7]
Y? Yam! Hidden under soil | a thick tuber stores food below the leaves.
Z — Zebu! Hump above the shoulders | sturdy cattle standing warm in the sun.

[Interlude]
The yard goes quiet for one beat—can the morning sounds come back?
