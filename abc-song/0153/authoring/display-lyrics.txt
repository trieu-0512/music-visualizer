[Intro]
A sign can say City Hall, a marquee can say Theater, a school gate can mark student access.
A building's facade gives clues, not the whole story.

B ... B ... Bakery! | Shop/workplace produces or sells breads/pastries; hot ovens and back-of-house equipment stay staff-only.
C? City Hall! | Municipal government building/offices support local administration and public services; access areas and procedures vary.
D is Doctor. Medical professional diagnoses/treats health conditions; examination and treatment require consent, training, and clinical setting. |

[Verse 2]

G? Garage! | Building or bay stores/repairs vehicles; active repair areas can contain moving cars, tools, chemicals, and are not play spaces.
H is Hotel. Lodging business provides guest rooms and services; guest/private/service areas have different access. |
