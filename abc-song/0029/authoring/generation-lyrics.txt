[Intro]
A green curve vanishes behind one wet leaf.
Do not chase the clue—hold still and let the picture come back.

[Verse 1]
First clue A — Anaconda. A long scaled curve near wet cover | enough to name from far away, never a reason to move closer.
B ... B ... Banana! Huge torn-edged leaves make the first shape | curved fruit appears only after the eye settles.
C? Ceiba Tree! Massive trunk fills the frame before the crown | a tree too large for one quick glance.
D is Dragonfly. A flash, then hover | four clear wings become visible when the tiny shape pauses.

[Verse 2]
E — Emerald Tree Boa! Green coil around a branch | camouflage turns into a snake once the outline is found.
F ... F ... Frog! Strong back legs fold beneath a small body | wet leaf and animal separate when we look carefully.
G? Gecko! Toes spread against bark | the small lizard shape appears where color nearly disappears.
H is Howler Monkey. Dark body high in leaves | sometimes the call reveals the animal before binoculars do.

[Chorus]
Glimpse, shape, reveal—A-B-C!
Good explorer eyes can wait before they name.

[Verse 3]
I — Iguana! Long tail, row of back spines | a jagged silhouette turns into a large lizard.
J ... J ... Jungle Vine! One green line crosses another | follow the stem with your eyes until it reaches higher light.
K? Kinkajou! Round face, branch-gripping body | a night-active mammal best known through careful observation.
L is Leopard. Rosette-like spots break up a powerful cat shape | camouflage works until motion gives one clue away.

[Verse 4]
M — Monkey! A hand grips first, then the body swings into view | one clue becomes a whole animal.
N ... N ... Nest! Twigs form a cup between branches | binoculars stay back so the nest stays undisturbed.
O? Orchid! Strange petal shape in shade | color and central form reveal the flower without touching it.
P is Parrot. Curved beak, gripping feet, feather color | three field marks solve one branch-top clue.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quetzal! Green-bright feathers and long tail can flash through a canopy gap | a brief reveal worth waiting for.
R ... R ... Rainforest Frog! Moist skin, strong legs | small shape sits where wet leaf and animal colors meet.
S? Sloth! Rounded body hangs beneath a branch | slow motion makes the hidden shape easier to keep in frame.
T is Tapir. Short flexible snout appears ahead of a sturdy body | a low forest mammal crossing the opening.

[Chorus]
Glimpse, shape, reveal—A-B-C!
Good explorer eyes can wait before they name.

[Verse 6]
U — Umbrella Leaf! Broad leaf makes a green canopy of its own | one simple shape hiding smaller clues beneath.
V ... V ... Viper! Patterned venomous snake | binocular distance is exactly the right distance.
W? Waterfall! White moving strip through green | sound and motion reveal it before the full rock face appears.
X is Xenops. Small bird works along bark | narrow bill and creeping branch search make the clue clearer.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Anaconda! Dark blotches on yellowish skin | a large South American snake identified without approach.
Z ... Z ... Zingiber Plant! Leafy tropical stems rise from rhizomes below | final clue stays rooted while our eyes move on.
