[Intro]
Drip-drop, flutter, rustle—listen!
Rainforest letters hide in every layer.

[Verse 1]
A — Anteater! It has a long snout and sticky tongue for reaching ants and termites.
B ... B ... Boa! A boa is a thick-bodied snake that moves by muscular curves and can coil around branches.
C is for Chameleon. It has gripping feet, independently moving eyes, and a long tongue for catching prey.
What does D bring? Drongo! A drongo is a dark-colored bird known for agile flight and a forked or notched tail.

[Verse 2]
Under the green canopy, E meets Elephant. It uses its long flexible trunk to smell, touch, drink, and pick things up.
F — Fern! It unfurls divided green fronds from curled young growth called fiddleheads.
G ... G ... Gecko! Many geckos have gripping toe pads that help them cling to bark, leaves, or other surfaces.
H is for Hornbill. It is a tropical bird with a large curved bill, often topped by a raised casque.

[Verse 3]
What does I bring? Iguana! An iguana is a large lizard with a long tail and a row of spines along its back.
Under the green canopy, J meets Jungle Vine. It climbs through other plants to reach light in the canopy.
K — Kinkajou! Near the ground | tiny footsteps move around
L ... L ... Leopard! A leopard is a powerful spotted cat that can climb and move quietly through cover.

[Chorus]
Rainforest rhythm—A-B-C!
Jungle words bounce back to me!

[Verse 4]
M is for Macaw. Bright in flight | broad wings flash with color and light
What does N bring? Nectar Bat! A nectar bat visits flowers and drinks nectar with a long tongue.
Under the green canopy, O meets Orchid. Bloom and show | little petals open as they grow
P — Parrot! It has a strong curved beak and gripping feet for holding onto branches and food.

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Queen Butterfly! A queen butterfly has orange-brown wings marked with dark veins and pale spots.
R is for Rainforest Frog. It has moist skin and strong legs, and many species live among wet leaves or branches.
What does S bring? Snake! A snake moves without legs by bending its long scaled body in muscular curves.
Under the green canopy, T meets Toucan. It is a tropical bird with an exceptionally large colorful bill.

[Verse 6]
U — Uakari! It is a rainforest monkey with a short tail and a distinctive bare face.
V ... V ... Viper! A viper is a venomous snake; we learn its shape and markings while observing safely from a distance.
W is for Waterfall. Water falls tall | rushing sheet by rocky wall
What does X bring? Xylophone! A xylophone is our X-word helper: a row of tuned bars makes different notes when struck.

[Verse 7]
Under the green canopy, Y meets Yellow Anaconda. It is a large South American snake with dark blotches on yellowish skin.
Z — Zingiber Plant! It is a tropical ginger relative that grows leafy stems from underground rhizomes.
