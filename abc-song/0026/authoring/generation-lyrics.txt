[Intro]
Drip. Leaf-rustle. Wing-flutter. Hush.
The rainforest has layers, and each layer keeps a different pulse.

[Verse 1]
A — Anteater! Long snout, sticky tongue | quiet searching for ants and termites beneath the larger forest sounds.
B ... B ... Boa! Thick body curves along a branch | muscle makes the motion, not a drumbeat.
C? Chameleon! Gripping feet hold bark | two eyes can scan in different directions before the long tongue strikes.
D is Drongo. Dark bird, agile flight | a forked or notched tail cuts through a quick wing rhythm.

[Verse 2]
E — Elephant! Trunk curls, touches, smells, drinks | one large body moving carefully through the green.
F ... F ... Fern! Curled growth opens into divided fronds | a silent green rest between animal sounds.
G? Gecko! Toe pads grip bark and leaf | tiny feet hold where smooth surfaces would make us slip.
H is Hornbill. Large curved bill, raised casque | heavy wingbeats can pass high above the wet leaves.

[Verse 3]
I — Iguana! Long tail, strong body, back spines | a lizard shape stretched across branch and light.
J ... J ... Jungle Vine! It climbs through other plants toward brighter canopy | green motion measured in growth, not footsteps.
K? Kinkajou! Gripping feet and long tail help it move among branches | a night visitor in the canopy.
L is Leopard. Spotted coat, powerful body | quiet paws move through cover and can climb.

[Chorus]
Drip, rustle, hush—A-B-C!
Layer by layer, the rainforest keeps time.

[Verse 4]
M — Macaw! Broad wings flash red, blue, or gold | one bright sweep across the upper rhythm.
N ... N ... Nectar Bat! Long tongue reaches flower nectar | quick wings carry it from bloom to bloom.
O? Orchid! A shaped flower opens from green | color holds still while wings and rain move around it.
P is Parrot. Curved beak, gripping feet | branch hold, bite, climb—small motions with clear purpose.

[Verse 5]
Q — Queen Butterfly! Orange-brown wings with dark veins and pale spots | a soft flutter crossing the stronger jungle pulse.
R ... R ... Rainforest Frog! Moist skin, strong legs | wet leaves make the close world around its hop.
S? Snake! Long scaled body bends in muscular curves | slither is movement, not a reason to move closer.
T is Toucan. Huge colorful bill above a light body | one bold shape perched inside layered green.

[Verse 6]
U — Uakari! Rainforest monkey with a short tail and bare face | branch movement adds another level to the canopy.
V ... V ... Viper! Venomous snake, patterned body | we learn the shape only from a safe distance.
W? Waterfall! Sheet and spray over rock | the longest, loudest rhythm in the rainforest scene.
At the research shelter, X is Xylophone—our X-word helper | tuned bars echo the drip pattern without pretending to be wildlife.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Anaconda! Large yellowish snake with dark blotches | a heavy curve through wet South American habitat.
Z ... Z ... Zingiber Plant! Leafy stems rise from underground rhizomes | tropical green closing the rhythm in stillness.
