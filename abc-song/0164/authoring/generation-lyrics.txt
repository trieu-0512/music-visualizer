[Intro]
Bed supports rest, cupboard stores, lamp lights, iron is an adult heat tool, vase decorates.
Home helper is a role, not permission to use everything.

[Verse 1]
A — Alarm Clock! | Timepiece with alarm function signals a set time; it does not know whether a person has slept enough.
B ... B ... Bed! | Furniture supports sleep/rest; safe setup depends on age, frame, mattress, rails, and surrounding space.
C? Cupboard! | Enclosed storage furniture with shelves/doors; contents determine whether child locks or high storage are needed.
D is Door. Movable barrier opens/closes a passage; hinges and closing edges are pinch points. |

[Verse 2]
E — End Table! | Small table beside seating/bed holds lightweight items; heavy/hot objects need stable adult placement.
F ... F ... Frame! | Border/support presents or protects a picture; glass-front frames can break.
G? Garage! | Vehicle/storage/work room; moving cars, tools, chemicals, and doors make it adult-managed space.
H is Hallway. Passage connects rooms; keeping it clear supports safe movement and emergency exit. |

[Chorus]

Name the role before touching the object.

[Verse 3]
I — Iron! | Heated appliance presses fabric; hot soleplate, steam, and cord make it adult-only.
J ... J ... Jug! | Handled container pours liquid; material and contents determine weight, breakage, or heat hazards.
K? Kettle! | Appliance/vessel heats water; boiling water, steam, and hot surfaces are adult-only.
L is Lamp. Light fixture converts electrical energy to light; bulbs/shades can heat and cords/outlets need safe placement. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mirror! | Reflective surface forms an image; securely mounted/nonbreakable designs reduce injury risk.
N ... N ... Napkin! | Cloth/paper wipes hands/mouth during meals; it is not a substitute for washing when cleaning is needed.
O? Oven! | High-heat cooking appliance; door, racks, cookware, and steam remain adult-only.
P is Pillow. Soft head/body support; sleep-safety recommendations vary with age, especially for infants. |

[Chorus]

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilt! | Layered stitched bedcover/textile; warmth and sleep suitability depend on age/context.
R ... R ... Rug! | Textile floor covering adds comfort or defines an area; non-slip backing/flat edges reduce trips.
S? Sofa! | Upholstered multi-seat furniture for sitting/resting; arms/back are not climbing platforms.
T is Table. Flat raised work/eating surface; stable use means no climbing or edge-loading. |

[Verse 6]
U — Umbrella! | Portable canopy protects from rain/sun; pointed tips and opening mechanism need space.
V ... V ... Vase! | Container displays flowers; glass/ceramic versions can break and should sit on stable surfaces.
W? Window! | Opening admits light/air/view; screens are not fall protection and children stay away from unsafe open heights.
X is X-shaped Shelf. Shelf/support arranged in an X-like form; wall anchoring/load limits still matter. |

[Chorus]

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Cushion! | Cushion with yellow cover; color changes appearance, not its support function.
Z ... Z ... Zen Garden! | Small arranged sand/stone garden used for visual/tactile contemplation; tiny stones may be choking hazards for young children.
