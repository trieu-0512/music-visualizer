[Intro]
First label: dinosaur. Next drawer: fossil bone. Ceiling mount: pterosaur.
The museum gets more interesting when 'prehistoric' is not one category.

[Verse 1]
A — Allosaurus! Theropod dinosaur | large Jurassic predator with three-fingered hands.
B ... B ... Bone! Anatomical tissue that may fossilize | specimen part, not a taxonomic animal.
C? Carnotaurus! Theropod dinosaur | brow horns and tiny forelimbs mark the genus.
D is Diplodocus. Sauropod dinosaur | long neck, long tail, peg-like front teeth.

[Verse 2]
E — Egg! Reproductive evidence | fossil eggs need nest and embryo context before assigning an exact parent.
F ... F ... Fossil! Preserved remain or trace of past life | evidence category spanning plants, animals, and activity.
G? Giganotosaurus! Carcharodontosaurid theropod dinosaur | enormous South American predator distinct from Tyrannosaurus.
H is Hadrosaur. Ornithopod dinosaur group | broad beaks and dental batteries define the duck-billed hall.

L is Lambeosaurus. Crested hadrosaurid dinosaur | hollow cranial crest is the label feature.

P is Pachycephalosaurus. Pachycephalosaurid dinosaur | thick domed skull roof, behavior still debated.

[Verse 6]
U — Unenlagia! Dromaeosaurid theropod dinosaur relative from South America | lightly built and likely feathered.
V ... V ... Velociraptor! Small feathered dromaeosaurid dinosaur | not the giant scaly movie version.
W? Wannanosaurus! Small pachycephalosaur relative | fragmentary Chinese fossils keep some details uncertain.
X is Xenoceratops. Ceratopsid dinosaur | frill ornament and horned skull place it with horned dinosaurs.
