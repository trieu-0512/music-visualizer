D for Date—A date is an oval fruit with wrinkled skin around sweet flesh and one elongated stone.

Which picture starts with O? Okra! It is a green ridged pod with rows of round seeds inside.
P ... listen ... Pumpkin! It has a firm often-ribbed rind around flesh and a hollow center filled with seeds.

S for Spinach—Spinach is leafy produce with smooth to slightly crinkled green leaves on tender stems.
Which picture starts with T? Tomato! It has smooth skin around juicy flesh divided into seed-filled chambers.

X for Xigua—Xigua is a name used for watermelon, a large fruit with a hard rind and juicy flesh.
