[Intro]
A flower can become fruit; a root can swell below ground; a grape vine can fruit again another year.
Harvest stories do not all use the same clock.

[Verse 1]
A — Apple! Perennial tree flowers, sets fruit, then rests through seasons | orchard returns year after year.
B ... B ... Beet! Root enlarges while leaves capture light | often harvested before the plant completes its full biennial cycle.
C? Cherry! Perennial tree flowers then develops stone fruits | woody branches persist after harvest.
D is Date. Date palm produces flowers and clustered fruits on a long-lived perennial plant.

[Verse 2]
E — Eggplant! Warm-season flowering crop sets berries after pollination | often grown as annual despite perennial potential in frost-free climates.
F ... F ... Fig! Woody fig tree produces syconia through a specialized internal flower system | perennial fruiting story.
G? Grapes! Woody vines flower then set berry clusters | can fruit for many years when managed.
H is Honeydew. Melon vine grows, flowers and sets fruit within a warm season | annual crop cycle.

[Verse 3]
I — Iceberg Lettuce! Leaves form a tight head before flowering | harvested in vegetative stage.
J ... J ... Jalapeno! Pepper flower becomes fruit; green fruit can ripen red | harvest may occur at different maturity stages.
K? Kale! Leaves are harvested during vegetative growth | plant can later flower if allowed to continue.
L is Leek. Leaf sheaths thicken during vegetative growth | usually harvested before flower stalk develops.

[Verse 4]
M — Mango! Long-lived tropical tree flowers and bears stone fruits seasonally.
N ... N ... Nectarine! Perennial peach-type tree flowers then forms smooth-skinned stone fruits.
O? Okra! Flowers quickly become ridged pods | pods are harvested young before seeds fully harden.
P is Pumpkin. Vine flowers then enlarges a pepo fruit on the ground | annual warm-season crop.

[Verse 5]

R ... R ... Raspberry! Perennial root/crown sends canes; cane fruiting cycle varies by cultivar | bramble persists beyond one berry.
S? Spinach! Leaves harvested before bolting/flowering for typical use | cool-season leafy crop.
T is Tomato. Flower becomes berry; fruits pass green to cultivar-specific ripe colors | annual crop in many gardens.

[Verse 6]
U — Ulluco! Andean plant forms edible underground tubers late in the growing season | harvest focuses below ground.
V ... V ... Vanilla Bean! Vanilla orchid flower must be pollinated to form the long fruit, then pods are cured after harvest.
W? Watercress! Perennial/semi-perennial aquatic herb can regrow stems and leaves | harvested as leafy shoots.
X is Xigua. Mandarin name for watermelon | same annual melon-vine lifecycle, not a new crop species.
