[Intro]
Cone marks the boundary; brick is material; crane is a lifting machine; voltage tester belongs to trained electrical work.
A building site can be learned without entering it.

[Verse 1]
A — Auger! | Rotating helical bit drills holes in soil or material; powered augers are professional tools with entanglement hazards.
B ... B ... Brick! | Masonry unit laid in courses with mortar or other systems; load-bearing use follows structural design.
C? Crane! | Lifting machine hoists suspended loads with boom/tower and cables; swing/load zones are restricted.
D is Dozer. Tracked machine pushes soil/debris with a front blade; professional work-zone equipment. |

[Verse 2]
E — Excavator! | Boom, stick, and bucket dig and move material; rotating upper body creates a large exclusion zone.
F ... F ... Forklift! | Industrial truck raises loads on forks; only trained operators use it and it never carries casual riders.
G? Gravel! | Loose stone aggregate supports drainage, base layers, concrete mixes, or landscaping depending size/use.
H is Hammer. Handled striking tool drives/removes fasteners or shapes material; real tool use requires suitable age, eye protection, and supervision. |

[Chorus]
Zone, tool, material—A-B-C!
Look from outside before naming the job.

[Verse 3]
I — Impact Driver! | Powered rotary impact tool drives screws/fasteners with high torque; adult/professional operation in this preschool set.
J ... J ... Joint! | Connection where two building parts meet; joint design controls alignment, load transfer, sealing, or movement.
K? Keyhole Saw! | Narrow pointed hand saw cuts small openings/curves; sharp teeth make it adult-only here.
L is Ladder. Portable climbing equipment; stable setup, angle, footing, and fall rules matter—children do not imitate jobsite climbing. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mallet! | Hammer-like tool with softer/large head delivers controlled blows without the same metal-face impact.
N ... N ... Nut! | Internally threaded fastener turns onto a matching bolt/stud; thread size must match.
O? Orange Cone! | High-visibility cone marks boundaries, hazards, or routes; it communicates space rather than providing protection itself.
P is Pliers. Hand tool grips, bends, or cuts depending jaw design; pinch/cut edges require supervised adult use. |

[Chorus]
Zone, tool, material—A-B-C!
Look from outside before naming the job.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quoin Brick! | Corner masonry unit or emphasized corner block; quoin describes corner position/detail rather than a unique material.
R ... R ... Rake! | Long-handled tool gathers or grades loose material; clear spacing prevents striking others.
S? Saw! | Toothed cutting tool; hand and powered saws are adult/professional tools in this activity.
T is Toolbox. Container organizes and transports tools; closed storage keeps sharp tools inaccessible. |

[Verse 6]
U — Utility Cart! | Small wheeled work cart carries materials/tools; load and route must stay controlled.
V ... V ... Voltage Tester! | Electrical test instrument indicates or measures voltage depending design; qualified adults use it before electrical work.
W? Wrench! | Tool turns nuts/bolts by gripping flats or fittings; correct size reduces slipping.
X is Xylophone. Tuned-bar musical instrument; deliberate non-construction X comparison target. |

[Chorus]

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Vest! | High-visibility garment improves wearer visibility; it does not replace hard hat, barriers, or other PPE.
Z ... Z ... Zinc Screw! | Steel screw may be zinc-coated for corrosion protection; coating does not change the need for correct size/driver.
