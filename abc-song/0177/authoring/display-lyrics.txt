[Intro]
Sharing can include a turn, kindness can include a no, bravery can include asking an adult for help.
Friendship works in both directions.

[Verse 1]
A — Angry Face! | Anger cue; one face cannot show the whole reason or intensity.
B ... B ... Brave Badge! | Courage symbol; bravery can include asking help, speaking up, or declining unsafe risk.
C? Calm Cloud! | Calm metaphor; nobody must feel calm on command.
D is Disappointed Face. Disappointment cue; a face alone cannot prove another person's feeling. |

[Verse 2]
E — Emotion Cards! | Cards help name/discuss feelings; they are vocabulary tools, not emotion tests.
F ... F ... Feelings Wheel! | Chart groups emotion words for reflection; categories are not fixed scientific boxes.
G? Gratitude Jar! | Optional gratitude notes can record appreciated moments without dismissing difficult feelings.
H is Happy Face. Happiness cue; people show happiness differently and smiles have many meanings. |

[Chorus]
Kind, ask, respect—A-B-C!
A good boundary can be a kind choice.

[Verse 3]
I — Interest Card! | Prompt represents curiosity or engagement; interest can change by activity, time, and person.
J ... J ... Joy Jar! | Joy notes recall enjoyable moments without requiring constant happiness.
K? Kindness Card! | Prompt for considerate action; kindness includes respecting another person's no.
L is Lonely Face. Loneliness cue; someone can feel lonely even around others. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mood Meter! | Self-reflection chart for feeling/energy; not a diagnosis.
N ... N ... Note of Thanks! | Written appreciation; gratitude works best when genuine, not forced.
O? Okay Sign! | Okay symbol varies by culture; verbal confirmation can be clearer.
P is Patience Timer. Timer makes waiting visible; frustration need not vanish. |

[Chorus]
Kind, ask, respect—A-B-C!
A good boundary can be a kind choice.

[Verse 5]
Q — Question Card! | Prompt to ask, clarify, or check when feelings or intentions are uncertain.
R ... R ... Respect Badge! | Respect symbol; boundaries, listening, fairness, and safety matter more than obedience.
S? Sharing Box! | Turn-taking container; sharing respects ownership, safety, and boundaries.
T is Thank-you Card. Written gratitude; appreciation works best when specific and sincere. |

[Verse 6]
U — Upset Face! | Upset cue; ask what is happening instead of guessing from a face.
V ... V ... Valued Badge! | Reminder that a person matters; worth is not earned by winning or cheerfulness.
W? Worry Box! | Optional worry-note activity; it does not erase worry or replace needed support.
X is Xylophone Calm Toy. Soft music toy may settle some people; others may prefer quiet. |

[Chorus]
Kind, ask, respect—A-B-C!
A good boundary can be a kind choice.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yoga Card! | Optional comfortable movement/breathing prompt; range stays pain-free.
Z ... Z ... Zigzag Breath Card! | Visual breathing pace; return to normal breathing if uncomfortable or dizzy.
