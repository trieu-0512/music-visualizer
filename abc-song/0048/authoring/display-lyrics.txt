[Intro]
Zip, pull, button—what's the sky doing?
A weather letter helps us choose what to wear.

[Verse 1]
A ... A ... Altocumulus! Cloudlets in rows | middle-sky patches where the wind blows
Boots — remember B. Boots cover the feet and ankles; weather boots help keep feet dry, warm, or protected.
Under the changing sky, C finds Coat. Coat wrapped round | warm outer layer when cold comes down
D — Drizzle. Drizzle is very light rain made of small drops.

[Verse 2]
Which clue starts with E? Earmuffs! Ears tucked warm | padded cups for winter storm
F ... F ... Fog! It is a cloud close to the ground that makes distant things harder to see.
Gust — remember G. Gust goes rush | quick strong wind, then back to hush
Under the changing sky, H finds Humidity Meter. It measures how much water vapor is in the air.

[Chorus]
Ready for weather—A-B-C!

[Verse 3]
I — Icicle. Drip then freeze | hanging ice grows in the breeze
Which clue starts with J? Jacket! It is an outer layer worn over other clothes for warmth or protection.
K ... K ... Kelvin Thermometer! Kelvin scale | temperature numbers tell the tale
Low Cloud — remember L. A low cloud forms close to the ground compared with higher cloud layers.

[Verse 4]
Under the changing sky, M finds Mist. Mist near ground | tiny floating droplets all around
N — Nimbus Cloud. Nimbus is a weather word associated with rain-bearing clouds.
Which clue starts with O? Overcast Sky! Clouds spread wide | one gray layer side to side
P ... P ... Puddle! It is a small shallow pool of water on the ground.

[Verse 5]
Quilt — remember Q. Stitch by stitch | warm layered fabric, soft and rich
Under the changing sky, R finds Rainbow. A rainbow appears when light is separated into colors by water droplets in the air.
S — Snowflake. Branching white | six-sided crystal, tiny and light
Which clue starts with T? Thunder! It is the sound made by air rapidly expanding around lightning.

[Chorus]

[Verse 6]
U ... U ... Umbrella! Open it wide | raindrops slide from side to side
Vane — remember V. A weather vane turns to show the direction the wind comes from.
Under the changing sky, W finds Wind. Whoosh and bend | moving air from end to end
X — X-band Radar. X-band radar uses short radio waves to detect things such as rain or moving objects.

[Verse 7]
Which clue starts with Y? Yellow Rain Boots! Boots in the rain | waterproof steps through puddles again
Z ... Z ... Zipper Jacket! It opens and closes with interlocking zipper teeth down the front.
