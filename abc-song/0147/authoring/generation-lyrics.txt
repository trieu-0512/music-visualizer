[Intro]
Level checks direction, vise holds, wrench turns, X-brace stiffens a frame.
Tool shape makes sense when we connect it to one real function.

[Verse 1]
A — Axe! | Sharp edged chopping tool for wood; professional/adult-only and never a preschool action target.
B ... B ... Bolt! | Externally threaded fastener commonly works with nut or threaded hole; diameter/pitch/grade must suit the joint.
C? Crane! | Lifting machine hoists suspended loads with boom/tower and cables; swing/load zones are restricted.
D is Dozer. Tracked machine pushes soil/debris with a front blade; professional work-zone equipment. |

[Verse 2]
E — Extension Ladder! | Ladder with sliding sections for extra reach; setup and fall protection require trained adult practice.
F ... F ... Framing Hammer! | Longer/heavier carpenter hammer designed for framing; adult/professional striking tool.
G? Goggles! | Eye protection shields against dust/chips/splashes when rated for the hazard; fit and standard matter.
H is Hammer. Handled striking tool drives/removes fasteners or shapes material; real tool use requires suitable age, eye protection, and supervision. |

[Verse 3]
I — I-beam! | Structural beam with I-shaped cross-section places material far from center to resist bending efficiently.
J ... J ... Jackhammer! | Powered percussive demolition tool breaks concrete/asphalt; noise, vibration, debris, and weight require trained workers/PPE.
K? Kneepad! | Protective pad cushions knees during floor-level work; it reduces pressure but not every injury risk.
L is Level. Tool compares a surface to horizontal/vertical using vial, laser, or electronic sensing. |

[Verse 4]
M — Measuring Tape! | Flexible graduated tape measures length; hook/zero position and straight alignment affect accuracy.
N ... N ... Nut! | Internally threaded fastener turns onto a matching bolt/stud; thread size must match.
O? Oil Can! | Container/applicator delivers lubricant to machinery; contents and maintenance belong to adults.
P is Plywood. Engineered sheet made from bonded wood veneers with alternating grain directions for dimensional strength. |

[Chorus]

[Verse 5]
Q — Quoin Brick! | Corner masonry unit or emphasized corner block; quoin describes corner position/detail rather than a unique material.
R ... R ... Rivet! | Permanent/semi-permanent fastener deforms to clamp materials together; installation uses specialized tools.
S? Saw! | Toothed cutting tool; hand and powered saws are adult/professional tools in this activity.
T is Toolbox. Container organizes and transports tools; closed storage keeps sharp tools inaccessible. |

[Verse 6]
U — Utility Knife! | Retractable/replaceable sharp blade cuts sheet materials; adult-only.
V ... V ... Vise! | Bench tool clamps work securely between jaws; fingers stay clear of closing jaws.
W? Wrench! | Tool turns nuts/bolts by gripping flats or fittings; correct size reduces slipping.
X is X-brace. Diagonal members cross in an X to resist racking/lateral movement in a frame. |

[Chorus]
Measure, hold, turn—A-B-C!

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Vest! | High-visibility garment improves wearer visibility; it does not replace hard hat, barriers, or other PPE.
Z ... Z ... Zinc Screw! | Steel screw may be zinc-coated for corrosion protection; coating does not change the need for correct size/driver.
