[Intro]
Boot has a higher upper, cap may have a brim, hoodie has a hood, zipper jacket closes with interlocking teeth.
Clothing construction gives clues about use.

[Verse 1]
A — Anorak! | Hooded outer jacket, often wind/water resistant; exact insulation and waterproofing depend on fabric/construction.
B ... B ... Boots! | Footwear extending above ankle; waterproofing, insulation, safety toe, or fashion role depends design.
C? Cap! | Close-fitting/brimmed headwear depending style; baseball-style cap shades face but not full sun exposure.
D is Dress. One-piece garment covering torso and extending below waist; length/material/style vary. |

[Verse 2]
E — Earmuffs! | Pads cover outer ears for warmth or hearing protection depending design; winter earmuffs do not replace certified hearing protectors.
F ... F ... Flip-flops! | Open sandals held by toe/foot straps; easy airflow but little toe/heel protection or traction for rough activity.
G? Gown! | Long garment; can mean formal dress, nightwear, medical gown, or robe depending context.
H is Hoodie. Sweatshirt/jacket with attached hood; drawstrings can be snag hazards in some contexts. |

[Verse 3]
I — Infinity Scarf! | Looped scarf has no loose ends but can still snag; remove around machinery/climbing hazards.
J ... J ... Jacket! | Hip/waist-length outer garment; insulation/weather resistance varies widely.
K? Knit Cap! | Soft knitted close-fitting hat retains warmth around head; fiber and thickness affect insulation.
L is Lace-up Shoes. Shoes secured with laces; tied laces prevent trips and maintain fit. |

[Verse 4]
M — Moccasins! | Soft footwear inspired by Indigenous North American moccasin traditions; modern products vary, so avoid treating one commercial style as universal.
N ... N ... Nightgown! | Loose sleepwear garment; fit/fabric should suit sleep and fire-safety guidance.
O? Overall! | One-piece or bib garment covers torso/legs for work, protection, or style; exact meaning varies.
P is Pajamas. Sleepwear designed for comfort; fabric, fit, and fire-safety standards vary by market/age. |

[Verse 5]
Q — Quilted Jacket! | Jacket stitches insulation between layers; quilting holds filling in place and creates warmth.
R ... R ... Running Shoes! | Athletic shoes designed for running with cushioning/traction/support features that vary by model and runner.
S? Socks! | Foot layers absorb moisture, reduce friction, and add warmth; clean dry fit supports comfort.
T is Trainers. UK/common term for athletic sneakers; purpose ranges casual wear to sport-specific models. |

[Verse 6]
U — Uniform! | Standardized clothing identifies school/work/team; it does not determine skill or personality.
V ... V ... Vest! | Sleeveless garment adds layer, visibility, or protection depending design; ordinary fashion vest is not PPE.
W? Wellies! | Waterproof rubber-style boots protect feet from puddles/mud; traction and fit still matter.
X is Xmas Sweater. Holiday-themed sweater label describes decoration/seasonal style, not fabric or warmth standard. |

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow Jacket! | Jacket whose color is yellow; color does not define insulation, waterproofing, or visibility certification.
Z ... Z ... Zipper Jacket! | Jacket closes with zipper teeth/slider; fingers/fabric can catch if rushed.
