[Intro]
What washed up beside the blue?
Shoreline letters—here's our clue!

[Verse 1]
Anchor — that's A. Down, down, around | anchor drops toward the ground
Spot B: Boat. A boat floats on water and carries people or things.
C brings Coral. Shore to ocean floor | branching shelter, more and more
What does D bring? Dock! A dock reaches over the water where boats can stop.

[Verse 2]
E — Estuary. River to sea | fresh and salt mix naturally
Flipper — that's F. It is a broad limb that helps a sea animal swim.
Spot G: Gull. Fly high in the sky | over shore as waves roll by
H brings Harbor. It is sheltered water where boats can rest.

[Verse 3]
What does I bring? Isopod! Small and low | many little legs in a row
J — Jellyfish. It drifts with a soft bell-shaped body and trailing tentacles.
Kelp — that's K. Sway all day | long ribbons under the bay
Spot L: Lagoon. A lagoon is shallow water separated from the open sea.

[Chorus]
Shoreline letters, one-two-three!
Name the coast from A to Z!

[Verse 4]
M brings Manatee. Slow in the sea | paddle flippers moving free
What does N bring? Narwhal! A narwhal is a whale that can have one long spiral tusk.
O — Oyster. Shell, can you tell? | rough two halves hold well
Pufferfish — that's P. It can make its body look rounder when threatened.

[Verse 5]
Spot Q: Quahog. Clam in sand | hard shell close at hand
R brings Reef. It is a rocky or coral habitat with many hiding places.
What does S bring? Seahorse! Curl and twirl | tail grips plants in a whirl
T — Turtle. It carries a hard shell and swims with steady strokes.

[Verse 6]
Urchin — that's U. Round on the ground | stiff spines point all around
Spot V: Vessel. A vessel is a boat or ship made to travel on water.
W brings Whale. Whale with a tail | rises to breathe along the trail
What does X bring? X-ray Fish! An X-ray fish has a partly see-through body that can reveal tiny bones.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellowfin Tuna. Zoom through blue | yellow fins flash into view
Zooplankton — that's Z. Tiny drifting zooplankton ride along with moving water.

[Chorus]
Shoreline letters, one-two-three!
Name the coast from A to Z!

[Call and Response 3]
Spot I ... Isopod! Walk fingertips in a tiny row.
J? ... Jellyfish! Pulse your fingers softly like a drifting bell.
Find K ... Kelp! Sway one hand like a long ribbon.
L ... Lagoon! Make a quiet bowl shape with both hands.
