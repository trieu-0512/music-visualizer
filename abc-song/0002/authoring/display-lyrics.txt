[Intro]
Low tide pulls a silver line away.
What little letter-clues did the shoreline save?

[Verse 1]
Anchor — that's A. Low tide shows the sand | heavy metal drops to hold a boat close at hand.
Spot B: Boat! A floating little carrier rocking on the blue.
C brings Coral. Branches lace | little reef rooms make a living space.
What does D bring? Dock! The shoreline reaches out | a long platform where boats come close.

[Verse 2]
E — Estuary. River meets the tide | fresh and salty waters mix side by side.
Flipper — that's F. A broad swimming limb sweeps water back to help an animal through.
Spot G: Gull! White wings lean into coastal wind and circle wide.
H brings Harbor—a calmer pocket where the boats can rest in blue.

[Verse 3]
What does I bring? Isopod! Under shoreline cover, a hard segmented body carries many little legs underneath.
J — Jellyfish. Soft bell drifting, trailing tentacles behind.
Kelp — that's K. Below the tide | long brown-green ribbons sway side to side.
Spot L: Lagoon! Shallow water set apart from open sea—a quiet place to find.

[Chorus]
Tide rolls out—look what we found!
Letter treasures all around!

[Verse 4]
M brings Manatee. Slow with ease | paddle flippers sweep warm rivers and seas.
What does N bring? Narwhal! Arctic whale; one spiral tusk can point ahead as it goes.
O — Oyster. Shell shuts well | rough two-part home where tidal waters swell.
Pufferfish — that's P. It can make its body rounder when a danger shows.

[Verse 5]
Spot Q: Quahog! Under sand | hard clam shell tucked close at hand.
R brings Reef—a rocky or coral home with cracks where little animals hide.
What does S bring? Seahorse! Hold a strand | curled tail grips the plant like a hand.
T — Turtle. Hard shell on its back; steady strokes carry it through the tide.

[Verse 6]
Urchin — that's U. Round on the ground | stiff sea spines point all around.
Spot V: Vessel! Beyond the beach line, a boat or ship travels over open water.
W brings Whale. Up, then down | giant mammal breathes at the surface, then turns around.
What does X bring? Xanthid Crab! Rounded shell and walking legs move among shore rocks.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellowfin Tuna. Fins flash into view | yellow-tinted swimmer racing through blue.
Zooplankton — that's Z. Too tiny to choose a path | moving water carries the drifters along.
