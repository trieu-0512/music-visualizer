[Intro]
Crosswalk asks us to check traffic, intersection asks us to read signals, overpass separates routes.
City movement begins with stopping where paths conflict.

[Verse 1]
A — Ambulance! | Emergency medical vehicle carries patients, clinicians, and equipment; sirens/lights do not give bystanders permission to follow closely.
B ... B ... Bakery! | Shop/workplace produces or sells breads/pastries; hot ovens and back-of-house equipment stay staff-only.
C? Crosswalk! | Marked pedestrian crossing; users still look/listen and follow signals because paint does not physically stop vehicles.
D is Department Store. Large retail store organized into multiple merchandise departments; it is a commercial place, not a public service office. |

[Verse 2]
E — Exit Sign! | Sign identifies an exit or escape direction; in emergencies follow the actual route and staff instructions.
F ... F ... Fountain! | Decorative water feature circulates/sprays water; it is not automatically safe for drinking or wading.
G? Grocery Store! | Retail store sells food and household goods; refrigerated, shelf, and checkout areas organize products/flow.
H is Hospital. Healthcare facility provides diagnosis/treatment, emergency, inpatient, or specialist services; access and roles vary by department. |

[Verse 3]
I — Intersection! | Road junction where paths cross; signals/signs/markings coordinate conflicting movements.
J ... J ... Juice Shop! | Food/drink business prepares or sells juice; ingredients/allergens and hygiene matter more than the shop name.
K? Kiosk! | Small booth or stand provides tickets, information, food, or goods depending location.
L is Laundromat. Self-service laundry facility contains washers/dryers; machines, detergents, hot surfaces, and doors require adult supervision. |

[Verse 4]
M — Mailbox! | Receptacle for outgoing/incoming mail; access and collection rules depend on postal system.
N ... N ... Neighborhood! | Local area around homes, streets, services, and shared spaces; it has no single universal boundary shape.
O? Overpass! | Bridge carries one road/path above another, separating traffic levels.
P is Park. Public green/recreation space can contain paths, play areas, fields, water, or habitat; posted rules vary. |

[Verse 5]
Q — Queue! | Ordered line of people waiting for service; it organizes turns rather than being a place/building itself.
R ... R ... Road! | Prepared route for vehicles and sometimes designated cycling/walking areas; pedestrians use sidewalks/crossings where provided.
S? School! | Education institution with classrooms, staff, shared facilities, and safety/arrival routines.
T is Taxi. Passenger-for-hire road service; legal licensing, fares, and pickup rules vary by place. |

[Verse 6]
U — Underground Train! | Rail transit operating through tunnels/underground sections; platforms and tracks remain controlled areas.
V ... V ... Van! | Enclosed road vehicle for passengers/cargo; exact use depends configuration.
W? Water Fountain! | Public drinking fountain supplies potable water when maintained for that purpose; it differs from decorative fountain.
X is X-crossing. Project X-shaped crossing marker; users must follow the actual road/rail crossing signals rather than the X shape alone. |

Z ... Z ... Zone Sign! | Sign marks a regulated or named area such as parking, speed, school, fare, or loading zone; exact rule comes from text/symbols.
