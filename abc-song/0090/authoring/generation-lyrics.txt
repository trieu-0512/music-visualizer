[Intro]
Waffle has a grid; lemon peel carries aromatic oils; ice changes state in the freezer.
Kitchen science can start with careful observation.

[Verse 4]

T is Toast. Dry heat makes bread browner, drier/crisper and more aromatic | degree varies.
