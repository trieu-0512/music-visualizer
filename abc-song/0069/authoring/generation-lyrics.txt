[Intro]
Tap-tap, twig-twig—build a little nest beat.
Bird names fly in where letters and wingbeats meet.

[Verse 1]
A is here; Albatross is why. Albatross wings spread wide | ocean winds can carry its glide
Bluebird answers B. A bluebird is a small thrush with blue plumage and a short straight bill.
around the nest-building tree, what belongs to C? Crane! Crane stands tall | long neck, long legs above them all
D—short beat—Duck. A duck has webbed feet for paddling and a broad bill suited to its feeding style.

[Verse 2]
Hear E; see Egret. Egret steps light | long legs and pointed bill in sight
F is here; Flamingo is why. It is a long-legged water bird with a bent bill adapted for filter feeding.
Goldfinch answers G. Goldfinch small and bright | cone bill, yellow-dark wings in sight
around the nest-building tree, what belongs to H? Hummingbird! It is a tiny bird that can hover by beating its wings extremely fast.

[Chorus]
Twig by twig, beat by beat!
Bird and letter finally meet!

[Verse 3]
I—short beat—Ibis. Ibis probes down | curved bill searches soft wet ground
Hear J; see Junco. It is a small ground-feeding sparrow relative with a short cone-shaped bill.
K is here; Kiwi is why. Kiwi on the ground | tiny wings, strong legs stepping round
Lark answers L. A lark is a ground bird with walking feet and, in many species, a strong aerial song.

[Verse 4]
around the nest-building tree, what belongs to M? Macaw! Macaw tail streams long | curved bill, bright colors, parrot strong
N—short beat—Nuthatch. A nuthatch is a small bird that often climbs along tree trunks and branches while searching bark for food.
Hear O; see Owl. It is a bird with forward-facing eyes and soft flight feathers; many species are active at night.
P is here; Parrot is why. It has a strong hooked bill and gripping feet with two toes forward and two backward.

[Verse 5]
Quail answers Q. Quail on the sky-line | feathered body gives a clue to find
around the nest-building tree, what belongs to R? Robin! It is a ground-hopping songbird with a slim bill used to pick small foods.
S—short beat—Swan. Swan on the lake | long neck curves, webbed feet make a wake
Hear T; see Tern. It is a slender water bird with narrow pointed wings; many species dive for fish.

[Verse 6]
U is here; Umbrellabird is why. Umbrellabird up high | umbrella crest makes the clue easy to spy
Veery answers V. A veery is a small thrush with warm brown upperparts and faint spotting on the upper chest.
around the nest-building tree, what belongs to W? Woodpecker! Woodpecker on a tree | tapping bill and gripping feet are clues to see
X—short beat—Xantus Hummingbird. Xantus Hummingbird is tiny, with a slender bill and rapid wingbeats for hovering.

[Chorus]
Twig by twig, beat by beat!
Bird and letter finally meet!

[Verse 7]
Hear Y; see Yellow Warbler. Yellow Warbler, small and bright | slender bill, yellow feathers in sight
Z is here; Zenaida Dove is why. It is a warm brownish dove with a compact head and strong pointed wings.

[Call and Response 1]
Hear A ... say Albatross! Stretch very long wings and glide slowly.
Find the picture for B ... Bluebird! Make one small perch, then flash two blue-wing shapes.
C calls ... Crane answers! Stretch a long neck; take two tall wading steps.
D ... wait ... Duck! Paddle two webbed feet with your fingers; show a broad bill.

[Call and Response 2]
Hear E ... say Egret! Take two tall wading steps; point a long straight bill.
Find the picture for F ... Flamingo! Stand one finger-leg tall; bend a filtering bill shape.
G calls ... Goldfinch answers! Pinch a seed bill; flick two bright wing patches.
H ... wait ... Hummingbird! Hover two tiny hand-wings quickly in one spot.

[Call and Response 3]

Find the picture for J ... Junco! Hop twice on the ground; pinch a small cone bill.
K calls ... Kiwi answers! Walk two sturdy ground steps; trace one long bill.
L ... wait ... Lark! Take two ground steps, then lift one small song-flight arc.

[Call and Response 4]

Find the picture for N ... Nuthatch! Walk two fingers downward along a pretend tree trunk.
O calls ... Owl answers! Make two big forward eyes, then spread silent wings once.
P ... wait ... Parrot! Hook one strong bill; grip a pretend branch with two toes.

[Call and Response 6]

X ... wait ... Xantus Hummingbird! Hover two tiny hand-wings quickly in one spot.
