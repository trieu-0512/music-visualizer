[Verse 1]
A is Angelfish, fins open wide | turning gently with the tide.
B is Buoy, bobbing on blue | anchored while boats pass through.
C is Coral, animals side by side | building reefs where fish hide.
D is Dock, by the shore | boats tie up once more.

[Verse 2]
E ... Eel! Long fish, no feet | bending through a rocky retreat.
F is Fish, fins steer | gills take oxygen from water near.
G ... Grouper! Waits by a ledge | broad mouth hiding near the edge.
H is Harbor, sheltered from spray | calmer water where boats can stay.

R is Ray, fins spread wide | gliding low where shadows slide.
S? Seahorse! Small and slow | curled tail grips seagrass in the flow.
T is Turtle, at sea flippers sweep | steady through water deep.

[Verse 6]

V is Violet Sea Snail, afloat | hangs beneath its bubble raft.
W is Whale, up for air | big breath, down from there.
X? X-ray Tetra, freshwater not sea | translucent sides, some bones we see.

[Verse 7]

Z ... Zooplankton! Ride the flow | tiny drifters where the currents go.
