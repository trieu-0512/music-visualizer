[Intro]
Plip—one silver ripple starts small.
Follow it past reef and shore; watch the blue grow wide.

[Verse 1]
A - Angelfish! Tall fins fan; flat body turns | slipping through coral branches where reef light gleams.
B ... B ... Buoy! Bob, bob, bright on blue | anchored in place, a floating marker for boats.
C is for Coral. Tiny animals, side by side | hard skeletons can build reefs where little swimmers hide.
D? Dock! Shore meets boat | boats tie up, load up, rocking with the tide.

[Verse 2]
Here comes E with Eel. Long fish, no legs | bend through a rocky gap, wave after wave.
F - Fish! Fin flick, tail swish | gills take oxygen from water as it swims.
G ... G ... Grouper! Stout in reef shade | broad mouth waiting by a ledge.
H is for Harbor. Waves grow mild behind shelter | boats find calmer water to rest in.

[Chorus]
Splash-splash! Ride the ripple with me,
Ocean letters, A to Z!

[Verse 3]
I? Island! Land in a ring of blue | one still shape with moving water all around.
J - Jetty! Built out from shore | rock, concrete, or timber reaches into the water.
Kayak - that's K. Narrow boat, double-bladed paddle | left stroke, right stroke, glide and turn.
L ... L ... Lobster! Step-step on the seafloor | long antennae feel the way ahead.

[Verse 4]
M is for Mussel. Shell shuts tight | silky threads hold fast when the tide pulls by.
N? Narwhal! Cold Arctic blue | that spiral tusk is really one very long tooth.
O - Octopus! Eight arms feel and grip | soft body squeezes through a narrow gap.
P - Pelican! Skim, dip, scoop | throat pouch catches fish, then lets the water drain.

[Chorus]
Splash-splash! Ride the ripple with me,
Ocean letters, A to Z!

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Quahog! Say KOH-hog | hard-shell clam tucked in coastal sand or mud.
R is for Ray. Wide fins ripple like wings | gliding low like a shadow over sand.
S? Seahorse! Upright, tiny fin flutter | curled tail grips seagrass when the current pulls.
T - Turtle! In the sea, broad flippers sweep | steady strokes through open water.

[Verse 6]
U - Undersea Cave! Rock opens under waves | a dark saltwater hollow below the light.
V ... V ... Violet Sea Snail! Tiny purple-blue | hanging from a bubble raft at the surface.
W is for Whale. Deep below, then up for air | one huge breath at the surface, then down again.
X? X-ray Tetra! Freshwater visitor, not sea | translucent body lets some bones partly show.

[Chorus]
Splash-splash! Ride the ripple with me,
Ocean letters, A to Z!

[Verse 7]
Y - Yellowfin Tuna! Yellow finlets flash | streamlined body slices through open blue.
Z ... Z ... Zooplankton! Tiny drifters ride the flow | current carries our last letter where the first ripple goes.

[Interlude]
The ripple turns. I call the letter; you bring the ocean word back.

[Call and Response 1]

[Call and Response 2]

F - fin flick, tail swish ... Fish! Swish one hand once.
G in reef shade ... Grouper! Make one slow glide.
H - calmer water ... Harbor! Curve both hands into a bay.

L - seafloor walker ... Lobster! Walk two fingers.

[Call and Response 4]

[Outro]
One last silver ripple touches the shore.
A to Z grows quiet; the water moves once more.
