[Intro]
Plip—one silver ripple starts small.
Reef, shore, deep blue—the alphabet widens with it.

[Verse 1]
A - Angelfish! Tall fins fan; flat body turns | slipping between coral branches as reef light flickers.
B ... B ... Buoy! Tethered, it bobs without drifting away | a floating marker may show a channel, hazard, or mooring.
C is for Coral. Tiny animals live together in colonies | their hard skeletons build reef structure layer by layer.
D? Dock! A platform by the shore | boats tie up or load beside it.

[Verse 2]
Here comes E with Eel. Long fish, no legs | the whole body waves side to side through dark reef gaps.
F - Fish! Fins steer; gills take oxygen from water | one tail beat sends the swimmer farther.
G ... G ... Grouper! Stout reef fish, broad mouth | waiting near ledges, holes, and shade.
H is for Harbor. Wind and waves ease behind shelter | boats find calmer water to anchor or dock.

[Chorus]
Splash, glide, rise—A-B-C!
Ocean Letter Splash—from shore to sea!

[Verse 3]
I? Island! Land with water all around | one still shape wrapped in moving blue.
J - Jetty! Built from shore into moving water | it can protect a coast or help guide a channel.
Kayak - that's K. Narrow boat, double-bladed paddle | left stroke, right stroke, it moves and turns.
L ... L ... Lobster! Jointed legs walk the seafloor | long antennae feel the way ahead.

[Verse 4]
M is for Mussel. Two shell halves close tight | silky threads grip rock when the tide pulls by.
N? Narwhal! Arctic whale in cold blue | its famous spiral tusk is really a long tooth.
O - Octopus! Eight arms feel and grip | a soft body can squeeze through tight openings.
Here comes P with Pelican. Wings over the surface, bill dipping low | the throat pouch catches fish and drains water.

[Chorus]
Splash, glide, rise—A-B-C!
Ocean Letter Splash—from shore to sea!

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Quahog! Say KOH-hog | a hard clam burrowed in coastal sand or mud.
R is for Ray. Wide pectoral fins ripple like wings | the body glides just above the sand.
S? Seahorse! Upright fish, tiny fin flutter | a curled tail grips seagrass when the current tugs.
T - Turtle! Shell on its back; ocean turtles use broad flippers | steady strokes carry them through open water.

[Verse 6]
U is Undersea Cave. Rock opens below the waves | a dark hollow where seawater reaches in.
V ... V ... Violet Sea Snail! Purple-blue and tiny | it hangs beneath a bubble raft at the ocean surface.
W is for Whale. A marine mammal with air in its lungs | it rises to breathe at the surface, then rolls below.
X? X-ray Tetra! Our X visitor is freshwater, not sea | translucent sides let a pale backbone show.

[Chorus]
Splash, glide, rise—A-B-C!
Ocean Letter Splash—from shore to sea!

[Verse 7]
Y - Yellowfin Tuna! Yellow fins and finlets flash | a streamlined body powers through open ocean.
Z ... Z ... Zooplankton! Many are tiny drifters carried by currents | our last letter rides the moving water that opened the song.

[Interlude]
The ripple turns. I give the letter; you bring the sea-and-shore word back.
