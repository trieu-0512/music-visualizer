Bright, polished acoustic ocean-pop for preschool, 88 BPM, rolling 4/4 with a gentle triplet undercurrent. Warm clear adult female lead, close and dry, crisp en-US consonants, stable vowels, minimal vibrato, natural sung delivery. Keep mapped target words highly intelligible and rhythmically exposed; Q, V and X stay near speech rhythm on first appearance. Use a narrow, mostly stepwise major-key melody with short memorable motifs; verses stay conversational-sung, while each chorus opens slightly higher and wider without belting. Instrumentation: light ukulele and acoustic guitar, rounded bass, soft shaker and brushed percussion, with a two-note marimba ripple as the signature motif. Intro begins sparse with the marimba ripple and voice, then the arrangement gradually widens like water moving from reef to open sea. Round 1 alternates quick rhythmic hits with longer two-phrase teaching lines; let bob-bob, fin-flick/tail-swish, step-step and skim-dip-scoop land as playful rhythmic cells, and let phrase-break rhyme breathe naturally instead of forcing equal bar lengths. Chorus is the clearest singalong peak: simple melody, strong pulse, light child-group echo only after the lead, and an easy me/Z rhyme payoff. After Round 1, strip almost to silence for the interlude. Round 2 becomes spacious stop-start call-and-response: cue first, one real beat of silence, then the target answer and a tiny action; thin the accompaniment immediately before every answer, keep the target-word onset dry and solo, then let the marimba ripple answer afterward. Maintain simple stable harmony and low arrangement density around vocabulary. Build the final chorus to the fullest but still uncluttered texture, then return to the opening two-note marimba ripple under the soft shore/more outro cadence and end cleanly with no long instrumental tail.
