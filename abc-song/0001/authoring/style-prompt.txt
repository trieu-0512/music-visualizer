Bright polished acoustic ocean-pop for preschool, 88 BPM, rolling 4/4 with gentle triplet feel. Warm clear adult female lead, close and dry, crisp en-US diction, minimal vibrato. Narrow mostly stepwise major-key melody. Keep learning lines compact, usually 6-12 words, one natural breath, no syllable rushing. Resolve rhyme inside the same line or the very next line; make the | break a tiny musical breath. Light ukulele/acoustic guitar, rounded bass, soft shaker, brushed percussion and a two-note marimba ripple. Four-line chorus is the catchy singalong peak with light child echo after lead. Round 2: cue, one beat silence, target, short action; thin accompaniment before answers and let marimba reply after. Stable harmony, low density around vocabulary. Final chorus fullest, then strip to marimba ripple and end cleanly.
