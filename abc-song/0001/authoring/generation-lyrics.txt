[Intro]
Splash-swish—what letter jumped in?
Splash the letters, A-B-C!

[Verse 1]
A — Angelfish! Tall fins open wide | gliding through reef water side to side.
B ... B ... Buoy! Bobbing bright, it marks a place where boats can ride.
C is for Coral. Reef rooms grow below | branching shelter, row by row.
D? Dock! A long arm from the shore where boats can pause beside the tide.

[Verse 2]
Here comes E with Eel. Slip through blue | winding body disappears from view.
F — Fish! Fins steer; gills breathe as water passes through.
G ... G ... Grouper! Round and low | wide mouth waits where reef shadows grow.
H is for Harbor—sheltered water quiets waves and lets the boats rest too.

[Chorus]

[Verse 3]
I? Island! Ringed by sand | water wraps around the land.
Here comes J with Jetty—a walkway starting on shore and reaching out over the water.
K — Kayak! Paddle the track | forward, turn, and paddle back.
L ... L ... Lobster! Claws out front, it walks the seafloor forward and back.

[Verse 4]
M is for Mussel. Shell shuts well | two dark halves hold fast below.
N? Narwhal! Arctic whale | one long spiral tusk may grow.
Here comes O with Octopus. Eight arms flow | soft body squeezes through a narrow place.
P — Pelican! Bill dips low | throat pouch scoops a fish with grace.

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Quahog! Hard-shell clam | tucked under coastal sand.
R is for Ray—wide fins wave | gliding low above that sand.
S? Seahorse! Upright swimmer | curled tail holds fast around a plant.
Here comes T with Turtle. Shell rides light | steady strokes beneath it all.

[Chorus]
Splash the letters, A-B-C!

[Verse 6]
U — Undersea Cave! Under the wave | a deep rocky hollow where dark water stays.
V ... V ... Violet Sea Snail! Purple-blue and tiny, it floats near the surface on the swell.
W is for Whale. Rise for a breath | giant mammal diving down to depth.
X? X-ray Tetra! Small and translucent; through its body we can partly see.

[Verse 7]
Here comes Y with Yellowfin Tuna. Yellow fins in view | fast body cutting through the blue.
Z — Zooplankton! Tiny drifters ride wherever moving water carries them too.
