[Intro]
Splash-swish—what letter jumped in?
Splash the letters, A-B-C!

[Verse 1]
A — Angelfish! Glide with the tide | tall fins open wide
B ... B ... Buoy! A buoy bobs on the water to mark a place.
C is for Coral. Shore to ocean floor | branching shelter, more and more
D? Dock! A dock reaches over the water where boats can stop.

[Verse 2]
Here comes E with Eel. Slide with the tide | long body side to side
F — Fish! It swims with fins and breathes through gills.
G ... G ... Grouper! Round all around | wide mouth near the ground
H is for Harbor. It is sheltered water where boats can rest.

[Chorus]
Splash the letters, A-B-C!
Ocean words roll back to me!

[Verse 3]
I? Island! Land ringed by sand | water all around the land
Here comes J with Jetty. It sticks out into the water from the shore.
K — Kayak! Paddle the track | forward and back
L ... L ... Lobster! A lobster walks on the seafloor with claws held out front.

[Verse 4]
M is for Mussel. Shell, can you tell? | two dark halves hold well
N? Narwhal! A narwhal is a whale that can have one long spiral tusk.
Here comes O with Octopus. Squeeze with ease | eight arms bend as they please
P — Pelican! It scoops fish with a long bill and throat pouch.

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Quahog! Clam in sand | hard shell close at hand
R is for Ray. It glides through water with wide fins that look like wings.
S? Seahorse! Curl and twirl | tail grips plants in a whirl
Here comes T with Turtle. It carries a hard shell and swims with steady strokes.

[Chorus]
Splash the letters, A-B-C!
Ocean words roll back to me!

[Verse 6]
U — Undersea Cave! Under the wave | rocky hollow, undersea cave
V ... V ... Violet Sea Snail! A violet sea snail is a small purple-blue ocean snail that can float near the surface.
W is for Whale. Whale with a tail | rises to breathe along the trail
X? X-ray Tetra! An X-ray tetra is a small translucent fish with a partly see-through body.

[Verse 7]
Here comes Y with Yellowfin Tuna. Zoom through blue | yellow fins flash into view
Z — Zooplankton! Tiny drifting zooplankton ride along with moving water.

[Interlude]
The water goes quiet—can the letters swim back?

A ... Angelfish! Glide two hands like fins.
B ... B ... Buoy! Bob one hand gently up and down.
C? ... Coral! Spread fingers like reef branches.
Find D ... Dock! Stretch one arm straight like a dock.

[Call and Response 2]
E ... Eel! Wiggle one hand in a long curve.
F ... F ... Fish! Swish one hand like a fish tail.
G? ... Grouper! Make a slow fish-swim with one hand.
Find H ... Harbor! Curve both hands into a safe little bay.

[Call and Response 3]
I ... Island! Draw a little circle in the air.
J ... J ... Jetty! Slide one hand straight out over imaginary water.
K? ... Kayak! Paddle left, paddle right.
Find L ... Lobster! Make two gentle claw shapes.

[Call and Response 4]
M ... Mussel! Close two palms like a shell.
N ... N ... Narwhal! Point one finger forward like a tusk, then glide.
O? ... Octopus! Wiggle your fingers like octopus arms.
Find P ... Pelican! Make one gentle scooping motion.

[Chorus]

S? ... Seahorse! Curl one finger like a seahorse tail.
Find T ... Turtle! Make a shell shape with both hands.

[Call and Response 6]

W? ... Whale! Glide both hands wide, then lift for a breath.
Find X ... X-ray Tetra! Make a gentle fish-swim with one hand.

[Call and Response 7]

Splash the letters, A-B-C!

[Outro]
