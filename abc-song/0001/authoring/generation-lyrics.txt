[Intro]
Plip—one silver ripple starts small.
Follow it past reef and shore; watch the blue grow wide.

[Verse 1]
A - Angelfish! Tall fins open wide | flat body turns through coral shade, gliding with the tide.
B ... B ... Buoy! Bob-bob on the blue | anchored in place while boats pass through.
C is for Coral. Tiny animals, side by side | hard skeletons build reef homes where little swimmers hide.
D? Dock! Platform at the waterside | boats tie up and load alongside.

[Verse 2]
Here comes E with Eel. Long fish, no feet | whole body bends through a rocky retreat.
F - Fish! Tail swish, fins steer | gills take oxygen from the water passing near.
G ... G ... Grouper! Stout by a ledge | broad mouth waiting at the shadowy edge.
H is for Harbor. Sheltered from spray | waves settle softer where boats can stay.

[Chorus]
Splash-splash! Ride the ripple with me,
Ocean letters, A to Z!

[Verse 3]
I? Island! Land ringed by blue | water on every side, every view.
J - Jetty! Rock, wood, or concrete from shore | built into water to guard a coast or guide boats through a channel.
Kayak - that's K. Double-bladed paddle in hand | left stroke, right stroke, turn where you planned.
L ... L ... Lobster! Step-step on the seafloor | long antennae feel what lies before.

[Verse 4]
M is for Mussel. Two shell halves lock | silky threads hold fast to wave-washed rock.
N? Narwhal! Through Arctic blue | that spiral tusk is one long tooth - it's true.
O - Octopus! Eight arms open wide | soft body squeezes through a narrow gap to slip inside.
P - Pelican! Bill dips low | pouch catches fish, then lets the water flow.

[Chorus]
Splash-splash! Ride the ripple with me,
Ocean letters, A to Z!

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Quahog! Say KOH-hog | hard-shell clam burrowed in coastal sand or mud.
R is for Ray. Wide fins span | body glides like a shadow over sand.
S? Seahorse! Upright and slow | curled tail grips seagrass in the flow.
T - Turtle! Broad flippers sweep | steady strokes through water blue and deep.

[Verse 6]
U - Undersea Cave! Rock opens below | seawater slips where the sunlight cannot go.
V ... V ... Violet Sea Snail! Purple-blue, afloat | hangs beneath a bubble raft like a tiny boat.
W is for Whale. Deep below, then up for air | one huge breath at the surface, then down from there.
X? X-ray Tetra! Freshwater, not the sea | translucent sides show some bones we can partly see.

[Chorus]
Splash-splash! Ride the ripple with me,
Ocean letters, A to Z!

[Verse 7]
Y - Yellowfin Tuna! Yellow finlets flash | streamlined body powers through an open-ocean dash.
Z ... Z ... Zooplankton! Tiny drifters ride the flow | current carries Z where the first ripple goes.

[Interlude]
The ripple turns. I call the letter; you bring the ocean word back.

[Call and Response 1]
A ... Angelfish! Tall fins—fan and glide.
B - what bobs? ... Buoy! Bob one hand up and down.
C - reef builder? ... Coral! Open fingers like branches.
D at the shore ... Dock! One flat arm makes the platform.

[Call and Response 2]
E - long and bendy ... Eel! Wave one hand side to side.
F - fin flick, tail swish ... Fish! Swish one hand once.
G in reef shade ... Grouper! Make one slow glide.
H - calmer water ... Harbor! Curve both hands into a bay.

[Chorus]
Splash-splash! Ride the ripple with me,
Ocean letters, A to Z!

[Call and Response 3]
Water all around - I? ... Island! Circle one still fist.
J reaches out ... Jetty! Slide one flat hand outward.
K - double paddle ... Kayak! Paddle left, then right.
L - seafloor walker ... Lobster! Walk two fingers.

[Call and Response 4]
M - shell shuts tight ... Mussel! Close two palms.
N - spiral tooth ... Narwhal! Trace one spiral.
Eight arms - O? ... Octopus! Wiggle both hands.
P - bill and pouch ... Pelican! Scoop, then lift.

[Chorus]
Splash-splash! Ride the ripple with me,
Ocean letters, A to Z!

[Call and Response 5]
Q ... Quahog! KOH-hog—close two palms like a clam.
R - wide fins over sand ... Ray! Glide hands wide.
S - upright, curled tail ... Seahorse! Curl one finger.
T - shell and flippers ... Turtle! Make a shell, then sweep.

[Call and Response 6]
U - dark hollow under waves ... Undersea Cave! Arch both hands.
V - bubble raft ... Violet Sea Snail! Spiral, then float.
Who rises for air? W ... Whale! Lift, breathe, glide down.
X - freshwater visitor ... X-ray Tetra! Small fish glide—freshwater is home.

[Call and Response 7]
Y - yellow finlets ... Yellowfin Tuna! Sweep fast and smooth.
Z - tiny drifters ... Zooplankton! Let fingertips ride the flow.

[Chorus]
Splash-splash! Ride the ripple with me,
Ocean letters, A to Z!

[Outro]
One last silver ripple touches the shore.
A to Z grows quiet; the water moves once more.
