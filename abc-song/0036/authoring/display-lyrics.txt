[Intro]
One track crosses snow; one wing crosses air; one flipper cuts the sea.
There is no single way to move through the polar world.

[Verse 1]
A — Auk! Compact seabird uses wings in air and underwater | one body crossing two kinds of space.
B ... B ... Bear! Broad paws carry a large furry mammal over land | movement stays grounded and powerful.
C? Caribou! Broad hooves travel snow and soft tundra | long-distance walking begins our land route.
D is Dovekie. Small black-and-white seabird dives into cold water | flight turns suddenly into swimming.

[Verse 2]
E — Eider! Sea duck paddles, dives, then lifts on strong wings | shore, water, air in one route.
F ... F ... Fur Seal! Front flippers steer a fast swimmer | thick fur belongs to a body built for cold coasts.
G? Glacier! Huge ice mass moves very slowly over land | a non-animal motion measured on a far longer clock.
H is Harp Seal. Flippers push through water, body rests on ice | movement changes at the ice edge.

[Chorus]
Step, fly, dive—A-B-C!
Land, ice, sea—each body finds a way.

[Verse 3]
I — Iceberg! Floating ice drifts with water and wind | no legs, wings, or flippers needed.
J ... J ... Jellyfish! Soft bell pulses while current carries it | a drifting kind of ocean movement.
K? Kittiwake! Long wings carry a seabird over cold water | landing places wait on shore or cliffs.
L is Lemming. Small northern rodent runs close to tundra ground | tiny steps under a huge sky.

[Verse 4]
M — Murre! Seabird flies above waves and dives below them | another air-to-water switch.
N ... N ... Northern Fulmar! Stiff wings glide low over ocean | long smooth motion using moving air.
O? Orca! Powerful tail drives a large dolphin through cold sea | the back rises to breathe at the surface.
P is Polar Bear. Broad paws cross ice and snow, and the bear can swim | one animal linking land and sea.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quartz! Hard mineral stays where rock holds it | a still marker among all these moving bodies.
R ... R ... Reindeer! Reindeer are caribou | broad hooves support travel over snow and soft ground.
S? Snowy Owl! Broad wings cross open tundra | feathered flight above low snow.
T is Tundra. Cold open land with few or no trees | the ground stage for walkers, runners, and nests.

[Chorus]
Step, fly, dive—A-B-C!
Land, ice, sea—each body finds a way.

[Verse 6]
U — Upland Goose! Strong legs walk ground and wings take over in flight | another two-mode traveler.
V ... V ... Velvet Scoter! Dark sea duck with pale wing patch | paddling, diving, and flying across coastal water.
W? Walrus! Flippers move a heavy body through sea | tusks and foreflippers help at the shore and ice edge.
At the warm shelter, X is Xylophone—our X-word helper | tuned bars mark step, glide, dive without pretending to be polar wildlife.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yellow-billed Loon! Large loon flies and dives | yellow bill points ahead over cold northern water.
Z ... Z ... Zoarcid Fish! Eel-shaped fish bends through cold water | our final route stays completely below the surface.
