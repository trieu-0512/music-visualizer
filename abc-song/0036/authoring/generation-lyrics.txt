[Intro]
Crunch-crunch—tiny tracks in snow.
The polar parade is starting to go.

[Verse 1]
A — Auk! Wing to sky | feathered bird goes gliding by
B ... B ... Bear! A bear is a large furry mammal with strong legs, broad paws, and an excellent sense of smell.
C is for Caribou. Hooves go slow | sure-foot steps along below
What does D bring? Dovekie! A dovekie is a small black-and-white seabird that dives for food in cold water.

[Verse 2]
Across the snow and ice, E meets Eider. Feather light | beak and wings come into sight
F — Fur Seal! It swims with strong flippers and has a thick coat of fur.
G ... G ... Glacier! Ice moves slow | glacier creeping down below
H is for Harp Seal. It is a northern seal whose adults have a dark harp-shaped marking on the back.

[Chorus]
Polar parade—A-B-C!
Snowy animals march with me!

[Verse 3]
What does I bring? Iceberg! Iceberg bright | most stays hidden out of sight
Across the snow and ice, J meets Jellyfish. It drifts with a soft bell-shaped body and trailing tentacles.
K — Kittiwake! Feather light | beak and wings come into sight
L ... L ... Lemming! A lemming is a small northern rodent that lives on tundra and eats plants.

[Verse 4]
M is for Murre. Wing to sky | feathered bird goes gliding by
What does N bring? Northern Fulmar! A northern fulmar is a seabird that glides on stiff wings over cold ocean water.
Across the snow and ice, O meets Orca. Blue below | ocean swimmer on the go
P — Polar Bear! It is a large Arctic bear with thick fur and broad paws.

[Verse 5]
Q ... Q ... Quartz! Quartz is a hard mineral that can sparkle with a glassy surface.
R is for Reindeer. Reindeer are caribou; their broad hooves help them travel over snow and soft ground.
What does S bring? Snowy Owl! Wing to sky | feathered bird goes gliding by
Across the snow and ice, T meets Tundra. It is cold open land where trees are scarce and low plants grow.

[Chorus]
Polar parade—A-B-C!
Snowy animals march with me!

[Verse 6]
U — Upland Goose! Perch, then fly | quick wingbeats cross the sky
V ... V ... Velvet Scoter! A velvet scoter is a dark sea duck with a pale wing patch.
W is for Walrus. Tusks in view | whiskered walrus in the blue
What does X bring? Xylophone! A xylophone is our X-word helper: a row of tuned bars makes different notes when struck.

[Verse 7]
Across the snow and ice, Y meets Yellow-billed Loon. Wing to sky | feathered bird goes gliding by
Z — Zoarcid Fish! It is an eel-shaped fish from a family often called eelpouts.
