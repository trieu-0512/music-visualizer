[Intro]
Ripple... ripple... rings on the pond.
Follow the water—our letters flow on.

[Verse 1]
A — Alder. Grow by the flow | roots by wet banks down low
B is for Boat. A boat floats on water and carries people or things.
Catfish — remember C. Whiskers swish | barbels near the mouth of the fish
What drifts in with D? Dam! A dam holds back water and changes how it flows.

[Verse 2]
By the water, E meets Eel. Slide with the tide | long body side to side
F — Frog. It hops on land and swims with strong back legs.
G is for Green Algae. Green scene | soft patches grow between
Heron — remember H. It stands very still, then steps through shallow water.

[Chorus]
Ripple, ripple—A-B-C!
River words come back to me!

[Verse 3]
What drifts in with I? Iris! Wet-ground sight | iris flower opens bright
By the water, J meets June Bug. It is a shiny beetle that can fly on warm evenings.
K — Kingfisher. Flash and splash | dive for fish, then back in a dash
L is for Lily Pad. A lily pad floats flat on the water like a green plate.

[Verse 4]
Muskrat — remember M. Body low | paddle where bank waters flow
What drifts in with N? Nest! A nest is a place birds build to hold eggs and chicks.
By the water, O meets Otter. Side to side | float, turn, dive and glide
P — Pond. It is a small body of still water where plants and animals live.

[Verse 5]
Q is for Quillwort. Quillwort low | spiky leaves in water grow
Reed — remember R. It grows tall and straight at the water's edge.
What drifts in with S? Salamander! Long and low | four legs help it go
By the water, T meets Trout. It swims in cool freshwater and flicks its tail against the current.

[Chorus]
Ripple, ripple—A-B-C!
River words come back to me!

[Verse 6]
U — Underwater Plant. Sway all day | underwater leaves bend this way
V is for Vallisneria. Vallisneria is a ribbon-leaved freshwater plant that grows underwater.
Waterfall — remember W. Water falls tall | rushing sheet by rocky wall
What drifts in with X? X-ray Tetra! An X-ray tetra is a small translucent fish with a partly see-through body.

[Verse 7]
By the water, Y meets Yellow Water Lily. Yellow in the light | broad leaves float, flower bright
Z — Zooplankton. Tiny drifting zooplankton ride along with moving water.

Ripple, ripple—A-B-C!
River words come back to me!
