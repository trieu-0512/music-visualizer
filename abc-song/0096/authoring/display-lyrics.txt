[Intro]
Before the display fills, some dough rises, some pastry bakes in layers, and finished shells must cool before filling.
A bakery morning has an order.

[Verse 1]
A — Angel Cake! Light foam-style cake structure depends strongly on whipped egg whites | delicate crumb arrives after baking/cooling.
B ... B ... Brownie! Dense chocolate bar/cake can be fudgy or cakey | structure differs from airy sponge.
C? Cupcake! Small individual cake baked in a cup/liner | frosting belongs after the cake cools.
D is Danish. Yeasted laminated pastry uses repeated dough-fat layers | baking turns those layers flaky.

[Verse 2]
E — Eclair! Choux pastry shell expands from steam, then cools before cream filling or glaze.
F ... F ... Frosting! Thick spreadable cake finish | applied after a cake has cooled enough not to melt it.
G? Glaze! Thin coating can dry glossy or translucent | finishing stage, not dough.
H is Honey Bun. Sweet yeasted rolled/coiled bun | proofing and baking precede glaze.

[Verse 3]
I — Icing! Broad sweet finishing mixture | consistency can be thin, thick, poured, piped, or spread depending recipe.
J ... J ... Jelly Donut! Fried/baked donut filled with jelly or jam | filling stage follows cooked dough.
K? Kolache! Czech-origin yeasted pastry traditionally holds a sweet filling in soft dough | yeast stage comes before bake.
L is Loaf. One larger bread shape with crust and crumb | cooling helps crumb set before slicing.

[Chorus]
Rise, bake, cool—A-B-C!
Finish comes later when the structure is ready.

[Verse 4]
M — Macaron! Almond-meringue sandwich cookie: two smooth shells with filling between | distinct from coconut macaroon.
N ... N ... Napoleon Pastry! Napoleon/mille-feuille uses many thin puff-pastry layers with filling between | crisp layers need careful assembly.
O? Oat Cookie! Cookie with oats contributing visible flakes and chewy/crisp texture | portioned before baking.
P is Pretzel. Yeasted dough formed into a knot/loop and traditionally treated before baking for its characteristic crust.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quiche! Savory egg-custard tart in pastry crust | bakery item can be savory, not every case item is dessert.
R ... R ... Roll! Small individual bread form | proof, bake, cool.
S? Scone! Quick bread leavened without yeast fermentation in typical recipes | tender crumb differs from bread roll.
T is Tart. Shallow pastry shell carries open filling | shell/filling order depends recipe.

[Verse 6]
U — Ube Roll! Rolled cake flavored/colored with ube, the purple yam Dioscorea alata or ube preparations | ube is not taro.
V ... V ... Victoria Sponge! Two sponge layers traditionally sandwich jam and often cream/buttercream | assembly after cooling.
W? Waffle! Batter cooks between patterned hot plates | grid is formed by the iron, not cut afterward.
X is Xmas Cookie. Project label for a holiday-shaped/decorated cookie | design category, not one universal recipe.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yule Log! Rolled cake decorated to resemble a log | rolling/filling/decorating create the final form.
Z ... Z ... Zucchini Bread! Quick-bread loaf with grated zucchini contributing moisture | vegetable ingredient inside a baked loaf.

[Chorus]
Rise, bake, cool—A-B-C!
Finish comes later when the structure is ready.
