[Intro]
Earth fills the window; the first burn ends; now the spacecraft keeps falling around the planet.
Our letter quest begins by learning where each thing really belongs.

[Verse 1]
A — Asteroid! Rocky or metallic small body orbiting the Sun | many lie in the main belt.
B ... B ... Black Hole! Inside an event horizon, even light cannot escape outward | distant target, never a place we fly toward.
C? Comet! Ice and dust can form a coma and tails near the Sun | tails point generally away from the Sun.
D is Dwarf Planet. It orbits the Sun, is rounded by gravity, and has not cleared its orbital neighborhood | a distinct Solar System class.

[Verse 2]
E — Earth! Third planet from the Sun | oceans, land, atmosphere, and our launch site begin here.
F ... F ... Flag! Mission marker | in vacuum it cannot wave from air because there is no air.
G? Gemini Capsule! NASA Gemini carried two astronauts in the 1960s | a historical crew capsule.
H is Helmet. Spacesuit helmet protects the head and suit environment | crew equipment, not propulsion.

[Chorus]
Launch, orbit, observe—A-B-C!
Space is a map of objects, paths, and tools—not one floating pile.

[Verse 3]
I — International Space Station! The ISS orbits Earth | crew and station share continuous free fall, producing microgravity.
J ... J ... Jet Pack! Some astronaut maneuvering units use small thrusters | the Jet Pack label is not standard gear for every spacewalk.
K? Krypton Tank! Some electric-propulsion spacecraft store krypton propellant | the tank feeds a thruster system.
L is Lander. Spacecraft designed to descend to a surface | landing hardware, not open-orbit hardware.

[Verse 4]
M — Meteor! A meteor is light from a meteoroid entering an atmosphere | the solid object in space is a meteoroid.
N ... N ... Neptune! Eighth major planet from the Sun | distant blue world.
O? Orbit! A curved path shaped by motion and gravity | spacecraft keep falling around a body.
P is Planet. Rounded world orbiting a star; Solar System planets have cleared their orbital neighborhoods.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quarter Moon! Half the visible Moon is illuminated at first or last quarter | the word quarter names the cycle position, not a quarter-lit disk.
R ... R ... Rocket! Expelled propellant creates thrust | rocket carries its own reaction mass and works in vacuum.
S? Satellite! Natural or artificial object in orbit | the Moon and spacecraft can both be satellites.
T is Telescope. Instrument collects electromagnetic radiation | designs differ by wavelength.

[Chorus]
Launch, orbit, observe—A-B-C!
Space is a map of objects, paths, and tools—not one floating pile.

[Verse 6]
U — Uranus! Seventh planet from the Sun | its rotation axis is tilted about 98 degrees.
V ... V ... Visor! Helmet shield protects the face and manages light | spacesuit part, not spacecraft window.
W? World! Broad word for a planet-like place | not a precise astronomical class.
X is X-ray Telescope. Cosmic X-rays are mostly absorbed by Earth's atmosphere | X-ray telescopes usually observe from space.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yoke! A yoke is a vehicle control shape | our mapping helper is not universal spacecraft hardware.
Z ... Z ... Zero-gravity Chair! A reclining chair may use this marketing name | it changes posture, not gravity.

[Interlude]
Engines hush; one star keeps blinking. Now the answers come back from memory.
