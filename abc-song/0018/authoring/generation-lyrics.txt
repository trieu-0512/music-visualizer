[Intro]
Pat the soil flat; stop and listen to the little garden sounds.
Pat, pour, wait—the garden asks for little things, one at a time.

[Verse 1]
A — Apple Tree! Trunk steady, branches spread | a living garden anchor that needs soil, water, and time.
B ... B ... Bee! Small visitor at the flowers | wingbeats pass while garden work stays gentle.
C? Compost! Crumble and mix | broken-down plant and food material can enrich garden soil.
D is Daisy. Round center, petal ring | one simple bloom showing what a cared-for bed can hold.

[Verse 2]
E — Eggplant! Broad leaves shade glossy fruit | a living crop beside the tools and paths.
F ... F ... Flower! Bud opens into bloom | color arriving after soil, water, and time.
G? Garden Gate! Swing, stop | a simple entrance that keeps the bed boundary clear.
H is Herb. Pinch-shaped leaves, strong scent | a useful plant growing close to the kitchen path.

[Chorus]
Pat, pour, pause—A-B-C!
Care for the bed, then let the garden be.

[Verse 3]
I — Iris! Long blade-like leaves | showy petals rise from the cared-for soil.
J ... J ... Jasmine! Small fragrant flowers | scent drifts while hands move to the next job.
K? Kneeling Pad! Soft square under knees | cushioning low work near the bed.
L is Lavender. Narrow leaves, purple spikes | a scented plant we leave rooted while we care around it.

[Verse 4]
M — Mulch! Spread, rest | a cover over soil that helps hold moisture and reduce weeds.
N ... N ... Nest! Twigs cup eggs or chicks | garden care also means leaving wildlife homes undisturbed.
O? Orchid! Distinct flower shape | a delicate living thing to notice rather than handle roughly.
P is Pea. Tendrils climb, pods fill | care gives the vine support, water, and time.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quince! Firm yellow fruit | a shrub or small tree can carry it after a season of growth.
R ... R ... Radish! Leaves up, root down | loosened soil leaves room for the crisp root below.
S? Sunflower! Tall stem holds a broad head | care below supports height above.
T is Trowel. Small hand tool for moving soil | use it only with appropriate adult guidance.

[Chorus]
Pat, pour, pause—A-B-C!
Care for the bed, then let the garden be.

[Verse 6]
Rain check, U — Umbrella! Wide canopy keeps drops off a person | waiting beside the bed when showers come.
V ... V ... Violet! Low bloom, soft color | a small plant tucked close to the soil.
W? Worm! Wiggle, tunnel | the long soft body moves through moist soil under our work.
X is Xeranthemum. Dry, papery-looking flower heads | a texture clue among softer petals.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yam! Thick tuber stores food underground | careful soil work happens above what we cannot see.
Z ... Z ... Zucchini! Broad leaves, yellow blossoms, long green squash | the bed answers care with a crop.
