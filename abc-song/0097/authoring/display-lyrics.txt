[Intro]
Bread can rise with yeast; Danish gets lift from yeast and layers; scone is a quick bread; ice cream is not dough at all.
Bakery names belong to different structure families.

[Verse 1]
A — Apple Pie! Fruit filling enclosed partly or fully by pastry crust | crust family, not yeast bread.
B ... B ... Bread! Flour-water dough develops structure and often rises through yeast fermentation | broad family with many formulas.
C? Cupcake! Small cake made from batter | chemical leavening and/or whipped-air structure depends recipe.
D is Danish. Laminated yeasted dough creates flaky layers | pastry family.

[Verse 2]
E — Egg Tart! Pastry shell holds baked egg custard | crust plus custard, not bread crumb.
F ... F ... Fruitcake! Dense cake batter carries dried/candied fruit | cake family.
G? Gingerbread! Can be cookie, cake, or loaf depending formula | one name spans different structures.
H is Honey Bun. Soft sweet yeast dough rolled/coiled before baking | yeast family.

[Chorus]
Yeast, layer, batter—A-B-C!
Sort the structure before you bounce the rhyme.

[Verse 3]
I — Ice Cream! Frozen aerated dessert/emulsion | deliberate non-dough exception.
J ... J ... Jam Tart! Shallow pastry crust with jam filling | tart-crust family.
K? Kaiser Roll! Individual yeast bread roll with segmented top pattern | bread family.
L is Lemon Tart. Pastry shell plus lemon filling | tart family.

[Verse 4]
M — Muffin! Quick bread/cake-like batter typically leavened chemically | no long yeast proof required.
N ... N ... Napoleon Pastry! Crisp puff-pastry layers plus filling | lamination/steam creates many sheets.
O? Orange Cake! Cake batter flavored with orange | cake family.
P is Pie. Pastry crust holds a filling | broad crust family.

[Verse 5]
Q — Quiche! Savory egg custard baked in pastry crust | crust family with savory filling.
R ... R ... Raisin Bread! Yeast bread with raisins through crumb | bread family.
S? Scone! Quick bread using chemical leavening in typical recipes | different from yeast roll.
T is Tart. Shallow pastry shell with open filling | crust family.

[Verse 6]
U — Upside-down Cake! Cake batter bakes over fruit/topping and is inverted for serving | cake family.
V ... V ... Vanilla Cake! Vanilla-flavored cake batter/foam structure | cake family.
W? Waffle! Pourable batter sets between hot patterned plates | batter family, not oven loaf.
X is X-shaped Pretzel. Pretzel yeast dough shaped into X | same dough family, different geometry.

[Chorus]
Yeast, layer, batter—A-B-C!
Sort the structure before you bounce the rhyme.

[Verse 7]
Y — Yule Log! Rolled sponge cake with filling | cake/foam family, not actual bread log.
Z ... Z ... Zucchini Bread! Sweet quick-bread loaf commonly chemically leavened | loaf shape does not make it yeast bread.
